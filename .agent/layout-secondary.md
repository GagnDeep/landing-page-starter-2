# Layout Documentation: Secondary Pages

This document outlines the layout intent and visual structure for the 11 required secondary pages. All pages utilize the Global Header and Global Footer defined in the Home Page layout.

---

## 1. About Us (`/about`)
**Intent**: Tell the story of heritage, culinary passion, and growth.
- **Hero**: Split layout. Left text ("Our Story"), right image of the original kitchen or founder.
- **Timeline**: A vertical line connecting major milestones (establishment, expansion to international events, etc.).
- **Values Grid**: 2x2 grid of core principles (Quality, Authenticity, Elegance, Service).
- **Team**: Grid of key chefs and event managers, focusing on professional, editorial-style portraits.

## 2. Features / Services (`/features`)
**Intent**: Detail the specific types of events and catering styles offered.
- **Hero**: Centered text, large typography. "Catering Designed for the Extraordinary."
- **Service Blocks**: Alternating full-width rows (Image Left/Text Right, then Text Left/Image Right) detailing: Buffet, Plated Dinners, Live Stations, Bar Services.
- **Inclusions**: A clean, checked list layout of what is included (staffing, cutlery, setup).

## 3. The Menu (`/menu`)
**Intent**: A highly appetizing, categorized list of offerings.
- **Hero**: Atmospheric image of a grand feast.
- **Category Navigation**: Sticky sub-nav (Punjabi, International, Desserts, Live Counters).
- **Menu Items**: Elegant list view. Dish name in serif, description in sans-serif, dietary icons (V, GF) right-aligned.
- **Highlight Cards**: Interspersed large cards featuring a hero image of a specific signature dish.

## 4. Gallery (`/gallery`)
**Intent**: Pure visual inspiration.
- **Header**: Minimalist title.
- **Filter Bar**: Subtle text buttons (All, Weddings, Corporate, Details, Venues).
- **Masonry Grid**: Fluid, edge-to-edge masonry layout. Hovering over an image reveals the event name and location in a dark, semi-transparent overlay.

## 5. Pricing / Packages (`/pricing`)
**Intent**: Transparent, premium presentation of investment.
- **Hero**: Clean, minimal. "Invest in Unforgettable Taste."
- **Pricing Cards**: 3-column layout. Highlighting a "Signature" package in the center with a slight elevation and brand-color border.
- **Add-ons**: A 2-column list of extras (e.g., Mixologist, Ice Sculptures).
- **FAQ Teaser**: Link to the full FAQ.

## 6. Booking / Contact (`/contact`)
**Intent**: Frictionless, elegant inquiry process.
- **Layout**: 50/50 split viewport (Desktop).
- **Left Column**: Contact details, phone numbers, office locations (Delhi, London, Dubai), and a warm greeting.
- **Right Column**: A clean, multi-step form (Name, Event Date, Guest Count, Location, Message). Large input fields, minimal borders.

## 7. Blog / Journal (`/blog`)
**Intent**: SEO content and brand authority.
- **Hero**: Featured article taking up the top half of the page.
- **Grid**: 3-column grid of recent articles.
- **Sidebar (Optional)**: Categories, Popular Tags, Newsletter signup.

## 8. FAQ (`/faq`)
**Intent**: Address common concerns for large-scale events.
- **Header**: Centered title and short paragraph.
- **Categories**: Jump links to sections (Booking, Food, Logistics).
- **Accordion**: Beautifully styled expanding rows. Only one row open at a time to keep the layout clean.

## 9. Careers (`/careers`)
**Intent**: Attract top-tier culinary and hospitality talent.
- **Hero**: Image of the kitchen team in action.
- **Culture**: 3-column text blurbs on why work with Sangam.
- **Open Roles**: A simple table or list of accordion items (Role Title, Location, "Apply" button).

## 10. Terms of Service (`/terms`)
**Intent**: Legal compliance.
- **Layout**: Narrow, single-column text block (`max-w-3xl`) centered on the page for optimal readability.
- **Typography**: Heavy reliance on clear headings (H2, H3) and legible body copy.

## 11. Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)
**Intent**: Legal compliance.
- **Layout**: Identical to Terms of Service. Narrow reading column, clean typography, distinct section breaks.
