# Layout Intent: Pricing Page

## Core Intent
Provide clear, transparent, and premium pricing. Avoid looking cheap; emphasize value, quality, and the bespoke nature of the services.

## Key Sections
1. **Hero Header:** "Investment in Your Personal Style."
2. **Pricing Philosophy:** A brief explanation of how pricing is determined (complexity, fabric, time) and the commitment to no hidden fees.
3. **Core Service Tiers/Categories:** Elegant tables or cards breaking down starting prices for different categories (e.g., Simple Blouse, Elaborate Gown, Bridal Suite).
4. **Bespoke/Custom Design Add-ons:** Pricing guidelines for hand-embroidery, specialized sourcing, or rush deliveries.
5. **The Value Proposition:** A reminder of the alteration-free guarantee, making the initial investment worthwhile.
6. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):** Answers regarding deposits, payment methods, and refund policies.
7. **Call to Action:** "Request a Custom Quote" or "Book a Consultation."
