# Pricing Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Present pricing for bespoke services not as tiers, but as starting points for luxurious investments. Avoid SaaS-style "pricing cards."

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (Investment in Elegance):** Minimal text, soft background, focusing on the value of craft.
3. **The Pricing Philosophy:** A brief text section explaining that true bespoke work depends on fabric and embellishment, providing transparency.
4. **Category 1 (Everyday Elegance):** An elegant, list-based presentation of starting prices for simpler suits/kurtas. Includes a representative image.
5. **Category 2 (Festive & Occasion Wear):** Similar layout, highlighting more intricate work.
6. **Category 3 (Bridal & Couture):** A dedicated, more expansive section emphasizing custom consultations for these high-end pieces.
7. **What's Included:** A delicate checklist (using custom icons, not standard checkmarks) detailing consultations, fittings, and shipping.
8. **FAQ Teaser:** A few common questions regarding payment terms and timelines.
9. **Call to Action:** "Schedule a Consultation for a Custom Quote."
10. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
