# Layout Blueprint: Pricing & Catalog

**Purpose**: Transparent pricing for core items and custom order baseline costs.
**Vibe**: Clear, elegant, no hidden surprises.

## Sections
1. **Global Header**
2. **Hero: Transparent Pricing**
   - "Premium quality, clear prices."
3. **Pricing Tiers for Custom Cakes**
   - 3 columns: Classic, Signature, Premium Custom (highlighting the 6-pound base).
4. **Standard Cakes Menu**
   - Grid layout with prices (Black Forest, Pineapple, Truffle, etc.).
5. **Gift Hampers Pricing**
   - Small, Medium, Large/Luxury tiers.
6. **Individual Items (Flowers, Chocolates)**
   - List or small cards.
7. **Midnight Delivery Surcharges**
   - Clear breakdown of 24/7 logistics costs.
8. **Bulk/Corporate Discounts**
   - Contact form teaser for large orders.
9. **Customization Add-ons**
   - Fondant work, edible prints, special toppers (price ranges).
10. **Payment Methods Accepted**
    - Icons for UPI, Cards, Cash.
11. **Cancellation & Refund Policy Summary**
12. **FAQ on Pricing**
13. **"Not sure? Get a Quote" Form**
14. **Customer Review on Value**
15. **CTA: Order Now**
16. **Global Footer**
