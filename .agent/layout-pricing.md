# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page (`/pricing`)

## Goal
To present clear, transparent pricing tiers for different learner needs, reducing friction in the booking process.

## Section Architecture (15-20 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Hero Section:** "Transparent Pricing for Your Journey to the Road."
3.  **Pricing Philosophy:** Short text on why investing in quality instruction saves money (and lives) in the long run.
4.  **Pricing Tier 1 (The Beginner Package):** Card detailing the full 15/20-day course. Price, hours, inclusions (theory + practical).
5.  **Pricing Tier 2 (The Refresher Package):** Card for licensed drivers needing a confidence boost. Price, hours, focus areas.
6.  **Pricing Tier 3 (The Test Prep Package):** Card focused solely on passing the RTO test. Mock tests included.
7.  **Custom Packages:** A block mentioning hourly rates or custom plans for specific needs.
8.  **What's Included in Every Package:** Checklist (e.g., dual-control car, door-to-door pickup in Sector 47, progress reports).
9.  **Add-ons / Extras:** Extra mock tests, highway-specific training hours, etc.
10. **Payment Methods Accepted:** Icons/text indicating how students can pay (UPI, Cards, Cash).
11. **Cancellation & Refund Policy:** Clear, concise summary of the policy to build trust.
12. **Corporate/Group Training (Optional):** Teaser for corporate road safety workshops.
13. **Testimonial (Value Focus):** A quote emphasizing that the school was "worth every penny."
14. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):** "Are there hidden fees?", "Can I pay in installments?", etc.
15. **Booking Process Outline:** 3 simple steps (Choose plan -> Contact us -> Start driving).
16. **Final CTA:** Prominent contact/booking form or button.
17. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
