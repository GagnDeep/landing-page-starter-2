# Layout Intent: Pricing Page (`/pricing`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Title: "Invest in Peace of Mind". Subtext: Transparent, tailored curation.
3. **Pricing Philosophy:** A brief text block explaining that luxury is custom, hence there are no rigid "packages", but rather tiers of service engagement.
4. **Tier 1: Full Orchestration:** Detailed card highlighting complete end-to-end planning.
5. **Tier 2: Partial Planning / Design Only:** Card for clients who have venues but need aesthetic and logistical support.
6. **Tier 3: The Destination Experience:** Specialized tier for multi-day, multi-city logistics.
7. **What's Included (Table):** A highly styled, non-cluttered comparison table for the tiers.
8. **The Consultation Process:** A 3-step visual guide (Discovery Call -> Proposal -> Retainer).
9. **Custom Quotes for NRI Clients:** A specific note on handling international payments and specialized logistics.
10. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):** Accordion addressing payment terms, minimum budgets, and vendor direct payments.
11. **Value Proposition:** A visual break showing the ROI of a planner (saving time, preventing costly mistakes).
12. **Testimonial on Value:** A quote specifically highlighting that the service was "worth every penny".
13. **Add-on Services:** A subtle list of extras (e.g., dedicated RSVP management team).
14. **Transparency Promise:** A statement guaranteeing no hidden vendor markups (if applicable to business model).
15. **CTA:** "Request a Custom Proposal".
