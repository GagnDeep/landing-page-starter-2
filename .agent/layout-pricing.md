# Pricing Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **Pricing Hero**: Clean, transparent hero setting expectations about bespoke pricing.
3. **Pricing Philosophy**: A short paragraph explaining why custom decor varies in price.
4. **Tier 1: Essential Elegance**: A pricing card focusing on intimate events.
5. **Tier 1 Features**: List of inclusions.
6. **Tier 2: The Signature Anand Karaj**: The highlighted, most popular pricing card.
7. **Tier 2 Features**: Comprehensive list of inclusions.
8. **Tier 3: The Royal Patiala Gala**: The premium, all-inclusive pricing card.
9. **Tier 3 Features**: Everything included, plus bespoke imports.
10. **A La Carte Options Title**: Section introducing add-ons.
11. **Add-on: Premium Floral Upgrades**: Details and starting price.
12. **Add-on: Custom Installations**: Details and starting price.
13. **Add-on: Extended Venue Lighting**: Details and starting price.
14. **What's Not Included**: Clear communication on external costs (e.g., venue fees).
15. **Payment Terms & Schedule**: A simple timeline showing deposit and final payment dates.
16. **Value Proposition**: Why the investment is worth it (quality, peace of mind).
17. **Client Testimonial**: A strong quote specifically praising the value for money.
18. **Custom Quote Request**: A form specifically tailored to getting a custom estimate.
19. **FAQ Teaser**: Link to the FAQ.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
