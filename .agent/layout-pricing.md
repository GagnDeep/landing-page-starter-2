# Pricing Page Layout Map

**Goal:** Present the pricing structure clearly and elegantly, reflecting the premium nature of the services without being intimidating.

## Section 1: Hero Header
- **Visuals:** Minimalist.
- **Structure:** "Investment in Yourself" or "Service Menu".

## Section 2: Pricing Note / Disclaimer
- **Visuals:** Small, muted text block.
- **Structure:** Note that prices are starting points and may vary based on hair length, nail complexity, or stylist tier.

## Section 3: Nail Services Pricing
- **Visuals:** Clean list or table format.
- **Structure:** Basic Gel, Extensions, Chrome Add-on, Intricate Art pricing.

## Section 4: Hair Services Pricing
- **Visuals:** Clean list or table format.
- **Structure:** Cuts, Blowouts, Color, Specialized Curly Cut pricing.

## Section 5: Bridal Packages
- **Visuals:** Highlighted cards.
- **Structure:** Tiered packages (e.g., The Minimalist Bride, The Grand NRI Experience).

## Section 6: Consultation / Contact CTA
- **Visuals:** Banner.
- **Structure:** "Unsure what you need? Book a free consultation."
