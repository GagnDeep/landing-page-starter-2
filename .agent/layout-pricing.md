# Layout Documentation: Pricing / Menu Page (`/pricing`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
The Pricing page acts as a high-end menu. It should be highly legible, structured, and mouth-watering. It avoids cheap "pricing tables" in favor of an elegant, restaurant-style menu layout, occasionally interspersed with rich imagery.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Pricing-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`Pricing-02-MenuHero`**: Simple, elegant title ("Our Menu & Offerings") with a subtle background texture.
3.  **`Pricing-03-CategoryFilter`**: Horizontal list of buttons (Breads, Pastries, Custom Cakes, Savories) to filter the menu.
4.  **`Pricing-04-MenuSectionHeader`**: Elegant serif category title with a decorative separator line.
5.  **`Pricing-05-MenuItemList`**: A clean, vertical list layout for items.
6.  **`Pricing-06-MenuItemRow`**: Flex layout. Item name and dotted leader line to the price. Description below in muted text.
7.  **`Pricing-07-FeaturedMenuItem`**: A highlighted item within the list, perhaps with a small image and a "Chef's Choice" badge.
8.  **`Pricing-08-MenuImageBreak`**: A full-width or large inset image breaking up the text-heavy menu sections.
9.  **`Pricing-09-CustomCakePricing`**: Distinct section for bespoke cakes, explaining base prices and factors that affect the final cost.
10. **`Pricing-10-TieredCakeGraphic`**: Visual representation of cake sizes/tiers and their corresponding serving sizes.
11. **`Pricing-11-FlavorOptionsList`**: Two or three-column text list of available sponge and frosting flavors.
12. **`Pricing-12-AddOnsSection`**: Boxed layout detailing extras like custom toppers, floral arrangements, or premium fillings.
13. **`Pricing-13-BulkOrderDiscount`**: A visually distinct callout box explaining pricing for large quantities.
14. **`Pricing-14-DietarySurchargeInfo`**: Subtle text block detailing any extra costs for gluten-free or vegan modifications.
15. **`Pricing-15-OrderingProcess`**: 3-step horizontal layout (Choose, Consult, Enjoy) explaining how to place an order.
16. **`Pricing-16-PaymentMethods`**: Small, elegant icons showing accepted payment types.
17. **`Pricing-17-TermsOfOrder`**: Fine print section (cancellations, deposits) styled cleanly at the bottom of the menu.
18. **`Pricing-18-StartOrderCTA`**: Prominent button leading to the contact or order form.
19. **`Pricing-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`Pricing-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
