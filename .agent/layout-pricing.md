# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page
**Intent:** Provide transparent, structured pricing tiers while maintaining a premium feel. Avoid looking "cheap".

## Section Inventory
1. **Hero:** "Invest in Unforgettable Flavors".
2. **Pricing Philosophy:** Explaining value (ingredients, service ratio).
3. **Tier 1: The Silver Feast:** Basics for smaller or budget-conscious events.
4. **Tier 2: The Gold Standard:** Most popular, extensive menu options.
5. **Tier 3: The Royal Platinum:** Unlimited live stalls, imported ingredients.
6. **Add-ons & Enhancements:** Mocktail bars, specialized dessert stations.
7. **What's Included:** Clarifying that service staff, crockery, etc., are included.
8. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):** Minimum guest counts, payment terms.
9. **CTA:** "Get a precise quote."
