# Pricing & Packages Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Provide clarity on the investment required for Oasis Caterers' services while maintaining the luxury aesthetic. Avoid "cheap" pricing tables; focus on "Investments" and "Tiers."

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation.
2. **Hero Section (The Investment)**: "Curated experiences tailored to your scale."
3. **Pricing Philosophy**: Text block explaining that every event is bespoke, but providing baselines.
4. **Tier 1: The Classic Affair**: Pricing card (left-aligned) with detailed inclusions.
5. **Tier 2: The Signature Gala**: Highlighted pricing card (center or expanded) with premium inclusions.
6. **Tier 3: The Bespoke Journey**: "Price upon request" tier for ultimate customization.
7. **What's Always Included**: A grid of standard luxury provisions (e.g., dedicated manager, premium flatware).
8. **Add-On Services**: Accordion list of optional enhancements (e.g., late-night stations, specialty bars).
9. **Guest Count Scaling**: A visual guide or slider showing how per-head costs adjust with scale.
10. **The Consultation Fee**: Details on tasting and consultation deposits.
11. **Payment Milestones**: A simple timeline of when deposits and final payments are due.
12. **Cancellation & Adjustment Policy**: Clear, professional text block.
13. **Cost vs. Value**: Editorial section comparing the Oasis standard to typical catering.
14. **Testimonial on Value**: A quote highlighting that the service was "worth every penny."
15. **Venue Fees & Taxes**: Clarification on external costs.
16. **Travel & Logistics Surcharges**: Information for out-of-town events.
17. **FAQ Snippet**: Pricing-specific questions.
18. **Custom Proposal Generator Teaser**: "Let's build your estimate."
19. **Primary Call to Action**: "Schedule a Consultation."
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
