# Layout Documentation: Pricing Page

## Intent
Tailoring pricing is often complex (based on fabric, style, etc.). This page must handle that complexity elegantly, setting expectations without looking like a SaaS pricing grid. It should feel like a menu at a high-end restaurant.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Minimalist. Title: "Investment in Elegance." Subtitle explaining that final prices depend on fabric selection.
2. **Starting Prices List:** A clean, typographic list (e.g., "Two-Piece Suits ... starting at $X"). No boxes or heavy borders. Just text and delicate leader lines (dots).
3. **The Fabric Tiers:** Explaining how fabric choice affects price (House fabrics, Premium Italian, Luxury Vicuna, etc.).
4. **Alterations Menu:** A categorized list of common alterations and base prices.
5. **FAQ:** Accordion style for questions like "Do I pay upfront?" or "What if my measurements change?"
6. **CTA:** "Schedule a Consultation for a Detailed Quote."
