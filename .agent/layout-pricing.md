# Layout: Pricing Page

The Pricing page must handle costs delicately, positioning the studio as a premium investment while remaining clear and approachable.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Clean, minimal hero. Title: "Investment". Subtitle: "Bespoke collections for your legacy."
2.  **Pricing Philosophy:** A brief text section explaining the value of investing in professional memories.
3.  **Base Collections (Grid):** A 3-column layout detailing the starting points for different services (e.g., Photography Only, Photo + Video, Destination). Clear "Starting at" prices.
4.  **What's Always Included:** A list of standard inclusions across all packages (e.g., high-res images, pre-wedding consultation).
5.  **A La Carte / Add-Ons:** A clean list of optional extras (e.g., extra hours, rush delivery, parent albums).
6.  **The Heirloom Albums:** A dedicated section showcasing the physical albums. Emphasize craftsmanship and materials.
7.  **The Booking Process:** A simple 1-2-3 step guide on how to secure a date.
8.  **Call to Action:** "Request a Custom Quote" leading to a detailed inquiry form.
