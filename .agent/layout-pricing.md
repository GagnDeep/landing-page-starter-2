# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page

## Structural Rhythm
Clear, transparent, yet framed as an investment in quality rather than a simple transaction.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:** "Investment in Craftsmanship."
2. **Pricing Philosophy:** A brief text section explaining the value of bespoke.
3. **Starting Prices Grid:** Clean, elegant cards showing starting prices for Shirts, Suits, Trousers, and Traditional Wear.
4. **What's Included:** A subtle checklist of the bespoke experience (fittings, consultations).
5. **Fabric Tiers:** Explanation of how fabric choice affects final pricing.
6. **Embroidery Add-ons:** Pricing structure for custom hand-work.
7. **FAQ:** Specific questions about payment, deposits, and timelines.
8. **CTA:** "Schedule a consultation for a detailed quote."
