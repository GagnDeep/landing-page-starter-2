# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page

## Intent
Clear, transparent, yet elegantly presented pricing for all services, emphasizing value over cost.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** Title "Investment Menu" or "Service Pricing".
3. **Pricing Philosophy:** A brief note on why quality matters.
4. **Stylist Tier Explanation:** Defining Junior, Senior, and Master Stylist pricing differences.
5. **Haircut & Styling Menu:** List of cutting services.
6. **Color Services Menu:** Balayage, tints, highlights.
7. **Chemical Treatments Menu:** Smoothing, perms.
8. **Spa & Rituals Menu:** Deep conditioning, scalp care.
9. **Extensions Pricing:** Base costs and consultation requirements.
10. **Bridal Packages:** Tiered bridal pricing.
11. **Men's Services Menu:** Specialized pricing for men.
12. **Add-on Services:** Glosses, toners, quick treatments.
13. **Consultation Fees:** Details on costs that apply towards services.
14. **Package Deals:** Discounts for purchasing sessions in bulk.
15. **Membership Tiers:** Pricing for VIP or subscription models.
16. **Cancellation Policy:** Clear rules on no-shows and late cancellations.
17. **Payment Methods:** Accepted forms of payment.
18. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):** Questions about tipping, hidden fees, etc.
19. **Gift Cards:** Information on purchasing gift certificates.
20. **Booking CTA & Global Footer:** Final call to action and footer.
