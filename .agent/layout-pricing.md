# Pricing & Packages Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To present investment levels with transparency but maintain a feeling of bespoke luxury. Avoid cheap-looking pricing tables; instead, present them as "Curated Collections."

## Sections

1.  **Hero: Investment in Excellence**
    *   *Intent:* Set the tone that this is a premium investment.
    *   *Visuals:* A quiet, minimalist hero. A subtle background texture, very large, elegant typography.

2.  **Introduction: Our Approach to Pricing**
    *   *Intent:* Explain that everything is customized, but provide baseline expectations.
    *   *Visuals:* A single column, beautifully typeset paragraph explaining the bespoke nature of the quotes.

3.  **The Collections (Pricing Tiers)**
    *   *Intent:* Present the baseline packages.
    *   *Visuals:* A horizontal layout or a grid of cards. Instead of aggressive "Buy Now" buttons, use "Inquire About This Collection." Use elegant typography for the package names (e.g., "The Royal Standard," "The Imperial Feast").

4.  **What is Always Included**
    *   *Intent:* Justify the price point by listing premium standard inclusions.
    *   *Visuals:* A clean, multi-column list with gold checkmarks or subtle icons.

5.  **Bespoke Additions (A la carte)**
    *   *Intent:* Show opportunities for upselling/further customization.
    *   *Visuals:* A structured list or accordion detailing extras like specialty live stations or imported ingredients.

6.  **The Quotation Process**
    *   *Intent:* Explain the steps from inquiry to final quote.
    *   *Visuals:* A simple 3-step horizontal process flow.

7.  **FAQ Preview (Pricing Specific)**
    *   *Intent:* Address common financial questions (deposits, minimums).
    *   *Visuals:* A minimal accordion component.

8.  **CTA: Request a Custom Proposal**
    *   *Intent:* The primary action for this page.
    *   *Visuals:* A large, commanding section with a beautifully styled form or a link to the contact page.
