# Layout Blueprint: Investment Page (`/pricing`)

*Intent: Qualify leads and clearly communicate the premium nature of the service without looking like a SaaS pricing page.*

## 1. Investment Hero
- **Intent**: Set the tone—this is an investment in a seamless experience.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Stark, minimalist typography-driven hero. Very little imagery.

## 2. The Value of Expertise
- **Intent**: Justify the premium pricing.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A single, impactful paragraph centered with vast padding.

## 3. Tier 1: Comprehensive Planning
- **Intent**: Outline the base package.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A refined "card" or text block. Soft background color, large serif price indicator.

## 4. Tier 2: The Ultimate Experience
- **Intent**: Outline the highest-tier package.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Similar to Tier 1 but perhaps visually elevated via darker background or larger scale.

## 5. Custom Requirements
- **Intent**: Addressing bespoke needs outside standard packages.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A muted, secondary section with smaller typography.

## 6. What Determines Cost? (Variables)
- **Intent**: Educate the client on variables (guest count, location, days).
- **Visual Rhythm**: A subtle 3-column layout.

## 7. Payment Structure
- **Intent**: Clear terms on retainers and installments.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A simple typographic breakdown.

## 8. Final Call to Action
- **Intent**: Ready to commit.
- **Visual Rhythm**: High contrast, compelling text block leading to the contact form.
