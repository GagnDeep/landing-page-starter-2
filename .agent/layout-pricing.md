# Pricing Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Pricing Hero**: Clear, transparent, and elegant introduction to the menu costs.
2. **Category Tabs/Filter**: Navigation to jump between Sweets, Bakery, Namkeen, etc.
3. **Traditional Sweets Grid**: The core pricing table or grid for by-the-kilo items.
4. **Premium Sweets Grid**: Pricing for special items (e.g., Kaju Katli, Dry Fruit sweets).
5. **Bakery Classics**: Cakes, pastries, and bread pricing.
6. **Namkeen & Savories**: Pricing for snacks.
7. **Wedding Box Packages**: Tiered pricing (Classic, Premium, Royal) for custom boxes.
8. **Corporate Hamper Tiers**: Structured pricing for business clients.
9. **Custom Cake Base Pricing**: Starting prices based on tiers and weight.
10. **Live Catering Base Rates**: Starting costs for event live counters.
11. **Bulk Order Discount Structure**: A clear table showing percentage off for large weights.
12. **Shipping & Delivery Costs**: A breakdown of local vs. national delivery fees.
13. **Packaging Upcharges**: Costs associated with premium tins or wooden boxes.
14. **Seasonal Surcharges/Notes**: Information regarding peak festival pricing dynamics.
15. **Taxes & Hidden Fees Disclaimer**: Complete transparency on what is included in the stated price.
16. **Payment Methods Accepted**: Logos and text regarding cash, UPI, cards, and corporate invoicing.
17. **Refund & Cancellation Policy Summary**: Brief rules on cancelling large orders.
18. **Downloadable Menu (PDF)**: A link to get a printable version of the pricing.
19. **Request a Custom Quote**: A form specifically for complex, large-scale orders not covered by standard pricing.
20. **FAQ on Pricing**: Questions about minimum orders, deposits, etc.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
