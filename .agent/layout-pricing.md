# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Transparent Pricing Plans").
4.  `PricingIntro`: Brief text explaining value for money and no hidden fees.
5.  `PricingToggle`: Switch between standard packages and specialized courses.
6.  `Tier1_Basic`: Card for the most basic package (e.g., 10 days, manual).
7.  `Tier2_Popular`: Highlighted card for the standard package (e.g., 15 days, automatic/manual choice).
8.  `Tier3_Comprehensive`: Card for an all-inclusive package (e.g., 20 days, theory + highway + license assistance).
9.  `HourlyRates`: Option for those who just need a few hours of practice.
10. `RefresherRates`: Specific pricing for refresher courses.
11. `AddOnServices`: Pricing for extras (mock tests, extra highway hours, vehicle maintenance class).
12. `PaymentMethods`: Icons and text detailing accepted payments (UPI, Cash, Cards).
13. `InstallmentOptions`: Information on whether payment can be split.
14. `RefundPolicySnippet`: Brief mention of the cancellation/refund policy for trust.
15. `ComparisonTable`: A detailed feature-by-feature comparison of the main tiers.
16. `SpecialOffers`: Banner for any current discounts (student discount, seasonal offers).
17. `CorporatePackages`: Information for companies needing driver training for staff.
18. `FAQ_Pricing`: 3-4 specific questions related to payments and packages.
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Call to action to choose a plan and register.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
