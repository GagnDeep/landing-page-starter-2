# Layout: Pricing (Packages)
**URL**: `/pricing`
**Intent**: Provide a transparent but premium overview of catering tiers or packages, encouraging custom quotes.

## Structure
1. **Hero**: Title: "Curated Packages".
2. **Pricing Philosophy**: A note explaining that while they offer tiers, every event is customized.
3. **Tier 1: The Classic**: Card layout with base offerings.
4. **Tier 2: The Signature**: Highlighting premium additions.
5. **Tier 3: The Royal Patiala**: The ultimate luxury package.
6. **Add-ons & Enhancements**: Live stations, custom bars, late-night snacks.
7. **FAQ (Pricing Specific)**: Addressing minimums, travel fees, etc.
8. **CTA**: "Request a Custom Proposal".
