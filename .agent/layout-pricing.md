# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page

**Goal:** Present courses and packages clearly and transparently, guiding the user to the best option for their skill level.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* Standard global announcement.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* "Invest in Your Driving Confidence" or similar heading. Clear, no-nonsense intro.
4. **Pricing Toggle / Filters**
   - *Intent:* Allow users to switch between "Beginner", "Intermediate", or "Refresher" views.
5. **Package 1: The Beginner Course (15 Days)**
   - *Intent:* Detailed card showing price, duration, and inclusions (e.g., basics, parking, license assistance).
6. **Package 2: The Crash Course (7 Days)**
   - *Intent:* Card for quick learners. Highlight price and intensive schedule.
7. **Package 3: Refresher Sessions (Hourly/Daily)**
   - *Intent:* Card for those who already have a license but lack confidence.
8. **Package 4: VIP / Weekend Only**
   - *Intent:* Premium offering for busy professionals.
9. **Add-on Services**
   - *Intent:* List additional paid services (e.g., extra RTO mock test, highway driving special session).
10. **What's Included in Every Package**
    - *Intent:* A checklist of baseline features (dual controls, patient instructor, doorstep pickup).
11. **Pricing FAQ**
    - *Intent:* Address common money questions (refunds, installment options, hidden fees).
12. **Testimonials (Value for Money)**
    - *Intent:* Reviews that mention the course was worth the investment.
13. **Corporate / Group Bookings**
    - *Intent:* Information for businesses or families wanting to book multiple slots.
14. **Gift Cards / Vouchers**
    - *Intent:* Option to purchase a driving course as a gift.
15. **Guarantee / Promise**
    - *Intent:* Reassurance (e.g., "We won't stop until you're confident").
16. **Payment Methods Accepted**
    - *Intent:* Visual list of accepted payments (UPI, Credit Cards, Cash).
17. **Need Help Choosing? (Contact CTA)**
    - *Intent:* A softer CTA to call/WhatsApp for advice on which package to pick.
18. **Final CTA (Book Now)**
    - *Intent:* Primary action button below all the pricing details.
19. **Newsletter Subscribe**
    - *Intent:* Standard lead capture.
20. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
