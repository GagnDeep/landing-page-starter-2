# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - Pricing (Investment) Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Solid or transparent over hero.

2. **Hero Section (The Investment)**
   - *Layout intent:* A serene, minimalist hero. The title "Investment" rather than "Pricing".
   - *Visual rhythm:* Very clean, lots of negative space.

3. **Pricing Philosophy**
   - *Layout intent:* A text block explaining the value of peace of mind and bespoke service.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Serif typography, centered, acting as a soft introduction to numbers.

4. **Tier 1: The Essential Collection**
   - *Layout intent:* A detailed pricing card or horizontal row.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Clean typography, a clear list of inclusions. Less emphasis than the higher tiers.

5. **Tier 2: The Signature Experience (Highlighted)**
   - *Layout intent:* The central, most prominent pricing option.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Slightly larger card, perhaps a subtle border or different background color (`--secondary`). A "Most Popular" badge.

6. **Tier 3: The Pinnacle Commission**
   - *Layout intent:* The highest-end offering, often bespoke.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Elegant, perhaps lacking a specific price (e.g., "Starting at...").

7. **What's Always Included (The Standard)**
   - *Layout intent:* A 2-column or 4-column grid of icons and short text detailing baseline features (e.g., unlimited emails, custom timeline).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Small, refined icons. Highly scannable.

8. **Custom Additions**
   - *Layout intent:* A subtle list or accordion of optional extras.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Muted text, clean layout to avoid cluttering the main pricing.

9. **FAQ (Pricing Specific)**
   - *Layout intent:* Accordion style for questions like "Do you take a percentage or flat fee?".
   - *Visual rhythm:* Minimalist dividers, smooth expansion.

10. **Call to Action (Schedule a Consultation)**
    - *Layout intent:* A strong push to get on a call to discuss custom needs.
    - *Visual rhythm:* High contrast, placed immediately after the pricing details.
