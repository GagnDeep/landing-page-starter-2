# Layout Intent: Pricing Page

**Goal:** Present pricing clearly without losing the high-end, bespoke feel. Avoid looking like a SaaS pricing table; think luxury spa menu.

## 1. Hero: Investment in Yourself
- **Visual Rhythm:** Soft, inviting header.
- **Layout:** Centered serif heading, minimal text. Focus on the concept of value and experience over mere cost.

## 2. Pricing Philosophy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Text-heavy, trustworthy.
- **Layout:** A clean, centered paragraph explaining the transparent, no-hidden-fees approach to pricing.

## 3. Tier 1 / Base Services Concept
- **Visual Rhythm:** Clean column.
- **Layout:** A distinct section defining the starting point for standard services. Not a card, but a cleanly formatted list.

## 4. Tier 2 / Premium Enhancements
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elevated styling.
- **Layout:** A section with a slightly different background color, indicating upgrades, treatments, and premium additions.

## 5. The "At-Home" Convenience Fee Structure
- **Visual Rhythm:** Informational callout.
- **Layout:** A specific block explaining travel fees or minimums required for at-home visits vs. in-salon visits.

## 6. Haircut & Styling Prices
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elegant menu list.
- **Layout:** Service name on the left, dotted leader line (or wide spacing), price on the right.

## 7. Color Service Prices
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elegant menu list.
- **Layout:** Consistent with the previous menu list. Includes notes on starting prices vs. exact quotes.

## 8. Treatment & Add-on Prices
- **Visual Rhythm:** Subtle secondary list.
- **Layout:** Smaller typography, grouped together under the main hair services.

## 9. Visual Break: The Results
- **Visual Rhythm:** High-impact image.
- **Layout:** A full-width image of a stunning final look, breaking up the numbers and text.

## 10. Nail Service Prices
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elegant menu list.
- **Layout:** Consistent menu list style applied to manicures and pedicures.

## 11. Skincare Service Prices
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elegant menu list.
- **Layout:** Consistent menu list style applied to facials and skin treatments.

## 12. Membership / Subscription Tier 1 (e.g., The Regular)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Card layout, but restrained.
- **Layout:** A distinct card emphasizing the benefits of a monthly subscription (e.g., priority booking, standard blowout).

## 13. Membership / Subscription Tier 2 (e.g., The VIP)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Highlighted card layout.
- **Layout:** A visually dominant card (perhaps inverted colors) for the highest tier membership.

## 14. Bridal & Event Packages
- **Visual Rhythm:** Lifestyle focus.
- **Layout:** Split layout. Image of a bride/event on one side, bulleted list of package inclusions and starting price on the other.

## 15. Gift Cards
- **Visual Rhythm:** Product-focused.
- **Layout:** A stylized mockup of a physical or digital gift card, with text explaining how to purchase and gift.

## 16. Cancellation Policy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Serious, clear, readable.
- **Layout:** A text block with a muted background. Clear, uncompromising but polite typography.

## 17. Payment Methods Accepted
- **Visual Rhythm:** Iconography.
- **Layout:** A simple row of elegant, monochromatic icons representing accepted cards and digital wallets.

## 18. Custom Quote Request
- **Visual Rhythm:** Form-like text area.
- **Layout:** Instructions on how to request a consultation for color corrections or complex services, with a link to Contact.

## 19. Call to Action: Secure Your Spot
- **Visual Rhythm:** Bold, definitive.
- **Layout:** Large section, compelling heading, and a direct link to the booking engine.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
