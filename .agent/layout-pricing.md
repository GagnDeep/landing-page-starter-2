# Layout Intent: Pricing & Packages Page

Luxury services rarely list exact prices upfront due to bespoke nature, so this page focuses on 'Investment' and 'Package Tiers', setting expectations and highlighting value rather than commoditizing the service.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`PricingHero`**
    *   **Intent:** Establishing the premise that every event is a bespoke investment.
    *   **Rhythm:** A clean, minimalist header, heavily relying on typography and negative space.
3.  **`TheBespokeApproach`**
    *   **Intent:** Explaining why exact pricing requires a consultation.
    *   **Rhythm:** A centered, easily readable paragraph emphasizing exclusivity and custom design.
4.  **`PackageTiers`**
    *   **Intent:** Outlining the starting points for different levels of service (e.g., The Signature Tier, The Grand Tier, The Royal Tier).
    *   **Rhythm:** A 3-column layout of tall, elegant cards. The cards should have subtle borders, soft hover states, and use typography to differentiate the tiers rather than garish colors.
5.  **`WhatToExpect`**
    *   **Intent:** Detailing the unseen value (logistics, staffing, premium sourcing).
    *   **Rhythm:** An alternating left/right layout pairing concise text with icon-like, abstract imagery.
6.  **`CustomEnhancements`**
    *   **Intent:** Listing add-ons like live food stations, celebrity chefs, or premium vintage wine pairings.
    *   **Rhythm:** A simple, elegant list or grid below the main tiers.
7.  **`ConsultationProcess`**
    *   **Intent:** A visual timeline of how pricing is eventually determined (Discovery Call -> Proposal -> Tasting -> Contract).
    *   **Rhythm:** A horizontal flowchart design with delicate connecting lines.
8.  **`FAQTeaser`** (Shared component focused on pricing/contract questions)
9.  **`InquiryCallToAction`** (Specific CTA for booking a financial consultation)
10. **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
