# A2Zee Events - Pricing/Investment Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To communicate value and the premium nature of the service without appearing transactional. Emphasize "Investment" and "Bespoke" over rigid pricing tiers.

## Structural Sections
1.  **`investment-hero`:** Minimalist header. "An Investment in Perfection."
2.  **`investment-philosophy`:** A short paragraph explaining the bespoke nature of pricing based on scale, location, and complexity.
3.  **`the-framework-tiers`:** (If applicable) 2-3 broad tiers (e.g., "Full Service Planning," "Design & Production") presented in elegant cards. Emphasize that these are starting points.
4.  **`what-is-included-list`:** A detailed, rigorous list of the comprehensive services included in the baseline fee.
5.  **`transparency-commitment`:** A section assuring clients of clear, honest vendor billing practices.
6.  **`investment-faq`:** Accordion for specific financial questions (payment structures, currency handling for destinations).
7.  **`consultation-cta`:** "Schedule a Private Consultation."
