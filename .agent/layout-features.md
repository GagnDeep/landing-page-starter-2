# A2Zee Events - Features/Services Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To clearly articulate the comprehensive nature of the services offered, balancing the romantic aspects of design with the rigorous demands of production logistics.

## Structural Sections
1.  **`services-hero`:** Clean, focused hero section. "Comprehensive Curation."
2.  **`service-detail-1-planning`:** 50/50 split layout. Image on left, detailed explanation of logistical planning on right.
3.  **`service-detail-2-design`:** 50/50 split layout. Image on right, focus on aesthetic creation and bespoke tailoring on left.
4.  **`service-detail-3-concierge`:** Highlighting guest management and hospitality services.
5.  **`service-detail-4-production`:** Technical execution, lighting, sound, structural builds.
6.  **`the-a2zee-difference`:** A 3-column highlight of what sets the service apart (e.g., in-house production vs outsourced).
7.  **`services-cta`:** Call to request a detailed service brochure.
