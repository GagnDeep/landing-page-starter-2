# Features Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Detail the specific premium features of the bespoke service (fabrics, stitching techniques, fitting guarantees).

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (The Details):** A macro shot of intricate embroidery or fabric texture.
3. **Feature 1 (Premium Fabrics):** Image on left, detailed explanation on right. Focus on sourcing and quality.
4. **Feature 2 (Handcrafted Embroidery):** Image on right, text on left. Highlighting the artisans' skill.
5. **Feature 3 (Perfect Fit Guarantee):** Centered layout. Iconography paired with text detailing the measurement and alteration process.
6. **Feature 4 (Global Delivery & Packaging):** 2-column layout showing the unboxing experience and shipping reliability.
7. **Interactive Comparison (Bespoke vs Off-the-rack):** A refined, elegant table or sliding comparison that avoids feeling like a SaaS pricing page.
8. **Call to Action:** "Begin Your Bespoke Journey."
9. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
