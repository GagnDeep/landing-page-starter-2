# Layout Intent: Features / Services Page

This page details the specific offerings of Crown Caterers. It needs to be highly structured, allowing users to easily navigate the breadth of services while maintaining the luxury aesthetic.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`FeaturesHero`**
    *   **Intent:** An elegant title section introducing the portfolio of services.
    *   **Rhythm:** Centered layout, mid-height, with a subtle textured background or slow-moving abstract video.
3.  **`ServiceIndex`**
    *   **Intent:** A quick-jump navigation or visual index of the main service categories (e.g., Wedding Catering, Corporate Galas, Destination Management).
    *   **Rhythm:** A horizontal list of text links or small, elegant image tiles that scroll the user down to the relevant section.
4.  **`DetailSection: Wedding Catering`**
    *   **Intent:** Deep dive into the primary service.
    *   **Rhythm:** A sweeping, full-width image followed by a split layout (text left, supporting images right) detailing menu design, tastings, and on-the-day execution.
5.  **`DetailSection: Destination Management`**
    *   **Intent:** Exploring the logistical prowess for overseas events.
    *   **Rhythm:** Alternating split layout (text right, image left).
6.  **`DetailSection: Event Styling & Decor`**
    *   **Intent:** Highlighting the aesthetic services beyond food.
    *   **Rhythm:** A dense, masonry image gallery demonstrating various aesthetic themes, with a sticky sidebar describing the design process.
7.  **`DetailSection: Beverage & Mixology`**
    *   **Intent:** Showcasing the bar and sommelier services.
    *   **Rhythm:** Dark, moody styling (perhaps forcing dark mode for this specific section) with rich images of cocktails and wine cellars.
8.  **`ServiceInclusions`**
    *   **Intent:** A structured list of what is included in standard packages (e.g., fine china, crystal glassware, dedicated staff ratio).
    *   **Rhythm:** A clean, multi-column list with delicate checkmark icons.
9.  **`FinalCallToAction`** (Shared component)
10. **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
