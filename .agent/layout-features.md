# Layout Blueprint: Features/Methodology Page (`/features`)

## Goal
To detail *how* Armaan Driving School teaches. This page transitions from the "who we are" to the "what you get," focusing on the curriculum, safety measures, and pedagogical approach.

## Section Architecture (15-20 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Hero Section:** "The Armaan Methodology: Engineering Confident Drivers."
3.  **Introduction to the Curriculum:** Overview of how lessons are structured (theory, practical, review).
4.  **Feature Deep Dive: Dual-Control Vehicles:** Technical breakdown of the safety cars.
5.  **Feature Deep Dive: Step-by-Step Pedagogy:** Visual roadmap showing the progression from parking lot to highway.
6.  **Feature Deep Dive: Anxiety Management Techniques:** How instructors handle nervous students (calm environment, no yelling policy).
7.  **Feature Deep Dive: Mock Driving Tests:** Explaining the simulation of the RTO test environment.
8.  **Feature Deep Dive: Defensive Driving Principles:** What sets the curriculum apart from basic "pass the test" schools.
9.  **Curriculum Breakdown - Phase 1 (Basics):** Car controls, steering, stopping.
10. **Curriculum Breakdown - Phase 2 (Traffic):** Mirrors, signals, roundabouts, basic traffic.
11. **Curriculum Breakdown - Phase 3 (Advanced):** Parking (parallel, reverse), high-speed roads, defensive maneuvers.
12. **The "No-Yell" Guarantee:** A specific trust-building block emphasizing emotional safety.
13. **Flexible Scheduling:** Explaining how booking works and accommodating different schedules.
14. **Progress Tracking:** How students and instructors monitor improvement (e.g., report cards or feedback sessions).
15. **Compare Us:** A subtle comparison chart (Armaan vs. "Standard" driving schools) focusing on safety and patience.
16. **FAQ Snippet:** Questions specifically about the teaching method.
17. **Final CTA:** "Experience the difference. Start your journey today."
18. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
