# Layout Documentation: Features Page (`/features`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
This page highlights the unique aspects of the bakery's offerings (e.g., traditional whole-wheat-jaggery bakes, custom Swiss rolls). It uses a structured, feature-heavy layout with alternating text/image blocks to clearly communicate quality and specialization.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Features-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`Features-02-PageHero`**: Left-aligned heading ("The Verma Difference"), subtext, and a complementary right-aligned abstract baking graphic.
3.  **`Features-03-FeatureListNav`**: Sticky in-page navigation sidebar or top bar to jump between key features (Ingredients, Techniques, Packaging).
4.  **`Features-04-IngredientFocusSection`**: Large section dedicated to high-quality ingredients.
5.  **`Features-05-IngredientHighlight`**: Alternating rows (Image Left/Text Right, then Text Left/Image Right) for items like Jaggery, Whole Wheat, Butter.
6.  **`Features-06-TechniqueShowcase`**: Darker, moody section (using the dark mode palette) highlighting the overnight resting or slow baking processes.
7.  **`Features-07-TechniqueStepBlock`**: Numbered steps with iconography explaining the process.
8.  **`Features-08-CustomCraftsmanship`**: Focus on the bespoke nature of the cakes. Large central image of a cake being decorated.
9.  **`Features-09-DetailZoomGrid`**: 4-square grid of macro photography showing textures (crumb, frosting, crust).
10. **`Features-10-DietaryOptionsBanner`**: Clean, light block outlining vegan, gluten-free, or sugar-free options.
11. **`Features-11-PackagingDesign`**: Section showcasing the premium boxes and sustainable packaging.
12. **`Features-12-FreshnessGuarantee`**: Text block with a badge or seal graphic emphasizing daily baking.
13. **`Features-13-CorporateGifting`**: Two-column layout detailing bulk orders and custom corporate boxes.
14. **`Features-14-EventCatering`**: Image-heavy block showing wedding dessert tables or event setups.
15. **`Features-15-ComparisonTable`**: Elegant, minimalist table (no harsh borders) comparing generic baked goods to Verma's artisanal bakes.
16. **`Features-16-CustomerTestimonial`**: A single, powerful quote from a renowned local chef or long-time patron.
17. **`Features-17-FAQPreview`**: Accordion layout showing 3-4 most common questions related to the features.
18. **`Features-18-TasteTheDifferenceCTA`**: Large call to action leading to the pricing or contact page.
19. **`Features-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`Features-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
