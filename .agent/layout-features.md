# Layout Blueprint: Features / Services Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Our Training Features & Services").
4.  `ServiceOverview`: Brief introduction to the range of training offered.
5.  `AutomaticTrainingDetail`: In-depth section on modern automatic car classes.
6.  `ManualTrainingDetail`: Details on traditional manual transmission training.
7.  `RealTrafficCurriculum`: Detailed breakdown of how real-traffic driving is taught.
8.  `HighwayTraining`: Specific focus on high-speed highway driving safety.
9.  `NightDriving`: Information on evening/night classes (up to 11PM) for varied condition experience.
10. `RefresherCourses`: Courses designed for licensed drivers needing confidence boosts.
11. `LicenseAssistance`: Details on how the school helps with the driving test and license application process.
12. `MockTests`: Information on practice tests that simulate the real driving exam.
13. `TheoreticalClasses`: Details on classroom sessions covering traffic rules and signs.
14. `VehicleMaintenanceBasics`: Optional modules teaching basic car care (changing a tire, checking fluids).
15. `CustomizedSchedules`: Highlighting the flexible 6AM-11PM timing options.
16. `DoorstepPickup`: Information on pick-up and drop-off services for students.
17. `FemaleInstructorOption`: Mentioning if this service is available (important for some demographics).
18. `ProgressTracking`: How instructors evaluate and report student progress.
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Call to action to view pricing or book a service.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
