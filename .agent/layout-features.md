# Layout Intent: Features Page

## Core Intent
Detail the specific tailoring services and unique features that set Stitching Solutions apart from generic tailors. Focus on the "alteration-free" guarantee.

## Key Sections
1. **Hero Header:** Focus on "The Perfect Fit, The First Time."
2. **Service Breakdown:** Detailed sections for different garment types (Bridal, Formal Wear, Everyday Wear, Uniforms).
3. **The "Alteration-Free" Guarantee Explained:** A deep dive into the measurement and fitting process that ensures perfection.
4. **Fabric & Embellishment Handling:** Information on how delicate fabrics, heavy embroideries, and specific materials are treated with care.
5. **Express/Priority Services:** Details on turnaround times and rush order capabilities for reliable delivery.
6. **Consultation Process:** Step-by-step of what happens during a styling and measurement session.
7. **Quality Assurance:** The final checks performed before a garment is handed over to the client.
8. **Call to Action:** Prompt to book a specific service.
