# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - Features (Services) Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Solid or transparent over hero.

2. **Hero Section (Our Expertise)**
   - *Layout intent:* A stunning, high-detail image of a beautifully styled table setting or venue. Bold typography.
   - *Visual rhythm:* High impact, immediately establishing authority in design and execution.

3. **Introduction / Service Philosophy**
   - *Layout intent:* A centered, medium-width text block explaining the holistic approach to event planning.
   - *Visual rhythm:* High padding, slow fade-in animation.

4. **Service 1: Full-Service Destination Planning**
   - *Layout intent:* Large image on the left, detailed description and bulleted list on the right.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Expansive and thorough. Clear hierarchy in the list items.

5. **Service 2: Event Design & Styling**
   - *Layout intent:* Large image on the right, detailed description and bulleted list on the left (alternating from Service 1).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Service 1 but mirrored for visual interest.

6. **Service 3: Guest Concierge & Logistics**
   - *Layout intent:* Large image on the left, detailed description and bulleted list on the right.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Emphasizes the organizational aspect; perhaps uses a grid of smaller logistical icons.

7. **The Add-Ons / A La Carte Options**
   - *Layout intent:* A 3-column grid of smaller service cards (e.g., Rehearsal Dinner, Post-Wedding Brunch).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Compact cards with subtle borders and hover effects.

8. **The Toolkit (Technology & Tools)**
   - *Layout intent:* A section highlighting the bespoke software or client portals used for seamless planning.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A sleek, slightly darker section to emphasize "tech" but kept within the luxury aesthetic.

9. **Case Study Teaser**
   - *Layout intent:* A link to a specific, highly complex wedding they executed perfectly.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A full-width banner with an overlapping text card.

10. **Call to Action (Request the Brochure)**
    - *Layout intent:* An invitation to download a detailed service guide or book a call.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Centered, elegant, using the primary brand color as the background.
