# Features Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
This page details the specific services and unique selling propositions of Bawa Caterers. It should read like a menu of high-end capabilities, focusing on the scale and detail of their offerings.

## Sections

1.  **Hero: The Art of Catering**
    *   *Intent:* Introduce the breadth of services.
    *   *Visuals:* A dynamic video loop of a bustling, high-end kitchen or a beautifully arranged buffet. Clean, centered typography.

2.  **Service Overview Grid**
    *   *Intent:* Quick navigation to specific capabilities.
    *   *Visuals:* A clean CSS grid (e.g., 3x2). Each card features a muted background, a subtle icon, and a title (e.g., "Live Stalls," "Plated Dinners," "Bespoke Desserts").

3.  **Deep Dive: The Live Stall Experience**
    *   *Intent:* Highlight the most popular/dramatic offering.
    *   *Visuals:* An expansive, full-width image section followed by an asymmetric layout detailing the types of stalls (Tandoor, Chaat, etc.).

4.  **Deep Dive: Traditional Feasts (Dham/Langar Style)**
    *   *Intent:* Emphasize cultural authenticity.
    *   *Visuals:* Warm, rich imagery. Text heavy, emphasizing the communal and traditional aspects, set against a sandstone background.

5.  **Deep Dive: Modern Fusion Presentations**
    *   *Intent:* Show versatility and modern culinary trends.
    *   *Visuals:* Sleek, minimalist plating imagery. Sharp, clean layout lines.

6.  **The Logistics of Scale**
    *   *Intent:* Prove they can handle massive guest lists flawlessly.
    *   *Visuals:* Infographic style. Clean statistics and structural diagrams showing how they manage flow and service for 1000+ guests.

7.  **Customization & Tastings**
    *   *Intent:* Explain the personalization process.
    *   *Visuals:* A step-by-step layout. Images of couples at tasting sessions.

8.  **CTA: Schedule a Tasting**
    *   *Intent:* Convert interest into action.
    *   *Visuals:* A stark, high-contrast block demanding attention.
