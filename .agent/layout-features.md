# Features Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

*(Note: "Features" in this context translates to the distinctive services, product lines, and unique selling propositions of the bakery).*

## Sections & Intent

1. **Features Hero**: Highlighting what makes Verma's uniquely superior.
2. **Authentic Recipes**: Emphasizing the unbroken lineage of the recipes used.
3. **Custom Wedding Boxes (Bhaji)**: Detailing the bespoke packaging and sweet selection for weddings.
4. **Corporate Gifting Program**: B2B services for festivals (Diwali, etc.) and corporate events.
5. **Live Counter/Catering Services**: Explaining the capability to have fresh jalebis or chaat at events.
6. **Sugar-Free & Diet-Conscious Range**: Spotlighting modern adaptations of traditional sweets.
7. **Same-Day Local Delivery**: Details on the logistics and freshness guarantee for local orders.
8. **Nationwide Shipping**: Information on which dry sweets/bakery items can be shipped across the country.
9. **Custom Cake Design**: Showcasing the bakery side with bespoke celebration cakes.
10. **Festive Pre-Orders**: How the system works during peak rush times.
11. **Bulk Ordering Discounts**: Information for large family gatherings or wholesale.
12. **The Tasting Experience**: Inviting customers for a tasting session for wedding orders.
13. **Premium Packaging Options**: A visual showcase of the different tin, wood, and cardboard box designs.
14. **Quality Control Process**: A technical but reassuring look at hygiene and consistency.
15. **Allergen Information Matrix**: A clear, accessible guide to nuts, dairy, and gluten.
16. **Customer Portal/Account Features**: Explaining how regular customers can save favorites and track orders.
17. **Loyalty Program**: Details on rewards for frequent buyers.
18. **Interactive Sweet Selector**: A conceptual UI section helping users choose sweets based on taste profile (e.g., nutty, syrupy, dry).
19. **FAQ on Services**: Quick answers related to these specific features.
20. **Start an Order CTA**: A strong push to the pricing/contact page.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
