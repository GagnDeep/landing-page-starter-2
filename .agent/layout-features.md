# Features & Services Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Detail the specific offerings of Oasis Caterers. Emphasize the bespoke nature of the menus, the scale of execution, and the elegance of the service.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation.
2. **Hero Section (Our Offerings)**: "Bespoke Culinary Experiences." Minimal text, strong background.
3. **Introduction**: A brief overview of the holistic catering approach.
4. **Service 1: The Grand Wedding**: Large dedicated section with image and detailed description.
5. **Service 2: Corporate Galas**: Distinct section highlighting professional execution.
6. **Service 3: Intimate Gatherings**: Focus on bespoke, hyper-tailored menus.
7. **The Menu Design Process**: Step-by-step horizontal flow.
8. **Dietary & Cultural Expertise**: Section detailing accommodations (e.g., Vegan, Halal, Jain).
9. **The Tasting Session**: Description of the "Evening Fitting" experience.
10. **Venue Coordination**: Information on how Oasis works with luxury venues.
11. **Staffing & Presentation**: Details on the family-style service and uniform elegance.
12. **Equipment & Decor Provisioning**: Highlight on table settings, flatware, and linen options.
13. **Beverage & Bar Services**: If applicable, detailing mixology and sommelier services.
14. **Service Comparison Table**: A clean, elegant matrix of what is included in different tiers.
15. **Case Study 1**: A brief look at a successful large-scale event.
16. **Case Study 2**: A brief look at a complex, bespoke request fulfilled.
17. **FAQ Snippet**: Services-specific questions.
18. **Downloadable Brochure**: Form to request a detailed PDF.
19. **Primary Call to Action**: "Request a Proposal."
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
