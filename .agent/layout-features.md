# Layout Blueprint: Features (Services Detail) Page

## Intent
Deep dive into the specific techniques, tools, and premium experiences that set the salon apart.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** Focus on a specific high-end technique (e.g., precision cutting).
3. **The Bespoke Consultation:** Detailed explanation of the initial assessment process.
4. **Advanced Color Techniques:** Deep dive into balayage, highlights, and color correction.
5. **The Butterfly Cut Specialization:** Details on this specific trending service.
6. **Restorative Spa Treatments:** Information on high-end hair and scalp therapies.
7. **Premium Product Arsenal:** Brands used (e.g., Kerastase, Olaplex).
8. **Tools of the Trade:** Highlighting top-tier equipment (e.g., Dyson).
9. **The Bridal Suite:** Details on the dedicated space and experience for brides.
10. **VIP Amenities:** Beverages, comfortable seating, relaxing ambiance.
11. **Men's Grooming:** Specific features for male clients.
12. **Extensions & Volume:** Information on luxury hair extensions.
13. **Texture & Smoothing:** Keratin and rebonding specifics.
14. **Scalp Diagnostics:** Advanced scalp analysis features.
15. **Custom Formulations:** How color and treatments are personalized.
16. **Post-Care Guidance:** The education provided to clients after a service.
17. **Hygiene & Safety Protocols:** Commitment to a clean environment.
18. **Technology Integration:** Easy online booking and profile management.
19. **Service Packages:** Bundled features for full transformations.
20. **Booking CTA & Global Footer:** Final call to action and footer.
