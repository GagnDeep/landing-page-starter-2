# Layout Blueprint: Features (Services) Page

## Structural Rhythm
An elegant catalog of services, structured logically but presented artistically.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:** "Our Services" with an editorial subtitle.
2. **Bespoke Tailoring:** Deep dive section with expansive imagery and text.
3. **Custom Embroidery:** Details on the hand-stitching process.
4. **Bridal & Trousseau:** Specialized offering section.
5. **Corporate & Uniforms:** B2B or specialized bulk services.
6. **Gifting Services:** Highlighting the premium gift packaging and experience.
7. **Process Timeline:** A visual, horizontal or vertical timeline of how a garment is created.
8. **CTA:** "Book your service today."
