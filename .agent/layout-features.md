# Layout Intent: Features (Services) Page

**Goal:** Clearly and enticingly outline the specific services and features of the salon experience, maintaining the luxurious editorial aesthetic.

## 1. Hero: Service Menu Introduction
- **Visual Rhythm:** Minimalist, text-focused header.
- **Layout:** Large, centered serif title ("Our Services") with a brief, elegant subtitle. No hero image to keep focus on the offerings.

## 2. Service Modality Selector
- **Visual Rhythm:** Interactive toggle/tabs.
- **Layout:** A prominent, beautifully styled toggle switch or tab list to switch between "At-Home" and "In-Salon" features.

## 3. Feature: The At-Home Setup
- **Visual Rhythm:** Detailed, instructional but luxurious.
- **Layout:** A wide image showing the pristine at-home setup process, with a numbered list overlaid or to the side explaining the steps.

## 4. Feature: Premium Products
- **Visual Rhythm:** Highlight/Spotlight.
- **Layout:** Centered image of a product tray. Text below detailing the exclusive brands used.

## 5. Feature: Time-Saving Convenience
- **Visual Rhythm:** Text-heavy, graphic break.
- **Layout:** Large typography emphasizing time saved, perhaps utilizing a stylized clock or abstract geometric shape as a subtle background.

## 6. Category: Hair Services
- **Visual Rhythm:** Bold category divider.
- **Layout:** Full-width block with a deep background color, containing a large serif heading.

## 7. Hair Service Item 1 (e.g., Cut & Style)
- **Visual Rhythm:** List item, elegant row.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned service name and description. Right-aligned subtle pricing or duration tag. A thin border separates items.

## 8. Hair Service Item 2 (e.g., Color & Highlights)
- **Visual Rhythm:** List item, consistent.
- **Layout:** Same as Item 1.

## 9. Hair Service Image Break
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial pause.
- **Layout:** A large, highly stylized image of a finished hairstyle, interrupting the list format.

## 10. Category: Nail Services
- **Visual Rhythm:** Category divider.
- **Layout:** Matches the Hair Services category divider style.

## 11. Nail Service Item 1 (e.g., Signature Manicure)
- **Visual Rhythm:** List item.
- **Layout:** Matches the established list item layout.

## 12. Nail Service Item 2 (e.g., Spa Pedicure)
- **Visual Rhythm:** List item.
- **Layout:** Matches the established list item layout.

## 13. Category: Skincare & Facials
- **Visual Rhythm:** Category divider.
- **Layout:** Matches previous category dividers.

## 14. Skincare Service Item 1 (e.g., Rejuvenating Facial)
- **Visual Rhythm:** List item.
- **Layout:** Matches the established list item layout.

## 15. The Consultation Process
- **Visual Rhythm:** Process breakdown.
- **Layout:** A 3-step horizontal timeline explaining how stylists consult with clients before beginning any service.

## 16. Feature: Group Bookings & Events
- **Visual Rhythm:** Lifestyle imagery focus.
- **Layout:** Split layout featuring an image of a bridal party or group on one side, and details about booking events on the other.

## 17. Custom/Bespoke Requests
- **Visual Rhythm:** Text-heavy, intimate.
- **Layout:** Centered paragraph explaining how clients can request custom services not listed on the menu.

## 18. FAQ Snippet (Services Specific)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Accordion.
- **Layout:** 2-3 specific questions regarding preparation or aftercare, styled cleanly.

## 19. Call to Action: Book an Appointment
- **Visual Rhythm:** High contrast, urgent but elegant.
- **Layout:** Large block, primary brand background color, white text, bold button leading to the booking flow.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
