# Layout Blueprint: Features Page

**Goal:** Deep dive into the specific advantages, methodologies, and tools that make Capital Driving School the best choice.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* Standard global announcement strip.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* "The Capital Advantage" heading. Focus on what sets them apart.
4. **Feature 1: Patient & Calm Instruction**
   - *Intent:* Detailed explanation of the teaching methodology for nervous drivers. Image/text split.
5. **Feature 2: 24x7 Flexible Timings**
   - *Intent:* Emphasize convenience. Highlight early morning and late-night availability.
6. **Feature 3: Modern, Dual-Controlled Fleet**
   - *Intent:* Focus on safety. Showcase the cars and explain dual controls.
7. **Feature 4: Doorstep Pickup & Drop-off**
   - *Intent:* Convenience factor. Explain the service area and how it works.
8. **Feature 5: RTO / License Assistance**
   - *Intent:* Value-add service. Detail how they handle the paperwork and RTO visits.
9. **Feature 6: Mock Tests & Simulation**
   - *Intent:* Preparation advantage. Explain how they prepare students for the final RTO test.
10. **Feature Comparison Table**
    - *Intent:* "Us vs. Them" matrix highlighting the premium features offered.
11. **Video Walkthrough**
    - *Intent:* A video demonstrating a typical lesson or the safety features of the car.
12. **Student Success Stories (Feature Specific)**
    - *Intent:* Testimonials specifically praising the features mentioned above.
13. **Safety Protocol Overview**
    - *Intent:* Reassure students of hygiene, vehicle maintenance, and safety measures.
14. **Curriculum Breakdown**
    - *Intent:* Step-by-step look at what is covered in a standard course (theory, practical, parking, highway).
15. **Interactive Feature Explorer**
    - *Intent:* A tabbed or accordion section allowing users to click through different benefits.
16. **Instructor Qualifications**
    - *Intent:* Detail the rigorous training and certification the instructors undergo.
17. **FAQ (Features & Services)**
    - *Intent:* Answer questions about specific features (e.g., "Can I choose my car?").
18. **Bottom CTA (Book Your Slot)**
    - *Intent:* Strong call to action to start learning.
19. **Newsletter Subscribe**
    - *Intent:* Standard lead capture.
20. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
