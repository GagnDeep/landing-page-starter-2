# Layout: Features (Services) Page

This page details the specific services offered, emphasizing the "full-service" and premium nature of the studio.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Elegant, abstract or highly artistic hero image. Title: "Our Services" or "The Art of Celebration".
2.  **Service Overview:** A brief introduction to the comprehensive nature of the offerings.
3.  **Feature 1: Wedding Photography:** Detailed breakdown. Includes style descriptions (candid, traditional portraits). Image gallery slider.
4.  **Feature 2: Cinematic Films:** Focus on video services. Highlights storyboarding, drone footage, and editing style. Embedded video example.
5.  **Feature 3: Pre-Wedding Shoots:** Emphasize location scouting, styling advice, and concept creation.
6.  **Feature 4: The NRI Experience:** Specific section addressing the logistics and specialized care for international clients.
7.  **The Deliverables:** Explanation of what clients receive (e.g., heirloom albums, digital galleries, USBs). Highlighting premium quality.
8.  **FAQ (Services Specific):** Accordion for common service-related questions.
9.  **Call to Action:** "Inquire about availability" leading to Contact.
