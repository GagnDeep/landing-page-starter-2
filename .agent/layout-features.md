# Features & Services Page Layout Map

**Goal:** Detail the specific services offered, emphasizing the specialties (Nails, Curly Hair, Bridal).

## Section 1: Hero Header
- **Visuals:** Elegant typography on a muted background.
- **Structure:** "Our Curated Services".

## Section 2: Service Navigation Sticky Bar
- **Visuals:** Sticky horizontal menu.
- **Structure:** Anchor links: Nails, Hair, Bridal, Aesthetics.

## Section 3: The Nail Studio (Chrome & Art)
- **Visuals:** Rich imagery of nail art.
- **Structure:** Detailed descriptions of gel extensions, chrome finishes, BIAB, and bespoke nail art.

## Section 4: Advanced Hair Studio

- **Structure:** Cuts, coloring (balayage, vivids), and specialized curly hair treatments.

## Section 5: The Bridal & NRI Suite
- **Visuals:** Luxurious, soft, romantic imagery.

## Section 6: Add-on Aesthetics
- **Visuals:** Clean, clinical yet warm imagery.
- **Structure:** Brows, lashes, and skin prep services.

## Section 7: Booking CTA

- **Structure:** "Ready to transform? Book your consultation."
