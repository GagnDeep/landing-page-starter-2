# Layout Blueprint: Features Page

## Intent
To logically breakdown the specific operational, culinary, and service features that make Harbhajan's Catering the premium choice in Patiala.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** Crisp, modern header stating "The Harbhajan Difference".
3. **Introduction:** A brief paragraph explaining the commitment to excellence.
4. **Feature Category 1: The Culinary Experience:** Section header.
5. **Feature 1.1: Pure Vegetarian:** Details on pure veg preparation.
6. **Feature 1.2: Regional Specialties:** Focus on authentic Punjabi recipes.
7. **Feature 1.3: Live Counters:** Description of interactive food stations (Chaat, Tandoor).
8. **Feature Category 2: Service & Presentation:** Section header.
9. **Feature 2.1: Premium Tableware:** Details on the luxury presentation.
10. **Feature 2.2: Trained Waitstaff:** Emphasis on professional, courteous service.
11. **Feature 2.3: Uniformed Attendants:** Visual consistency in service.
12. **Feature Category 3: Logistics & Reliability:** Section header.
13. **Feature 3.1: On-time Guarantee:** Punctuality promise.
14. **Feature 3.2: Hygiene Standards:** Food safety and kitchen hygiene details.
15. **Feature 3.3: Backup Planning:** How contingencies are handled.
16. **Feature Comparison Table:** A table comparing Harbhajan's features against "Standard Caterers" to highlight the premium value.
17. **Client Testimonial (Feature-focused):** A specific quote praising the execution and service.
18. **Interactive Menu Preview:** A small interactive component to explore sample menu items.
19. **Downloadable Brochure:** A CTA to download a PDF of all features and services.
20. **Next Step CTA:** Link to Pricing or Contact.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
Highly structured and scannable. Uses alternating left/right image-and-text blocks for detailed features, and a clean grid for smaller features, culminating in a definitive comparison table.
