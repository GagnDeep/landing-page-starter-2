# Layout Blueprint: Features/Services Page (`/features`)

*Intent: A comprehensive breakdown of what exactly Guide Events provides, positioned as exclusive offerings.*

## 1. Services Hero
- **Intent**: Introduce the concept of full-service planning.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A dark, moody hero image with a large, delicate serif headline overlay.

## 2. Full-Service Coordination
- **Intent**: The flagship offering detailed.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large image left, dense but structured text block right.

## 3. Design & Styling (Creative Direction)
- **Intent**: Focus on the aesthetic offering.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large image right, dense structured text block left.

## 4. Guest Experience Management
- **Intent**: Highlighting concierge-level service for guests.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A 3-column grid highlighting transport, accommodation, and welcome experiences.

## 5. Vendor Curation
- **Intent**: Explaining the exclusive network of partners.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A text-heavy section with an accompanying collage of vendor-related imagery.

## 6. Inclusions & Exclusions
- **Intent**: Setting boundaries and clear expectations.
- **Visual Rhythm**: An elegant, two-column typographic list (no harsh borders).

## 7. Service Process Timeline
- **Intent**: A visual journey of the timeline from booking to the event day.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A vertical timeline or a horizontal scrolling step-by-step path.

## 8. Final Call to Action
- **Intent**: Direct path to inquiry.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimalist, expansive background block.
