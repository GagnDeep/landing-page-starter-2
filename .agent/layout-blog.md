# Layout: Blog Page

1. **Hero Header:** "The Tailor's Journal" - Insights on fabric, style, and care.
2. **Featured Post:** Large hero card for the most recent or most important article.
3. **Category Filter:** Simple text links (e.g., All, Style Guides, Fabric Care, Behind the Seams).
4. **Article Grid:** 3-column masonry or standard grid for older posts.
5. **Newsletter Signup:** Inline form to subscribe for updates.
6. **CTA:** "Book a Consultation" sticky banner at the bottom.
