# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - Blog (Journal) Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Solid header to support search/filter functionality immediately below.

2. **Hero Section (The Journal)**
   - *Layout intent:* A featured article spanning the full width, acting as the hero.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Large image, text overlaid or placed beautifully adjacent.

3. **Category Filters**
   - *Layout intent:* A horizontal sticky or static bar with categories (e.g., Real Weddings, Inspiration, Tips).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Elegant pill-shaped buttons or simple underlined text links.

4. **Latest Posts Grid (Top row)**
   - *Layout intent:* A 2 or 3 column grid of the most recent articles.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Large cards, image-heavy, distinct serif titles.

5. **Newsletter Interstitial**
   - *Layout intent:* A break in the grid to invite users to subscribe to the digest.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A full-width band with a contrasting background color.

6. **Editor's Picks / Trending**
   - *Layout intent:* A specialized layout (perhaps one large card on the left, two smaller ones stacked on the right).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Asymmetrical grid to break up the monotony of standard rows.

7. **Standard Posts Grid (Pagination)**
   - *Layout intent:* A continuation of the 3-column grid for older posts.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent, clean spacing.

8. **Pagination Controls**
   - *Layout intent:* "Next / Previous" or numbered pagination at the bottom of the list.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Minimalist, subtle hover states.

9. **Instagram Feed Teaser**
   - *Layout intent:* A horizontal row of recent Instagram images.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Edge-to-edge images, zero gap or very small gap (`spacing-xs`).

10. **Global Footer**
    - *Layout intent:* Consistent footer.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Standard layout.
