# Layout Blueprint: Blog Page

## Intent
Share styling tips, salon news, and trend forecasts to build authority and improve SEO.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** "The Editorial" or "Journal" with a featured article highlight.
3. **Featured Article (Hero Content):** Large image, title, and excerpt of the latest/most important post.
4. **Categories Navigation:** Horizontal scroll or pill list (e.g., Trends, Care, News).
5. **Latest Posts Grid:** 6-9 recent articles in a clean card layout.
6. **Trending Topic 1 Section:** Articles specifically about "Color Trends".
7. **Trending Topic 2 Section:** Articles specifically about "Hair Health".
8. **Stylist Spotlights:** Interviews or tips directly from team members.
9. **Video Content Teaser:** Embedded tutorials or salon tours.
10. **Client Transformation Stories:** Deep dives into specific before/after cases.
11. **Product Reviews:** In-depth looks at retail products.
12. **Seasonal Guides:** E.g., "Summer Frizz Control" or "Winter Hydration".
13. **Local Events:** Happenings in Chandigarh relevant to the brand.
14. **Newsletter Signup (Inline):** Break up the content with a subscription box.
15. **Popular Posts Sidebar/List:** The most read articles.
16. **Tags Cloud:** For granular searching.
17. **Search Bar:** Functional search for the blog.
18. **Pagination/Load More:** Navigation for older posts.
19. **Booking CTA (Banner):** Remind readers they can book the looks they read about.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
