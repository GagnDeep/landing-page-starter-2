# Layout Blueprint: Blog Page

## Intent
To establish authority in culinary arts and event planning, providing SEO value and engaging content about Punjabi food, wedding tips, and Harbhajan's news.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** "The Saffron Journal" or similar thematic title, with a search bar.
3. **Featured Post Highlight:** A large, prominent card for the latest or most important article.
4. **Category Filter/Navigation:** Horizontal scrollable list or pills (e.g., Recipes, Wedding Tips, News).
5. **Latest Posts Grid (Row 1):** Grid of standard article cards.
6. **Post Card 1:** Image, category badge, title, excerpt, date.
7. **Post Card 2:** Image, category badge, title, excerpt, date.
8. **Post Card 3:** Image, category badge, title, excerpt, date.
9. **Newsletter Signup Banner:** Mid-page break offering recipe updates.
10. **Popular Posts Title:** Section header.
11. **Popular Post 1:** Compact list view item.
12. **Popular Post 2:** Compact list view item.
13. **Popular Post 3:** Compact list view item.
14. **Latest Posts Grid (Row 2):** More standard article cards.
15. **Post Card 4:** Content.
16. **Post Card 5:** Content.
17. **Post Card 6:** Content.
18. **Pagination/Load More:** Button to view older posts.
19. **Author/Chef Spotlight:** A small widget highlighting the main author or chef contributing to the blog.
20. **Event Catering CTA:** A subtle reminder that they can book the catering service discussed in the articles.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
A classic editorial layout. Employs a hierarchy of scale (one large featured post, a grid of secondary posts, and a list of popular posts) to guide reading behavior, using the brand's serif font for article titles to maintain the elegant aesthetic.
