# Layout Blueprint: Journal/Blog Page (`/blog`)

*Intent: Share real weddings, industry insights, and establish thought leadership.*

## 1. Journal Hero
- **Intent**: Welcome to the editorial side of Guide Events.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A featured article hero. Massive image taking up 70% of the viewport, with article title overlapping.

## 2. Category Navigation
- **Intent**: Filter between Real Weddings, Inspiration, and Advice.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Very subtle, inline text links with an elegant active state.

## 3. Article Grid
- **Intent**: Display recent posts.
- **Visual Rhythm**: An asymmetrical masonry grid. Some images large, some small. Focus on stunning photography.

## 4. Newsletter Signup
- **Intent**: Capture emails for interested clients.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A sleek, full-width strip with a minimal input field.

## 5. Featured Wedding Teaser
- **Intent**: Highlight one spectacular event in the middle of the feed.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Full-width break in the grid.

## 6. Pagination/Load More
- **Intent**: Access to older content.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A subtle, elegant ghost button.
