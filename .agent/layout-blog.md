# Layout Blueprint: Blog Page

## Structural Rhythm
An editorial magazine feel, focusing on high-quality imagery and readable typography.

## Sections

1. **Hero / Featured Post:** A large banner highlighting the latest or most important editorial.
2. **Category Navigation:** Minimalist text links to filter (e.g., Styling, Craftsmanship, News).
3. **Recent Articles Grid:** Asymmetrical grid of article cards (image, title, brief excerpt).
4. **Newsletter Integration:** In-line block encouraging sign-ups for styling tips.
5. **Archive / Pagination:** Elegant "Load More" or numbered pagination.
