# Layout Blueprint: Blog Page

**Goal:** Provide valuable driving tips, RTO updates, and school news to build SEO and establish authority.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* Standard global announcement.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* "Driving Tips & News" heading.
4. **Featured Article**
   - *Intent:* Large, prominent card for the most recent or most important blog post.
5. **Category Filter / Search**
   - *Intent:* Allow users to filter by "Tips for Beginners", "RTO Rules", "Car Maintenance", etc.
6. **Recent Posts Grid**
   - *Intent:* Standard grid layout (3 columns) of the latest articles.
7. **Popular / Trending Posts Sidebar (or inline)**
   - *Intent:* Highlight the most read articles.
8. **Newsletter Subscribe (Prominent)**
   - *Intent:* "Get driving tips in your inbox". High conversion placement.
9. **Article Grid (Continued)**
   - *Intent:* More posts, with pagination or "Load More" button.
10. **Author Spotlight**
    - *Intent:* Brief bio of Jeet Singh or the editorial team writing the tips.
11. **Video Content Section**
    - *Intent:* Highlight YouTube tutorials or video tips if available.
12. **Student Spotlight Category**
    - *Intent:* Dedicated section for long-form student success stories.
13. **FAQ (Driving General)**
    - *Intent:* Quick answers to common driving questions linked to full articles.
14. **Call to Action (Book a Lesson)**
    - *Intent:* Convert readers into customers.
15. **Social Media Feed Integration**
    - *Intent:* Show recent Instagram or Facebook posts.
16. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
