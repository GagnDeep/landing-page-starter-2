# Layout Intent: Blog Page

## Core Intent
Provide editorial content, style guides, behind-the-scenes looks, and tailoring tips to establish authority and engage the community.

## Key Sections
1. **Featured Article Header:** A large, prominent highlight of the latest or most important post.
2. **Category Navigation:** Elegant pill-shaped filters (e.g., "Style Guides", "Bridal", "Behind the Seams").
3. **Article Grid:** A clean, image-led grid of recent posts with elegant typography for titles and short excerpts.
4. **Newsletter Signup:** Integrated seamlessly to capture readers interested in ongoing style advice.
