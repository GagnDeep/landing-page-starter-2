# Layout Documentation: Blog Page (`/blog`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
The Blog (or "Journal") should feel like an editorial food magazine. It emphasizes large, beautiful cover images, elegant typography for article titles, and a clean, readable layout for the content itself.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Blog-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`Blog-02-JournalHero`**: Clean header "The Bakery Journal" with a brief subtext about sharing recipes and stories.
3.  **`Blog-03-FeaturedArticle`**: Large hero-style block for the latest or most important post. Full-width image with overlaid or adjacent text.
4.  **`Blog-04-CategoryTags`**: Horizontal scrolling list of tags (Recipes, Behind the Scenes, Heritage).
5.  **`Blog-05-ArticleGrid`**: Standard 2 or 3-column masonry or even grid for regular posts.
6.  **`Blog-06-ArticleCard`**: Image top, small category tag, elegant serif title, brief excerpt, date, and reading time.
7.  **`Blog-07-Pagination`**: Elegant "Next/Previous" or numbered pagination links.
8.  **`Blog-08-NewsletterInline`**: A block inserted within the grid to encourage newsletter signups.
9.  **`Blog-09-ArticlePageHeader`**: (For individual posts) Huge serif title, author info, date, and a very large hero image.
10. **`Blog-10-ArticleContentBody`**: Narrow, highly readable text column. Generous line height.
11. **`Blog-11-PullQuote`**: Stylized block for highlighting important quotes within the article.
12. **`Blog-12-InlineImageGallery`**: 2x2 or 1x2 grid of images within the article body.
13. **`Blog-13-RecipeBlock`**: A distinct, boxed layout specifically for recipes (Ingredients list left, Instructions right).
14. **`Blog-14-AuthorBio`**: Small section at the bottom of the article with author photo and brief description.
15. **`Blog-15-ShareButtons`**: Minimalist icons to share the article on social media.
16. **`Blog-16-RelatedArticles`**: A smaller 3-column grid showing suggested reading at the end of a post.
17. **`Blog-17-CommentsSection`**: (If applicable) Clean, threaded layout for community discussion.
18. **`Blog-18-BackToJournalCTA`**: Simple link to return to the main blog listing.
19. **`Blog-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`Blog-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
