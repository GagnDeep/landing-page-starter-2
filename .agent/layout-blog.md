# Layout Intent: Blog Page (`/blog`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Title: "The Guide Events Journal". Subtitle: Insights, trends, and real weddings.
3. **Featured Article:** Large hero-style card for the latest or most important post.
4. **Category Filter:** Horizontal scrollable list of categories (e.g., Real Weddings, Decor Trends, Planning Advice, NRI Guides).
5. **Recent Posts Grid:** A 3-column grid of article cards (thumbnail, category label, title, excerpt).
6. **Newsletter Signup:** In-line banner interrupting the grid to capture emails.
7. **Popular Posts:** A sidebar or dedicated section listing top-read articles.
8. **Real Weddings Highlight:** A visually distinct carousel of specifically "Real Wedding" features.
9. **Expert Advice:** A section pulling quotes/excerpts directly from Mr. Negi or lead planners.
10. **Destination Spotlight:** A recurring feature block highlighting a specific city/venue.
11. **Pagination/Load More:** Elegant "Load More" button.
12. **Author Bios:** Brief mention of the team members writing the content.
13. **Search:** A prominent, styled search bar.
14. **Tags Cloud:** A subtle, styled list of popular tags at the bottom.
15. **CTA:** "Plan Your Wedding with Us".
