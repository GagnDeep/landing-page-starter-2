# Blog Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Blog Hero/Header**: "The Heritage Journal" - elegant typography introducing the content.
2. **Featured Article Banner**: A large, prominent highlight of the most recent or important post.
3. **Category Filter**: Tags like "Recipes", "History", "Festivals", "Behind the Scenes".
4. **Latest Posts Grid**: A 3-column or 2-column grid of recent articles.
5. **Newsletter Sticky Banner**: An inline prompt to subscribe for updates.
6. **"The Science of Sweets" Highlight**: A specific featured section on how certain items are made.
7. **Festival Guide Series**: A recurring block highlighting posts related to upcoming festivals.
8. **Video Content Highlight**: If applicable, a section featuring embedded video stories.
9. **Popular/Trending Posts**: A sidebar or horizontal list of the most read articles.
10. **Author Profiles**: Brief bios of the family members or chefs writing the posts.
11. **Customer Stories Spotlight**: A section dedicated to user-submitted memories.
12. **Historical Archives**: A link to older, foundational posts about the bakery's history.
13. **Recipe Corner**: A specific visual treatment for posts that share simple home recipes.
14. **Pagination/Load More**: Clear navigation for older content.
15. **Search Bar**: Easy text search for specific ingredients or topics.
16. **Social Sharing Prompt**: Encouraging readers to share articles.
17. **Related Products (within posts)**: A blueprint for how products will be linked inside an article layout.
18. **Comments/Discussion Area (within posts)**: Layout for community interaction.
19. **Next/Previous Post Navigation (within posts)**: Easy traversal between articles.
20. **Back to Shop CTA**: Ensuring users can easily return to buying.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
