# Blog / Editorial Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero (`BlogHero`)
- **Intent:** "The Celebration Journal".
- **Rhythm:** Featured article takes up 70% of the viewport. Huge typography.

## 2. Latest Articles (`BlogLatest`)
- **Intent:** Feed of recent posts.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid. Large images, very clean serif titles.

## 3. Category Filter (`BlogCategories`)
- **Intent:** Navigation.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky horizontal typographic menu.

## 4. Featured Category (`BlogFeaturedCat`)
- **Intent:** Highlight specific content (e.g., Wedding Trends).
- **Rhythm:** Asymmetric layout. 1 large post, 2 small stacked next to it.

## 5. Newsletter Signup (`BlogNewsletter`)
- **Intent:** Capture readers.
- **Rhythm:** Full width, elegant inline form.
