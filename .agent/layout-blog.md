# Blog Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
The blog (or "Journal") should feel like an editorial publication, sharing culinary insights, real wedding stories, and cultural deep dives.

## Sections

1.  **Hero: The Culinary Journal**
    *   *Intent:* Introduce the editorial nature of the page.
    *   *Visuals:* A featured post takes up the entire hero section, with a massive background image and a dramatic overlay title.

2.  **Category Filter**
    *   *Intent:* Allow easy navigation.
    *   *Visuals:* A subtle horizontal sticky navigation bar (e.g., "All," "Real Weddings," "Culinary Notes," "Heritage").

3.  **Featured Articles Grid**
    *   *Intent:* Highlight key pieces.
    *   *Visuals:* A 2-column or 3-column asymmetric grid. Larger cards for more important stories. Use elegant serif typography for headlines.

4.  **Recent Posts List**
    *   *Intent:* Show ongoing activity.
    *   *Visuals:* A vertical list layout. Small thumbnail images paired with brief excerpts and read-more links.

5.  **Newsletter Sign-up**
    *   *Intent:* Capture leads interested in content.
    *   *Visuals:* A distinct, color-blocked section with a minimal input field and a refined submit button.

6.  **Instagram Integration**
    *   *Intent:* Connect the journal to live social updates.
    *   *Visuals:* A clean grid of recent Instagram posts.
