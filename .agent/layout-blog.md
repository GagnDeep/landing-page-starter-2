# Blog Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **Blog Hero**: A visually striking hero introducing "The Artisan's Journal".
3. **Featured Post Category Tags**: A row of elegant, pill-shaped tags for filtering (e.g., Weddings, Floral Tips, Real Stories).
4. **Featured Hero Post**: A large, 2-column layout highlighting the most recent or important post.
5. **Recent Posts Grid Title**: "Latest from the Journal".
6. **Post Card 1**: Image, date, title, and "Read More" link.
7. **Post Card 2**: Image, date, title, and "Read More" link.
8. **Post Card 3**: Image, date, title, and "Read More" link.
9. **Newsletter Signup Banner**: A mid-page break inviting users to subscribe for inspiration.
10. **Category Spotlight: Real Anand Karaj Stories**: A dedicated section for case studies.
11. **Spotlight Post 1**: Story card.
12. **Spotlight Post 2**: Story card.
13. **Category Spotlight: Floral Guides**: Tips on choosing seasonal flowers.
14. **Guide Post 1**: Tips card.
15. **Guide Post 2**: Tips card.
16. **Author Highlight**: A small block introducing the main writer/designer.
17. **Pagination**: Elegant next/previous buttons.
18. **Popular Tags**: A cloud of commonly used tags at the bottom.
19. **Call to Action**: "Ready to start your own story?"
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
