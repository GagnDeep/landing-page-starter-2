# Layout Blueprint: Blog Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Driving Tips & School News").
4.  `FeaturedPost`: Large hero area highlighting the most recent or most important article.
5.  `CategoryFilter`: Buttons to filter posts (e.g., Tips, News, Licensing, Safety).
6.  `SearchSection`: Search bar to find specific topics.
7.  `PostGrid`: The main grid of blog post cards (Image, Title, Excerpt, Date, Read More).
8.  `Pagination`: Controls to navigate older posts.
9.  `NewsletterSignup`: Inline form to subscribe to updates.
10. `PopularPostsSidebar`: (Or section) Listing the most read articles.
11. `TagsCloud`: Common tags used in the blog.
12. `AuthorBioSnippet`: Brief intro to the main authors (e.g., Kadar Khan's tips).
13. `VideoContentHighlight`: Highlighting any posts that feature video tutorials.
14. `LocalNewsBanner`: Updates specific to driving in Chandigarh.
15. `GuestPostPromo`: Invitation for guest writers or student stories.
16. `SocialSharePrompt`: Encouragement to share useful articles.
17. `PromoBanner_Inline`: Ad for a driving package inserted between post rows.
18. `RecentComments`: (Optional) Displaying community engagement.
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Call to action bridging the gap from reading to booking a class.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
