# Layout Intent: Journal / Blog Page

Referred to as the "Journal" or "Editorial", this section functions as a luxury lifestyle magazine, featuring event highlights, culinary trends, and destination guides.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`JournalHero`**
    *   **Intent:** Featuring the most recent or most spectacular article.
    *   **Rhythm:** A massive, full-bleed hero image with the article title overlaid in large, elegant typography, resembling a magazine cover.
3.  **`CategoryFilter`**
    *   **Intent:** Allowing users to sort by Real Weddings, Culinary Arts, Destinations, etc.
    *   **Rhythm:** A subtle, sticky, horizontal bar below the hero with delicate, spaced-out links.
4.  **`FeaturedArticlesGrid`**
    *   **Intent:** Highlighting 2-3 prominent articles.
    *   **Rhythm:** A sophisticated asymmetric grid (e.g., one large article taking up 2/3 width, two smaller ones stacked in the remaining 1/3).
5.  **`RecentArticlesList`**
    *   **Intent:** The main feed of journal entries.
    *   **Rhythm:** A clean, repeating list where each row features a high-quality thumbnail on one side and the article excerpt on the other. Heavy use of whitespace between entries.
6.  **`NewsletterSignup`**
    *   **Intent:** Capturing emails for the brand's editorial newsletter.
    *   **Rhythm:** A wide, subtly colored block with a simple, elegant input field and button.
7.  **`Pagination`**
    *   **Intent:** Navigation for older posts.
    *   **Rhythm:** Minimalist "Previous / Next" text links or delicate numbers.
8.  **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
