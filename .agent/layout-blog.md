# Blog & Journal Page Layout Map

**Goal:** Establish authority in beauty trends, share salon news, and provide aftercare tips.

## Section 1: Hero Header
- **Visuals:** Featured latest article with a large hero image.
- **Structure:** "The Boho Journal". Highlight top post.

## Section 2: Category Filters
- **Visuals:** Pill-shaped toggle buttons.
- **Structure:** All, Nail Trends, Hair Care, Bridal Diaries, Salon News.

## Section 3: Article Grid
- **Visuals:** 3-column masonry or standard grid.
- **Structure:** Cards with article thumbnail, date, title, and excerpt.

## Section 4: Newsletter Signup
- **Visuals:** Embedded block within the grid or at the bottom.
- **Structure:** Subscribe for the latest trends and tips.
