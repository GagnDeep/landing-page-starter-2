# Layout: Blog (Journal)
**URL**: `/blog`
**Intent**: Share event features, culinary trends, and company news to build authority and aid SEO.

## Structure
1. **Hero**: Title: "The Journal".
2. **Featured Post**: A large, prominent highlight of the most recent or important article.
3. **Category Filter**: Simple text-based filter (e.g., Weddings, Corporate, Recipes, News).
4. **Post Grid**: A clean, uniform grid of article cards (Image, Date, Title, Excerpt).
5. **Pagination/Load More**: Simple, elegant button.
6. **Newsletter Signup**: Inline banner to capture emails.
