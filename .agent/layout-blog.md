# Blog/Journal Layout Map

## 1. Hero (`hero`)
**Intent:** Introduce the journal as a source of inspiration and behind-the-scenes access.
**Rhythm:** Clean typography, perhaps a featured article taking up the top half.

## 2. Featured Post (`featured-post`)
**Intent:** Highlight the most recent or important event/article.
**Rhythm:** Large image with an overlaid or adjacent large title and excerpt.

## 3. Post Grid (`post-grid`)
**Intent:** Display the archive of past entries.
**Rhythm:** 3-column masonry or standard grid, magazine-style thumbnails.

## 4. Categories/Tags (`categories`)
**Intent:** Allow easy filtering of content.
**Rhythm:** Horizontal list of subtle pill-shaped buttons.

## 5. Newsletter Sign-up (`newsletter`)
**Intent:** Capture readers for future marketing.
**Rhythm:** Minimalist form centered at the bottom of the page.
