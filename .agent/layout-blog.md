# Layout Blueprint: Blog

**Purpose**: Content marketing, SEO, sharing cake care tips and event inspiration.
**Vibe**: Editorial, magazine-like, inspiring.

## Sections
1. **Global Header**
2. **Hero: The Sweet Talk**
   - Featured latest article, large image.
3. **Categories Filter**
   - Sticky sub-nav (Tips, Inspiration, News, Recipes).
4. **Latest Posts Grid**
   - 3-column masonry or standard grid.
5. **Trending Articles**
   - Sidebar or distinct horizontal section.
6. **Highlighted Series (e.g., "Midnight Rescues")**
   - Special design block.
7. **Newsletter Signup**
   - In-line with content.
8. **Author Bios**
   - Small section highlighting the bakers writing the posts.
9. **Instagram Integration**
10. **Pagination / Load More**
11. **CTA: Need a cake for your event?**
12. **Global Footer**
