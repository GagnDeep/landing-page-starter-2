# Layout Blueprint: Blog/Journal Page
**Intent:** Share expertise on wedding planning, food trends, and company news.

## Section Inventory
1. **Hero:** "The Royal Kitchen Journal".
2. **Featured Article:** Large highlight of the latest or most important post.
3. **Categories Filter:** Weddings, Corporate, Recipes, News.
4. **Post Grid:** Masonry or standard grid of articles with elegant cards.
5. **Newsletter Subscribe:** Inline prompt to subscribe.
6. **Pagination/Load More.**
