# Layout Intent: Blog (Journal) Page

**Goal:** Create an editorial reading experience that positions the brand as an authority on beauty, lifestyle, and self-care.

## 1. Hero: Journal Header
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial masthead.
- **Layout:** Large, stylized "Journal" or "Editorial" text, resembling the top of a high-end magazine.

## 2. Featured / Latest Article Showcase
- **Visual Rhythm:** High impact, hero image.
- **Layout:** A massive, full-width (or near full-width) image. Headline and brief excerpt overlaid or placed directly beneath it in large typography.

## 3. Category Navigation
- **Visual Rhythm:** Horizontal, subtle navigation.
- **Layout:** A sticky or prominent row of links (e.g., Haircare, Skincare, Lifestyle, Salon News) using small, tracked-out sans-serif caps.

## 4. Article Grid Row 1
- **Visual Rhythm:** Balanced magazine layout.
- **Layout:** 2-column grid. One column is slightly wider than the other to create dynamic asymmetry.

## 5. Article Grid Row 2
- **Visual Rhythm:** 3-column index.
- **Layout:** 3 equally sized cards with square or portrait images, category tags, and concise headlines.

## 6. Newsletter Interstitial
- **Visual Rhythm:** Solid block break.
- **Layout:** A full-width band interrupting the grid, inviting users to subscribe for beauty tips.

## 7. Featured Category: "Stylist Tips"
- **Visual Rhythm:** Dedicated section highlight.
- **Layout:** A distinct background color. A large heading on the left, and a horizontal scroll of articles on the right.

## 8. Article Grid Row 3
- **Visual Rhythm:** Checkerboard / Alternating.
- **Layout:** Row with image on left, text on right; next row image on right, text on left.

## 9. Visual Break: Pull Quote
- **Visual Rhythm:** Typographic focus.
- **Layout:** A large, evocative quote from a recent article, styled elegantly with serif typography and plenty of whitespace.

## 10. Article Grid Row 4
- **Visual Rhythm:** 3-column index.
- **Layout:** Matches Row 2 layout.

## 11. Most Popular / Trending Sidebar (Desktop) or Section (Mobile)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Dense, text-focused list.
- **Layout:** A numbered list (1-5) of top-read articles, focusing purely on strong headlines without large images.

## 12. Article Grid Row 5
- **Visual Rhythm:** Single large feature.
- **Layout:** One large article spanning the full width of the content container, re-anchoring the page rhythm.

## 13. Brand Story Tie-In
- **Visual Rhythm:** Subtle promo.
- **Layout:** A small, elegant block that looks like an article but actually links back to the "About" or "Services" page.

## 14. Article Grid Row 6
- **Visual Rhythm:** 2-column grid.
- **Layout:** Matches Row 1 layout.

## 15. Instagram Integration
- **Visual Rhythm:** Visual mosaic.
- **Layout:** A 4x1 or 3x2 grid of recent Instagram posts, blending the journal content with social media.

## 16. Pagination / Load More
- **Visual Rhythm:** Minimal UI.
- **Layout:** A simple, elegant "Load More Articles" button or numbered pagination.

## 17. Search the Journal
- **Visual Rhythm:** Functional, clean input.
- **Layout:** A large, single-line input field with a minimalist search icon.

## 18. Tag Cloud / Topics List
- **Visual Rhythm:** Typographic cluster.
- **Layout:** A collection of clickable tags/pills for specific topics (e.g., #balayage, #bridal, #skincareroutine).

## 19. Call to Action: Book an Appointment
- **Visual Rhythm:** Subtle but clear.
- **Layout:** Unlike the main pages, this CTA is softer, offering booking as a natural next step after reading.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
