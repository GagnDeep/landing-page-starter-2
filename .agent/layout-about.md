# About Page Layout Rhythm

The About page focuses on the story of CakeDelights, establishing a personal connection with the Patiala community.

## 1. Hero Section
- **Intent:** Introduce the bakery's mission and origins with a warm, inviting hero image (e.g., the bakery storefront or founders).

## 2. Our Story
- **Intent:** A narrative section detailing how CakeDelights started. Focus on the passion for baking and community.

## 3. The Team / Master Bakers
- **Intent:** Humanize the brand by introducing the key people behind the creations.

## 4. Community Focus (Patiala Roots)
- **Intent:** Emphasize local ties, sourcing, or community events they support.

## 5. CTA
- **Intent:** Lead users to view the gallery or contact them.
