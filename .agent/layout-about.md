# Layout Architecture: About Page

## Structural Intent
The About page is narrative-driven, focusing on the story, values, and the people behind the events. It should feel intimate and trustworthy.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Global Notice Bar:** (Consistent with global layout)
2. **Main Navigation (Header):** (Consistent with global layout)
3. **Hero Section (The Origin Story):** Atmospheric hero image with an inviting headline.
4. **The "Why" (Our Mission):** A deep dive into why Blinking Eye Events exists.
5. **Meet Kavita (Detailed Profile):** Comprehensive bio, background, and vision of the founder.
6. **Kavita's Personal Philosophy:** A pull-quote or specific manifesto section.
7. **The Team Intro:** "The artisans behind the scenes."
8. **Team Member 1 (Lead Designer):** Profile and role.
9. **Team Member 2 (Logistics Director):** Profile and role.
10. **Team Member 3 (Client Liaison):** Profile and role.
11. **Our Core Values Intro:** Section header.
12. **Value 1 (Elegance & Detail):** Explanation of the aesthetic standard.
13. **Value 2 (Unwavering Reliability):** Explanation of the operational standard.
14. **Value 3 (Warmth & Family):** Explanation of the client relationship standard.
15. **The Studio / Our Workspace:** Images and text describing the physical or creative space where magic happens.
16. **Milestones / History:** A brief timeline of the firm's growth.
17. **Community Involvement / Philanthropy:** How the firm gives back (if applicable) or supports local artisans.
18. **Testimonial (The Collaborative Experience):** A review focusing on what it's like to work *with* the team.
19. **Secondary CTA (Start Your Story):** Call to action to connect.
20. **Global Footer:** (Consistent with global layout)
