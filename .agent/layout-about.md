# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - About Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Same as homepage, solid or transparent over hero.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent top navigation.

2. **Hero Section (The Story Begins)**
   - *Layout intent:* A muted, atmospheric full-width image or video. A large, elegant title introducing the agency's history.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Less contrast than the home hero; softer, inviting, and intimate.

3. **Founder's Letter (The Vision)**
   - *Layout intent:* A long-form text block styled like a handwritten note or editorial letter. Accompanied by a high-end signature graphic.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Asymmetrical. High line-height, beautiful serif typography. Very generous padding.

4. **The Team Grid (The Artisans)**
   - *Layout intent:* A grid (e.g., 2 or 3 columns) showcasing key team members (Planners, Designers, Logistics).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Uniform portrait images, perhaps in black and white until hovered (which reveals color). Clean sans-serif names and titles.

5. **Our Core Values**
   - *Layout intent:* 3 to 4 text-heavy blocks, each with a subtle icon or drop-cap.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A horizontal band with dividers between values, or a staggered list.

6. **The Studio / Office Vibe**
   - *Layout intent:* A cinematic image gallery showing the behind-the-scenes environment where the planning happens.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A masonry layout with varying image sizes to feel organic and bustling but refined.

7. **Milestones / History Timeline**
   - *Layout intent:* A vertical timeline detailing the agency's founding and major achievements.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Subtle connecting lines, fading in as the user scrolls.

8. **Press Mentions Grid**
   - *Layout intent:* A dedicated grid of article snippets or covers from luxury publications.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Cards with soft shadows (`shadow-md` on hover) and clean borders.

9. **Philanthropy / Sustainability (Our Commitment)**
   - *Layout intent:* A dedicated section explaining ethical practices or charitable partnerships.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A split layout (50/50 image and text) with a muted background color (`--muted`).

10. **Call to Action (Join the Family)**
    - *Layout intent:* A clean, compelling block asking the user to start their journey.
    - *Visual rhythm:* High contrast, centered typography.
