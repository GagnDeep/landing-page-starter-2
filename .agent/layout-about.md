# Layout Blueprint: About Page

## Structural Rhythm
A storytelling flow, taking the user through the heritage, the founder, and the philosophy of the atelier.

## Sections

1. **Hero/Intro:** Gentle, muted hero section with a portrait of Heena or the boutique.
2. **The Founder's Story:** A split-layout section (text left, image right) detailing the origins of Designlane Heena.
3. **Our Philosophy:** A large, centered typographic quote emphasizing craftsmanship.
4. **The Chandigarh Atelier:** A multi-image collage showing the boutique's interior and ambiance.
5. **Meet the Artisans:** A horizontal scrolling section or grid featuring the master tailors and embroiderers.
6. **Heritage & Technique:** Deep dive into specific traditional embroidery techniques used.
7. **Commitment to Quality:** Icon or subtle illustration-led section on material sourcing.
8. **Final CTA:** "Experience the craftsmanship in person."
