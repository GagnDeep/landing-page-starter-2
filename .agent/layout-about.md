# Layout: About Page

1. **Hero Header:** Editorial title ("Our Heritage") with a subtle background texture.
2. **The Purba Story:** Text-heavy section with drop caps and a large, high-quality image of the founder or historical shop photo.
3. **Our Philosophy:** 3-column text blurbs on Quality, Tradition, and Modern Fit.
4. **The Artisans:** Profiles or a group shot of the master tailors.
5. **Timeline/Milestones:** A vertical timeline of the shop's history in Patiala.
6. **Visit Us:** Map and detailed shop interior photos.
7. **CTA:** "Book a Fitting" (WhatsApp link).
