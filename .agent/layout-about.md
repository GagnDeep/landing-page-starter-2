# Layout Intent: About Page

**Goal:** Tell the story of Your Home Salon. Establish credibility, intimacy, and the luxurious standard of the brand.

## 1. Hero: The Origin Story
- **Visual Rhythm:** Full-width atmospheric image with a moody, artistic overlay.
- **Layout:** Text placed in the bottom third of the viewport, leading the user down into the story.

## 2. Mission Statement
- **Visual Rhythm:** Typographic pause.
- **Layout:** A massive, centered serif statement spanning almost the full width, emphasizing the core "why."

## 3. The Founder's Vision
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial portrait and deep text.
- **Layout:** Split layout. A full-height column for the portrait on the left; a scrolling text column on the right.

## 4. Brand Values Overview
- **Visual Rhythm:** Structured, balanced grid.
- **Layout:** 2x2 grid. Each quadrant contains a small elegant icon, a value heading, and a brief description.

## 5. Visual Break: The Salon Atmosphere
- **Visual Rhythm:** Cinematic interruption.
- **Layout:** A full-width parallax image showing the details of the physical or mobile salon setup (e.g., close-up of premium tools).

## 6. Our Standard of Luxury (Detail 1)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Offset imagery and text.
- **Layout:** Image aligned to the right, overlapping slightly into the background color of the next section. Text left-aligned.

## 7. Hygiene & Safety Promise (Detail 2)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Clean, stark, reassuring.
- **Layout:** Text heavy, centered alignment. Perhaps accompanied by very subtle, clinical line-art icons.

## 8. The Team Introduction
- **Visual Rhythm:** Gallery/Index.
- **Layout:** A headline introducing the team, followed by a grid layout of team members.

## 9. Stylist Profile 1
- **Visual Rhythm:** Portrait card.
- **Layout:** Black and white or desaturated portrait image, with name and specialty in delicate sans-serif below.

## 10. Stylist Profile 2
- **Visual Rhythm:** Portrait card (consistent with Profile 1).
- **Layout:** Matches previous profile layout.

## 11. Stylist Profile 3
- **Visual Rhythm:** Portrait card (consistent with Profile 1).
- **Layout:** Matches previous profile layout.

## 12. Join the Team Teaser
- **Visual Rhythm:** Distinct callout block.
- **Layout:** A solid color block breaking the gallery, with a bold heading inviting applications and a button linking to Careers.

## 13. Brand Timeline / History
- **Visual Rhythm:** Vertical progression.
- **Layout:** A delicate vertical line running down the center or left side, with text nodes and small imagery branching off.

## 14. Press & Mentions
- **Visual Rhythm:** Logo wall and pull-quotes.
- **Layout:** Horizontal band of publication logos, with a rotating text block featuring key quotes from the press.

## 15. The Products We Trust
- **Visual Rhythm:** Still-life photography focus.
- **Layout:** Large, stylized still-life image of premium products on one side, a list of brand partners on the other.

## 16. Sustainability / Ethics
- **Visual Rhythm:** Earthy, grounded.
- **Layout:** Centered text block with a muted, warm background color. Emphasis on natural elements in any accompanying imagery.

## 17. Client Stories (Highlight)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Intimate, text-forward.
- **Layout:** A long-form, multi-paragraph quote from a long-time client, styled elegantly like a magazine excerpt.

## 18. Gallery: Behind the Scenes
- **Visual Rhythm:** Asymmetric masonry grid.
- **Layout:** A collection of 4-6 images of varying sizes closely packed together, showing stylists in action.

## 19. Next Step: View Services
- **Visual Rhythm:** Transitionary Call to Action.
- **Layout:** Large, high-contrast block leading the user to explore what the salon actually offers (Features/Services page).

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
