# Layout Blueprint: About Page (`/about`)

*Intent: Establish personal connection, deep expertise, and the rich history of the Guide Events brand.*

## 1. Editorial Hero
- **Intent**: A striking opening statement introducing the founder/team.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Split layout: half large portrait image, half elegant typography.

## 2. Our Origin Story
- **Intent**: The narrative journey of how the company began.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A narrow, deeply readable column of text surrounded by massive whitespace.

## 3. The Philosophy
- **Intent**: Core beliefs about event design.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Three elegant text blocks arranged asymmetrically.

## 4. The Core Team
- **Intent**: Introduce key players.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Black-and-white or heavily stylized portraits in a sparse grid.

## 5. Global Reach (Map/Locations)
- **Intent**: Highlighting past event locations globally.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A subtle, stylized map or list of exclusive locations with hover states.

## 6. Signature Approach
- **Intent**: Detail the unique method of planning.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Numbered steps with large serif numerals and brief, tight paragraphs.

## 7. Media & Press Features
- **Intent**: Social proof and industry recognition.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A refined, muted logo grid of publications.

## 8. Final Call to Action
- **Intent**: Encourage booking a consultation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Centered, high contrast block similar to the home page CTA.
