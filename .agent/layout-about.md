# About Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Tell the story of YAVA DESIGNS, emphasizing its Chandigarh roots, premium craft, and the vision of its founders.

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (The Origin):** A striking, full-width image of the studio or a mood-setting editorial shot. Large, elegant title.
3. **The YAVA Story (Narrative):** A 2-column layout. Left: A sticky heading "Our Story". Right: A beautifully typeset, multi-paragraph narrative with drop caps or distinct styling for the first letter.
4. **The Chandigarh Connection:** A visually distinct section (perhaps a soft secondary background) detailing how the city's architecture and culture influence the designs.
5. **Meet the Founders/Artisans:** Individual portraits in an asymmetrical grid. Delicate text blocks describing their expertise.
6. **Our Philosophy:** A large, centered manifesto statement with generous top and bottom padding.
7. **The Atelier (Gallery):** A curated masonry grid of high-quality images showing the workspace, fabrics, and tools.
8. **Call to Action (Consultation):** A gentle push to book a custom fitting or consultation.
9. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
