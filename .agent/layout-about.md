# Layout Blueprint: About Page

## Intent
To tell the story of Villa Hair Sense, the founder's journey, and the passion behind the brand.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** Atmospheric image with a title like "Our Story."
3. **The Genesis:** How and why the salon was founded in Chandigarh.
4. **Founder's Vision:** A letter or statement from the lead stylist.
5. **Meet the Team:** Grid of stylists with brief bios and specialties.
6. **Our Core Values:** 3-4 pillars of the salon's philosophy (e.g., Artistry, Care, Innovation).
7. **The Space:** Detailed look at the interior design and ambiance of the salon.
8. **Our Journey (Timeline):** Key milestones in the salon's history.
9. **Commitment to Quality:** Details on the premium products and techniques used.
10. **Sustainability/Ethics:** Any eco-friendly or ethical practices.
11. **Community Involvement:** Local Chandigarh events or charities supported.
12. **Awards & Recognition:** Badges or text detailing industry accolades.
13. **Behind the Scenes:** Candid imagery of the team at work.
14. **Client Philosophy:** How every client is treated (the bespoke consultation process).
15. **Education & Training:** How the team stays updated with global trends.
16. **Media Mentions:** Features in publications.
17. **Gallery:** A masonry grid of life at the salon.
18. **Join the Team Teaser:** Link to careers page for aspiring stylists.
19. **Booking CTA:** Encourage users to experience the salon.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
