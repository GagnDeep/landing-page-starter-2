# Layout: About Us
**URL**: `/about`
**Intent**: Tell the story of Sangam Caterer, emphasizing their roots in Patiala, their journey, and their commitment to excellence.

## Structure
1. **Hero**: Elegant, subtle background. Title: "Our Story".
2. **The Heritage**: Text-heavy section (well-spaced) detailing the origins in Patiala.
3. **The Founders**: Portraits and brief bios of the leadership team.
4. **Our Philosophy**: A 3-column feature section (e.g., Ingredients, Authenticity, Service).
5. **Timeline**: A visually engaging vertical timeline of key company milestones.
6. **Behind the Scenes**: A gallery showing the kitchen, prep work, and staff in action.
7. **CTA**: "Let us be part of your story."
