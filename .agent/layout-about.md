# Layout Blueprint: About Page (`/about`)

## Goal
The About page establishes the history, credibility, and human element of Armaan Driving School, reinforcing trust through transparency and legacy.

## Section Architecture (15-20 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Hero Section:** Focus on legacy and mission. "Building Confident Drivers Since [Year]." Background: Heritage or team photo.
3.  **The Origin Story:** Narrative text block detailing how and why the school was founded in Sector 47.
4.  **Mission & Vision:** 2-column layout. Clear statements on safety, education, and community impact.
5.  **Government Registration Detail:** A dedicated block explaining what being "Govt. Registered" means for the student (standards, safety, legitimacy).
6.  **Our Philosophy:** Detailed breakdown of the "Calm, Methodical, Safe" approach.
7.  **Instructor Roster - Lead Instructor:** Large profile, photo, bio, philosophy, and credentials.
8.  **Instructor Roster - Team Grid:** Smaller cards for other instructors, highlighting their patience and specific expertise.
9.  **The Training Vehicles:** Details on the dual-control mechanisms, maintenance standards, and why the specific car models were chosen.
10. **Facility/Classroom (If applicable):** Details on theory classes or the physical office location.
11. **Community Impact:** Number of students passed, accident-free records, or local initiatives.
12. **Timeline/Milestones:** Visual timeline of the school's growth and achievements over the years.
13. **Behind the Scenes:** Image gallery showing cars being cleaned, instructors preparing, etc.
14. **Testimonial Focus:** Deep-dive testimonial, perhaps a video or long-form quote from a student who overcame severe anxiety.
15. **Join the Team CTA:** Small banner linking to the Careers page.
16. **Final CTA:** "Ready to meet your instructor? Book your first lesson."
17. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
