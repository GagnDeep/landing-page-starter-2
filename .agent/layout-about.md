# A2Zee Events - About Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To establish trust and convey the deep expertise and heritage of A2Zee Events. The layout should feel like a biography of a master craftsman or an established luxury institution.

## Structural Sections
1.  **`about-hero`:** Large atmospheric image, elegant serif title ("The Art of Celebration").
2.  **`our-story-narrative`:** A beautifully typeset narrative section detailing the company's origins and philosophy.
3.  **`the-team-grid`:** High-quality portraits of key team members. Focus on their specific expertise (design, logistics, culinary, etc.).
4.  **`core-values-list`:** A minimalist list or grid of principles (e.g., "Relentless Precision," "Discreet Luxury").
5.  **`global-footprint-map`:** A stylized map or list highlighting key destination locations where events have been successfully executed.
6.  **`press-mentions`:** Logos and short quotes from high-end publications.
7.  **`about-cta`:** A subtle call to connect with the team.
