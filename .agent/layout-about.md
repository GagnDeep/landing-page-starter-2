# About Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **About Hero Section**: A warm, welcoming header focused on the family name and legacy.
2. **Our Story - The Beginning**: Text-heavy historical account of the bakery's founding.
3. **Founder Profile**: A dedicated spotlight on the original founder(s).
4. **Generations Timeline**: A vertical or horizontal timeline showing the evolution over decades.
5. **The Philosophy**: A statement of values (quality, tradition, community).
6. **Our Kitchen/Workspace**: Behind-the-scenes look at the scale and cleanliness of the operation.
7. **The Team - Core Family**: Profiles of the current family members running the business.
8. **The Team - Head Chefs/Halwais**: Highlighting the master craftsmen.
9. **Sourcing Ingredients**: A deep dive into where the milk, flour, and nuts come from.
10. **The Pure Ghee Promise**: A specific section emphasizing this crucial quality marker.
11. **Community Impact**: How the bakery supports local events or charities.
12. **Awards & Milestones**: A grid of significant achievements.
13. **Evolution of Recipes**: How traditional recipes have been preserved while introducing new items.
14. **Customer Generations**: Stories of families who have bought from Verma's for generations.
15. **Sustainability/Packaging**: A note on modernizing while respecting the environment (if applicable).
16. **Photo Gallery - Historic**: Vintage photos of the shop.
17. **Photo Gallery - Modern**: High-quality shots of the bustling current shop.
18. **Press & Media Mentions**: Logos or quotes from publications.
19. **Join Our Family (Careers Teaser)**: A brief CTA linking to the careers page.
20. **Contact CTA**: A prompt to get in touch or visit.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
