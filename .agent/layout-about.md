# Layout Blueprint: About Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Simple, elegant title area ("About Azad Driving School").
4.  `StorySection`: The history of the school, its founding in Burail, Chandigarh, and its mission.
5.  `FounderMessage`: A personal note or quote from the founder, setting a tone of trust.
6.  `KadarKhanProfile`: Deep dive into Kadar Khan's expertise, years of experience, and teaching style.
7.  `GovtRegistrationBanner`: Prominent display of government registration and certification details.
8.  `CoreValuesGrid`: 4-6 key values (e.g., Patience, Safety, Integrity, Modern Methods).
9.  `FacilityShowcase`: Images/descriptions of the classroom or training areas.
10. `FleetDetails`: Specific details about the cars used, focusing on safety features and dual controls.
11. `TeachingMethodology`: Explanation of the pedagogical approach (theory + practical).
12. `RealTrafficFocus`: Reiteration of the commitment to real-world driving conditions, not just empty lots.
13. `CommunityImpact`: How the school serves the local Chandigarh/Burail community.
14. `MilestonesTimeline`: A visual timeline of the school's growth and key achievements.
15. `TeamGallery`: Photos and brief bios of other instructors or staff members (if applicable).
16. `StudentSuccessStats`: Numbers showing passing rates, total students taught, etc.
17. `AwardsRecognition`: Any local awards or recognitions received.
18. `GalleryGrid`: A mix of photos showing students learning, the cars, and the instructors.
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Call to action encouraging users to start their journey.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
