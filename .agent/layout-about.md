# Layout Intent: About Page

1. **Hero (The Origins):** Atmospheric image, large serif headline introducing the Crown Caterers legacy.
2. **Our Story (The Journey):** Timeline or narrative block. Long-form text with accompanying historical or establishment photos.
3. **Mission & Vision:** Minimalist text blocks on contrasting backgrounds (sage or sand).
4. **Leadership Team:** Editorial grid of founders/directors with brief bios.
5. **The Patiala Heritage:** Deep dive into the cultural roots and how it influences the service.
6. **Awards & Recognition:** Logo strip or subtle list of accolades.
7. **CTA:** Lead into the Contact or Features page.
