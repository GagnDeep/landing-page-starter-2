# About Page Layout Map

**Goal:** Tell the story of Boho Salon, highlighting the founder's vision, the commitment to high-end aesthetics, and the unique artistic vibe of the Sector 17 location.

## Section 1: Hero Header
- **Visuals:** Soft, blurred image of the salon interior.
- **Structure:** Large heading "Our Story" or "The Boho Vision".

## Section 2: The Origin Story
- **Visuals:** Split layout. Side image of the founder or early days.
- **Structure:** Narrative text explaining the gap in the market for a truly premium, artistic salon experience in Chandigarh.

## Section 3: The Space (Sector 17)
- **Visuals:** Large, high-res photo gallery (masonry).
- **Structure:** Describing the architectural and interior design choices that create the "Boho-Modern" sanctuary.

## Section 4: Our Philosophy & Values
- **Visuals:** Grid of 3-4 cards with subtle icons.
- **Structure:** Highlighting values: Craftsmanship, Inclusivity (NRI friendly), Premium Products, Artistic Freedom.

## Section 5: The Team
- **Visuals:** Grid of team member portraits.
- **Structure:** Names, roles, and a short bio for key master stylists.

## Section 6: Join Us / Careers CTA
- **Visuals:** Simple, elegant banner.
- **Structure:** "Looking to create art? We are hiring." Link to Careers page.
