# About Us Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Tell the rich story of Oasis Caterers. Focus on heritage, craftsmanship, and the meticulous attention to detail that defines the brand.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation.
2. **Hero Section (The Origin)**: Large evocative image of culinary preparation. "Our Heritage."
3. **The Genesis Story**: Long-form text block with a drop cap. Wide margins.
4. **Historical Timeline**: Vertical timeline highlighting key milestones since inception.
5. **The Founder's Letter**: Serif typography quote block with founder's signature.
6. **Core Values Grid**: 2x2 grid detailing principles (e.g., Integrity, Elegance).
7. **Behind the Scenes Video**: Framed video player showcasing kitchen operations.
8. **The Ingredients Philosophy**: Asymmetric layout. Text on left, ingredient macro shot on right.
9. **Meet the Chefs**: Carousel or grid of key culinary figures with brief bios.
10. **The Event Staff**: Highlighting the immaculate service team.
11. **Sustainability Commitment**: Muted card section detailing ethical sourcing.
12. **Community Impact**: Text block on local Patiala community involvement.
13. **Client Roster**: Notable past clients or event types (anonymized if necessary).
14. **Media Mentions / Press**: Grid of quotes from publications or high-profile reviews.
15. **The Oasis Standard**: A numbered list of guarantees that define the service.
16. **Gallery (The Craft)**: Grid of behind-the-scenes preparation images.
17. **Testimonial (Deep Dive)**: A single, long-form story from a satisfied client.
18. **Careers Teaser**: "Join the craft." Link to careers page.
19. **Primary Call to Action**: "Plan your next event with us."
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
