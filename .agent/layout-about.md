# Layout Intent: About Page

## Core Intent
Tell the story of Stitching Solutions. Establish trust, emphasize the master craftsmanship, and highlight the owner-led service that defines the boutique.

## Key Sections
1. **Hero Story:** High-quality image of the boutique exterior or the founders, with a compelling headline about legacy and precision.
2. **Our Mission & Vision:** A beautifully typeset statement focusing on alteration-free tailoring and reliable service in Patiala.
3. **The Founder's Journey:** A detailed narrative about the owner, their background in fashion/tailoring, and their commitment to the craft.
4. **The Team:** A gallery of the master tailors and staff, humanizing the business and showing the experts behind the scenes.
5. **Our Values:** An elegant grid or list highlighting principles like "Precision," "Punctuality," and "Personalized Care."
6. **Timeline / History:** A visual timeline of the boutique's growth and milestones.
7. **The Patiala Connection:** A section dedicated to the local community, emphasizing how the boutique serves and understands the regional style and needs.
8. **Call to Action:** An invitation to visit the boutique or book a consultation.
