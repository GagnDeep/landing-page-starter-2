# Layout Blueprint: About Page

**Goal:** Establish trust, tell the story of Jeet Singh and Capital Driving School, and reinforce the core values of patience, safety, and expertise.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* High-contrast thin strip for global announcements.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Clean, sticky header with navigation and primary CTA.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* Emotional connection. "Our Story" heading over a warm, high-quality image of the driving school or instructors.
4. **Mission Statement**
   - *Intent:* Clear, large typography stating the mission: building confidence and safe driving habits.
5. **The Founder's Story (Jeet Singh)**
   - *Intent:* Personalize the business. A timeline or narrative text block beside a high-quality portrait.
6. **Core Values Grid**
   - *Intent:* Highlight the 3-4 pillars (Patience, Safety, Flexibility, Excellence) using simple icons and short text.
7. **The Team / Instructors**
   - *Intent:* Show the faces behind the instruction. Grid of instructor cards with brief bios.
8. **Our Fleet**
   - *Intent:* Showcase the modern, dual-controlled vehicles used for training.
9. **History / Timeline**
   - *Intent:* Show longevity and experience. A vertical timeline of milestones (e.g., Year founded, 1000th student passed).
10. **Testimonial Highlight**
    - *Intent:* A deeply emotional or impactful review from a nervous learner who succeeded.
11. **Behind the Scenes / Methodology**
    - *Intent:* Explain *how* they teach differently (calm approach, tailored pacing).
12. **Community Involvement / Awards**
    - *Intent:* Build local authority. Mention local recognition or community service in Sector 40B, Chandigarh.
13. **Stats / Impact Counter**
    - *Intent:* Large numbers (Years in business, Students taught, 5-star reviews).
14. **Gallery (The Experience)**
    - *Intent:* Visual proof. A masonry grid of happy students holding their licenses, cars, and the office.
15. **FAQ (About the School)**
    - *Intent:* Answer common questions regarding the school's background or credentials.
16. **Partnerships / Accreditations**
    - *Intent:* Show official recognition or partnerships with local authorities.
17. **Call to Action (Join Us)**
    - *Intent:* Transition from reading about them to booking a lesson.
18. **Newsletter Subscribe**
    - *Intent:* Capture email leads.
19. **Service Area Map**
    - *Intent:* Reiterate the coverage area.
20. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer with links, contact info, and legal.
