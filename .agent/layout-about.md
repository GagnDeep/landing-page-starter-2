# Layout Blueprint: About Page

## Intent
To tell the rich, multi-generational story of Harbhajan's Catering, building emotional connection and trust through transparency and heritage.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** Warm, atmospheric header with the title "Our Family, Your Feast" and a background image of the bustling kitchen.
3. **The Heritage Story (Part 1):** Text-heavy section (well-spaced) detailing the founding of the business.
4. **Historical Photo/Timeline:** A visual representation of the company's growth over the years.
5. **The Heritage Story (Part 2):** Continuing the narrative to present day.
6. **Our Core Values Title:** Section introduction.
7. **Value 1: Authentic Taste:** Description and icon.
8. **Value 2: Generous Hospitality:** Description and icon.
9. **Value 3: Uncompromising Quality:** Description and icon.
10. **Value 4: Family First:** Description and icon.
11. **Meet the Team Title:** Section introduction.
12. **Founder/Head Chef Profile:** Image, bio, and quote.
13. **Operations Manager Profile:** Image, bio.
14. **Customer Relations Profile:** Image, bio.
15. **The Kitchen / Behind the Scenes:** A photo essay or grid showing the cleanliness, scale, and energy of the preparation area.
16. **Sourcing Philosophy:** A section detailing where ingredients (spices, dairy) come from, emphasizing quality.
17. **Community Impact:** Highlighting local involvement, charity, or sustainable practices.
18. **Awards & Recognition:** A band showcasing local business awards or press features.
19. **Milestones Counter:** Animated number counters (e.g., Events Catered, Happy Guests, Years of Service).
20. **Final CTA:** "Let our family serve yours" pointing to the Contact page.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
A narrative-driven layout that interweaves text with rich, historical, and behind-the-scenes photography, creating an intimate, documentary-style feel.
