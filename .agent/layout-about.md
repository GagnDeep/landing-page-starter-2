# About Page Layout Structure

1. **Page Header**: Elegant hero image of the boutique exterior or founders, with a large serif title.
2. **Our Story**: Detailed narrative of Kohinoor Trends' founding in 1998 in Chandigarh.
3. **The Founders**: Portraits and brief bios of the visionaries behind the brand.
4. **Our Philosophy**: Core values focusing on quality, tradition, and customer care.
5. **Generations Served**: A visual tribute to families who have shopped here for decades.
6. **Footer**: Standard expansive footer.
