# Layout Intent: About Page (`/about`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Full-width hero image with an elegant overlay. Title: "The Architects of Celebration". Subtitle about crafting legacies since inception.
3. **Founder's Welcome:** Split layout. A sophisticated portrait of Mr. Negi alongside a personal letter/statement about the philosophy of Guide Events.
4. **Our Story / Timeline:** A vertical, elegant timeline detailing the journey from a local setup to a destination wedding powerhouse.
5. **Core Values Grid:** 3-column grid highlighting values like Precision, Discretion, and Cultural Authenticity.
6. **The NRI Specialization:** Dedicated section explaining the deep understanding of cross-border planning, time-zone management, and remote coordination.
7. **Team Portraits:** High-editorial style grid of key team members with subtle hover reveals for roles/bios.
8. **Behind the Magic (Video/Gallery):** A curated masonry gallery or a video thumbnail showing the team in action during a setup.
9. **Catering Heritage:** A nod to Bawa Caterers Enterprises. Split section showing the evolution of their culinary excellence.
10. **Our Promise:** A bold, typography-driven statement section emphasizing the 4.9★ standard.
11. **Awards & Recognition:** A minimalist list or grid of industry accolades.
12. **Press / Media Features:** A section highlighting mentions in luxury lifestyle or wedding magazines.
13. **Community & Sustainability:** A brief section on ethical sourcing for decor and food.
14. **Offices / Presence:** Map or elegant list showing the Sector 21C Chandigarh headquarters and any other presence.
15. **CTA:** "Begin Your Journey" – prompting a consultation.
