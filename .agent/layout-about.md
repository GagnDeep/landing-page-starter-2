# Layout Documentation: About Page

## Intent
To tell the story of Imran Tailor, focusing on the history, the philosophy of craftsmanship, and the transition across generations. The tone should be narrative, respectful, and slightly intimate.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Subtle, historic image (perhaps black and white) with a soft overlay. Title: "Our Heritage."
2. **The Founder's Story:** Long-form text block with elegant typography. A drop cap to start the narrative.
3. **The Philosophy:** A 3-column layout highlighting core beliefs (e.g., "Measure Twice, Cut Once," "The Fabric is the Foundation").
4. **The Next Generation:** Transition to modern day. Image of the current master tailor.
5. **Timeline:** A vertical, elegantly styled timeline showing key milestones over the 20+ years.
6. **CTA:** "Experience the Legacy" leading to the booking page.
