# Layout Intent: About Page

The About page tells the story of Crown Caterers, focusing on heritage, mastery, and the people behind the flawless execution. It should feel like a biography of an artisan.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`AboutHero`**
    *   **Intent:** A majestic, static image (e.g., an expansive, beautifully set dining table) with a large, centered headline defining the brand's legacy.
3.  **`OurStory`**
    *   **Intent:** A long-form narrative section.
    *   **Rhythm:** Narrow reading column (max-w-prose) for comfortable reading, broken up by elegant pull quotes in the primary serif font.
4.  **`HeritageTimeline`**
    *   **Intent:** Tracing the history of the company.
    *   **Rhythm:** A vertical, elegantly styled timeline with dates and brief milestones.
5.  **`TheFounders`**
    *   **Intent:** Profiles of the key leadership.
    *   **Rhythm:** A 2-column layout with high-quality, editorial-style portraits and accompanying biographies.
6.  **`OurValues`**
    *   **Intent:** Detailing principles like Sustainability, Authenticity, and Precision.
    *   **Rhythm:** A 3-column grid of elegant cards, perhaps using subtle iconography or abstract imagery.
7.  **`TeamGallery`**
    *   **Intent:** Highlighting the broader team (chefs, event managers, staff).
    *   **Rhythm:** A fluid, interlocking masonry grid showing the team in action, emphasizing behind-the-scenes precision.
8.  **`PhilosophyStatement`**
    *   **Intent:** A concluding thought on the art of hospitality.
    *   **Rhythm:** Centered, large typography over a solid, muted background color.
9.  **`FinalCallToAction`** (Shared component)
10. **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
