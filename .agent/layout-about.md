# Layout Blueprint: About Page

**Purpose**: Tell the story of A-One Cakes, build trust, and highlight local roots in Patiala.
**Vibe**: Authentic, editorial, community-focused.

## Sections
1. **Global Header**
2. **Hero: Our Story**
   - Large centered typography. Soft background.
3. **The Beginning**
   - Timeline or narrative text block with a vintage or nostalgic image.
4. **Our Philosophy**
   - 3 pillars: Quality, Reliability (24/7), Community.
5. **The Bakers Behind the Magic**
   - Grid of team members with subtle hover effects.
6. **Local Roots (Patiala)**
   - Mention of the Shiv Mandir location and community impact.
7. **The 6-Pound Mastery**
   - Deep dive into why the 6-pound custom cake is the specialty.
8. **Quality Ingredients**
   - Macro photography placeholders, descriptive text.
9. **Sustainability/Sourcing (if applicable)**
   - Clean list or icon grid.
10. **Press & Mentions**
    - Logos of local news or prominent event features.
11. **Photo Gallery: Behind the Scenes**
    - Asymmetric collage of the kitchen.
12. **Milestones Counter**
    - "Cakes baked", "Midnight rescues", "Happy customers" (fluid numbers).
13. **Customer Stories**
    - Highlight one major event (e.g., a massive wedding).
14. **Visit Us**
    - Storefront image and opening hours (24/7!).
15. **Careers Teaser**
    - "Join our team."
16. **Global Footer**
