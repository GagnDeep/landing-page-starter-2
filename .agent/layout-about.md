# About Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero (`AboutHero`)
- **Intent:** Establish the brand's origins and passion.
- **Rhythm:** Large, centered typography over a warm, muted background color. Subtle, slow-moving imagery.

## 2. Our Story (`AboutStory`)
- **Intent:** Narrative flow detailing the journey.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating text and image blocks down the page. High typographic contrast.

## 3. The Founders (`AboutFounders`)
- **Intent:** Personal connection.
- **Rhythm:** 2-column layout. Elegant portrait on one side, biographical text on the other.

## 4. Our Philosophy (`AboutPhilosophy`)
- **Intent:** Core values (quality, urgency, joy).
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid of text blocks with prominent serif numerals (01, 02, 03).

## 5. Behind the Scenes (`AboutBehindScenes`)
- **Intent:** Show the craftsmanship.
- **Rhythm:** Masonry image gallery. Bleeding edges.

## 6. Footer CTA (`AboutCTA`)
- **Intent:** Drive to shop.
- **Rhythm:** Massive, bold typography block.
