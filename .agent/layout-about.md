# About Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **About Hero Section**: A soft, split-screen hero; large elegant serif on the left, high-res portrait of the founders/team on the right.
3. **Our Story Introduction**: A centered, wide text block explaining the origins in Patiala.
4. **Milestone Timeline**: A vertical, elegant timeline of the company's growth.
5. **Timeline Event 1**: Founding year and story.
6. **Timeline Event 2**: First major Anand Karaj setup.
7. **Timeline Event 3**: Expansion to full venue decor.
8. **The Core Philosophy**: A full-width section with a muted background outlining the belief in tradition mixed with modern aesthetics.
9. **Team Grid Title**: "Meet the Artisans" in the heading font.
10. **Team Member 1**: Image, name, and role with subtle hover scaling.
11. **Team Member 2**: Image, name, and role.
12. **Team Member 3**: Image, name, and role.
13. **Behind the Scenes Gallery**: A 3-column masonry layout showing the team at work.
14. **Sustainability/Sourcing**: Details on where the flowers come from, set in a two-column layout.
15. **Local Craftsmanship Focus**: Highlighting local Patiala artisans they work with.
16. **Awards & Recognition**: A clean, icon-based row of accolades.
17. **Client Trust Badge Row**: Logos or names of notable venues they frequently work with.
18. **Philosophy Quote**: A large, edge-to-edge section with a profound quote about weddings.
19. **Call to Action**: "Let's craft your story" block.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
