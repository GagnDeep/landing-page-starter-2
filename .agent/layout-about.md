# About Page Layout Blueprint

## 1. Hero
- **Visual:** Subdued, elegant header.
- **Layout:** Centered typography over a soft background or a single establishing shot of the kitchen.

## 2. Our Story
- **Layout:** Long-form editorial text block, narrow column width for readability. Interspersed with archival or historical imagery.

## 3. The Founders
- **Layout:** 2-column alternating grid (image left/text right, then reverse).

## 4. Our Philosophy
- **Layout:** A prominent, large quote block.

## 5. The Team
- **Layout:** Grid of team members (chefs, event managers) with subtle hover effects revealing their specialties.

## 6. Community & Roots
- **Layout:** A text and image block detailing ties to Patiala.

## 7. CTA
- **Layout:** "Start Planning" banner leading to Contact.
