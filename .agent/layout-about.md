# Layout Blueprint: About Page
**Intent:** Tell the story of Bawa Caterers Enterprises. Establish heritage, scale, and uncompromising quality.

## Section Inventory
1. **Hero:** Atmospheric background, title "Our Heritage, Your Feast".
2. **The Origin Story:** Timeline or narrative of how the business started.
3. **Mission & Vision:** Core values (Hospitality, Authenticity, Grandeur).
4. **The Leadership/Family:** Profiles of the founders.
5. **Behind the Scenes:** Kitchen hygiene, prep scale, and logistics capabilities.
6. **Community Impact:** Local hiring, sustainability efforts.
7. **Awards & Recognition:** Badges or mentions in local press.
8. **Call to Action:** "Meet us for a tasting."
