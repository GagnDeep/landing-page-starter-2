# Layout: About Page

The About page focuses on the story, the team, and the philosophy behind Studio Sanjay Arts, building deep trust and emotional connection.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** A warm, candid behind-the-scenes shot. Title: "Our Story" or "The Visionaries".
2.  **The Studio's Origin:** A narrative section. 2-column layout (Text left, Image right). Focus on the roots in Patiala and growth to global clientele.
3.  **The Philosophy:** A text-heavy, beautifully typeset section. Large serif pull quote. Focus on "Candid Storytelling & Family-Centered Experience".
4.  **Meet the Founder (Sanjay):** Detailed bio. High-quality portrait.
5.  **The Team:** A grid of team members (secondary photographers, cinematographers, editors). Consistent, approachable headshots.
6.  **Our Signature Style:** Visual explanation of what makes the studio unique (e.g., color grading, unobtrusive shooting).
7.  **Awards & Recognition:** A trust-building section highlighting industry awards or publications.
8.  **Call to Action:** "Ready to meet us?" leading to the Contact page.
