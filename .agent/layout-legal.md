# A2Zee Events - Terms & Privacy Pages Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
Clean, highly legible, and unstyled layout strictly for legal compliance.

## Structural Sections
1.  **`legal-header`:** Simple page title and "Last Updated" date.
2.  **`legal-content-body`:** Long-form text utilizing a prose/typography plugin for perfect reading width, appropriate header hierarchy, and legible lists.
