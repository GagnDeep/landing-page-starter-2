# Layout Intent: Booking Page

**Goal:** Create a frictionless, highly reassuring, and elegant conversion funnel. The booking process should feel like speaking with a high-end concierge, not filling out a sterile database form.

## 1. Hero: The Concierge Intro
- **Visual Rhythm:** Minimalist, focused header.
- **Layout:** Centered serif heading ("Reserve Your Time"). A very brief subtitle assuring them the process is simple and secure.

## 2. Step 1: Modality Selection (Location)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Large, visual toggle/cards.
- **Layout:** Two distinct, beautifully styled large cards side-by-side: "At My Home" vs. "In-Salon". Subtle hover effects indicate selectability.

## 3. Step 2: Service Category Selection
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elegant grid.
- **Layout:** A grid of categories (Hair, Nails, Skincare, Packages). High-quality icon or subtle background image for each category.

## 4. Step 3: Specific Service Selection
- **Visual Rhythm:** Interactive menu list.
- **Layout:** A clean vertical list expanding from the chosen category. Service name, brief description, duration, and starting price. Checkboxes or radio buttons to select.

## 5. Selection Summary (Sticky Sidebar / Bottom Bar)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Persistent, reassuring UI.
- **Layout:** On desktop, a right-hand sticky column showing "Your Reservation" building in real-time. On mobile, a sticky bottom bar showing total price/time and a "Next" button.

## 6. Step 4: Add-Ons & Enhancements
- **Visual Rhythm:** Subtle upsell grid.
- **Layout:** Smaller cards presenting optional add-ons (e.g., Deep Conditioning, Hand Massage) with a simple '+' button.

## 7. Step 5: Professional Selection (Optional)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Portrait carousel.
- **Layout:** Horizontal scrolling list of stylist portraits and names, plus an option for "First Available" or "Salon's Choice."

## 8. Step 6: Date & Time Picker
- **Visual Rhythm:** Clean, bespoke calendar UI.
- **Layout:** A custom-styled calendar (avoiding default browser datepickers). Soft colors for available days, elegant typography for the numbers. Time slots displayed as neat, clickable pills.

## 9. At-Home Specifics: Address Input
- **Visual Rhythm:** Form field group.
- **Layout:** Only appears if "At My Home" is selected. Clean inputs for Street, City, ZIP, and a specific box for "Parking Instructions/Entry Details".

## 10. Step 7: Client Details (The "Who")
- **Visual Rhythm:** Elegant form.
- **Layout:** Standard fields (Name, Email, Phone) styled with bottom borders only, or very subtle background colors.

## 11. Guest / Group Booking Option
- **Visual Rhythm:** Expandable section.
- **Layout:** A toggle to add additional people to the reservation, dynamically updating the time/price summary.

## 12. Step 8: Pre-Consultation Notes
- **Visual Rhythm:** Text area.
- **Layout:** An open text box inviting the client to describe their hair/skin history or current goals. "Tell us about your hair..."

## 13. Policy Acknowledgment
- **Visual Rhythm:** Micro-copy and checkbox.
- **Layout:** A very clean, small checkbox requiring acknowledgment of the cancellation policy. Link to the full policy opens in a modal, not a new page.

## 14. Step 9: Payment Security Assurances
- **Visual Rhythm:** Trust indicators.
- **Layout:** Subtle lock icons and text reassuring that payment details are encrypted. Monochromatic card logos.

## 15. Step 10: Secure Payment / Deposit Input
- **Visual Rhythm:** Embedded, seamless form.
- **Layout:** Stripe/payment integration styled to match the site perfectly. No harsh iframe borders.

## 16. Final Review Block
- **Visual Rhythm:** Visual confirmation.
- **Layout:** A final, clean summary of all details before the final button click.

## 17. The "Complete Reservation" CTA
- **Visual Rhythm:** High impact, definitive.
- **Layout:** A large, solid-color button. Should have a distinct active/loading state (e.g., a subtle spinner) to prevent double-clicks.

## 18. Success State / Confirmation View
- **Visual Rhythm:** Celebratory but elegant.
- **Layout:** Replaces the form upon success. A large, beautiful checkmark or animation. A clear message stating "Your Reservation is Confirmed," followed by the details and an "Add to Calendar" button.

## 19. "Need Help?" Escape Hatch
- **Visual Rhythm:** Secondary link.
- **Layout:** A text link placed subtly below the main booking flow, offering a phone number for those who get stuck.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Minimal.
- **Layout:** A stripped-down version of the global footer to keep focus on the booking funnel.
