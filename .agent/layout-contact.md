# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page

**Goal:** Make it as easy as possible for potential students to reach out, book a slot, or find the physical location.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* Standard global announcement.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* "Get in Touch" or "Start Your Journey Today".
4. **Contact Options Grid**
   - *Intent:* 3-4 distinct blocks for Phone, WhatsApp, Email, and Address. Large, clickable icons.
5. **Direct Contact Form**
   - *Intent:* Clean form (Name, Phone, Preferred Time, Message) for inquiries.
6. **Physical Location Map**
   - *Intent:* Large, interactive Google Map centered on the Sector 40B, Chandigarh office.
7. **Operating Hours**
   - *Intent:* Clear table showing office hours vs. driving lesson hours (highlighting 24x7 availability).
8. **Service Area Details**
   - *Intent:* Text listing the specific sectors/areas covered for doorstep pickup.
9. **"What to Expect After Contacting Us"**
   - *Intent:* Short timeline (e.g., 1. We call you back, 2. Choose a package, 3. Start learning).
10. **FAQ (Contact Specific)**
    - *Intent:* E.g., "How fast do you reply?", "Can I visit the office without an appointment?".
11. **Social Media Links**
    - *Intent:* Follow us on Instagram, Facebook, etc.
12. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
