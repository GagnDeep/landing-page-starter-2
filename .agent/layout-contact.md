# Contact Page Layout Map

**Goal:** Provide easy ways for clients to reach the salon, find the location, or submit a specific inquiry.

## Section 1: Hero Header
- **Visuals:** Split layout.
- **Structure:** "Get in Touch" on left, high-res salon exterior image on right.

## Section 2: Contact Information Grid
- **Visuals:** 3 or 4 subtle cards.
- **Structure:** Phone Number, Email Address, WhatsApp Link, Instagram Handle.

## Section 3: Visit Us (Map & Address)
- **Visuals:** Embedded Google Map or stylized static map.
- **Structure:** Full address in Sector 17, Chandigarh, and clear working hours.

## Section 4: Contact Form
- **Visuals:** Minimalist, clean form fields.
- **Structure:** Name, Email, Subject (General, Bridal, PR), Message, and Submit button.
