# Contact Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
The Contact page should feel like an exclusive invitation. It must be highly functional but deeply elegant, assuring the user that their inquiry will be handled with utmost care.

## Sections

1.  **Hero: Begin the Conversation**
    *   *Intent:* A warm, commanding opening.
    *   *Visuals:* A split screen. On one side, a beautiful, quiet image (perhaps an empty, perfectly set table). On the other, the hero text.

2.  **The Primary Inquiry Form**
    *   *Intent:* Capture necessary details for a bespoke quote.
    *   *Visuals:* A long, elegant form. Fields should have minimal borders, perhaps only bottom borders (like a formal document). Include fields for Event Date, Guest Count, Venue, and a large text area for their vision.

3.  **Direct Contact Information**
    *   *Intent:* Provide immediate alternatives to the form.
    *   *Visuals:* A structured, typographic block listing the physical office address, a dedicated phone number, and an email address.

4.  **Office Hours & Response Time Expectation**
    *   *Intent:* Manage expectations elegantly.
    *   *Visuals:* Small, neatly aligned text blocks. "We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours."

5.  **Location Map (Optional)**
    *   *Intent:* Show physical presence.
    *   *Visuals:* A highly stylized, monochromatic map that matches the brand colors, not a standard Google Map embed.
