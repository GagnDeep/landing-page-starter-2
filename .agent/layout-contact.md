# Contact Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **Contact Hero**: A warm, welcoming hero section with a subtle floral background.
3. **Introduction Text**: "We'd love to hear about your special day."
4. **Contact Layout Split**: Two-column layout; form on left, details on right.
5. **Contact Form - Name**: Input field.
6. **Contact Form - Email/Phone**: Input fields.
7. **Contact Form - Event Date & Location**: Input fields.
8. **Contact Form - Message**: Textarea.
9. **Contact Form - Submit Button**: Elegant primary button.
10. **Contact Details - Address**: The physical studio location in Patiala.
11. **Contact Details - Phone**: Direct line.
12. **Contact Details - Email**: General inquiry email.
13. **Contact Details - Hours**: Studio operating hours.
14. **Map Integration**: A beautifully styled, desaturated Google Map showing the studio location.
15. **Social Media Links**: Large, elegant icons linking to Instagram/Pinterest.
16. **What to Expect Next**: A short timeline explaining they will reply within 24 hours.
17. **Studio Visit Callout**: Information on booking an in-person consultation.
18. **FAQ Teaser**: "Looking for quick answers?" link to FAQ.
19. **Decorative Image Break**: A large, beautiful floral arrangement image.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
