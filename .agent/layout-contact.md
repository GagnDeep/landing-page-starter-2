# Contact Us Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Provide a frictionless, elegant pathway for high-net-worth clients to initiate contact. The feeling should be of reaching out to a bespoke concierge, not filling out a generic web form.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation.
2. **Hero Section (Connect)**: "Begin the conversation." Split layout with a subtle background.
3. **Primary Contact Form**: Clean, multi-step or well-spaced form (Name, Event Date, Guest Count, Venue, Vision).
4. **Direct Contact Information**: Phone, Email, and WhatsApp Business links.
5. **Office / Headquarters Location**: Address details for the Patiala base.
6. **Map Integration**: Stylized, desaturated map highlighting the service region.
7. **Operating Hours**: Clear indication of consultation availability.
8. **The Consultation Process**: Brief text explaining what happens after they submit the form.
9. **FAQ Link**: "Looking for quick answers? Visit our FAQ."
10. **Dedicated Corporate Contact**: Separate contact details for B2B inquiries.
11. **Dedicated Press/Media Contact**: Separate contact details for PR.
12. **Partner / Vendor Inquiry Form**: A secondary, smaller form or link for industry collaborations.
13. **Social Media Links**: Elegant icon set linking to active platforms.
14. **Testimonial Snippet**: A small quote reassuring the quality of service right at the point of contact.
15. **Newsletter Signup**: Optional opt-in checkbox within the main form.
16. **Privacy Assurance**: A brief line below the form ensuring data confidentiality.
17. **Response Time Guarantee**: "Our concierge will respond within 24 hours."
18. **Image Feature**: A beautiful, calming image next to the contact details to balance the layout.
19. **Secondary Call to Action**: (e.g., Download Brochure if they aren't ready to talk).
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
