# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page (`/contact`)

## Goal
To provide clear, multiple avenues for communication, prioritizing high-conversion channels like WhatsApp and Phone, while detailing physical location.

## Section Architecture (15-20 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Hero Section:** "Get in Touch." Welcoming, helpful tone.
3.  **Primary Contact Grid (2-3 Columns):**
4.  **Grid Item: Phone/WhatsApp:** Prominent numbers, emphasizing quick replies.
5.  **Grid Item: Email:** Standard email link.
6.  **Grid Item: Physical Address:** Full address in Sector 47.
7.  **Interactive Map:** Large, functional Google Maps embed showing the Sector 47 office.
8.  **Operating Hours Block:** Clear listing of days and times the office/instructors are available.
9.  **Contact/Booking Form (Left/Main Column):** Name, Email, Phone, Service Interested In (Dropdown), Message.
10. **"What to Expect" (Right Column next to form):** Short text explaining response times (e.g., "We aim to reply within 2 hours during business times").
11. **FAQ Snippet:** 2-3 most common questions to intercept unnecessary queries (e.g., "Do you do pick-up?").
12. **Social Media Links:** Icons linking to active profiles.
13. **Accessibility/Directions Note:** Specific instructions on how to find the office (landmarks).
14. **Emergency Contact (Optional):** For current students (e.g., last-minute cancellations).
15. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
