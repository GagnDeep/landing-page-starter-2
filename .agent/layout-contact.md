# Layout Intent: Contact / Inquire Page

This page is the gateway to acquiring a client. The form must feel like an exclusive application rather than a generic contact form.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`ContactHero`**
    *   **Intent:** A welcoming yet formal invitation to connect. "Begin Your Journey".
    *   **Rhythm:** Split layout. Left side: The introductory text and direct contact details. Right side: A stunning, evocative image.
3.  **`DirectContactInfo`**
    *   **Intent:** Providing immediate phone numbers, email addresses, and physical office locations (e.g., London, Dubai, Mumbai).
    *   **Rhythm:** A clean, multi-column typographic list located below the hero text on the left side.
4.  **`TheInquiryForm`**
    *   **Intent:** A multi-step or highly stylized form to gather detailed event requirements.
    *   **Rhythm:** Form fields should be minimalist—perhaps just bottom borders instead of full boxes. The typography for labels should be small and elegant. Spacing between fields must be generous to avoid feeling cramped.
5.  **`OfficeLocationsMap`**
    *   **Intent:** Showing the global presence.
    *   **Rhythm:** A highly stylized, custom-colored map (matching the brand palette) with subtle markers for office locations.
6.  **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
