# Layout Intent: Contact Page (`/contact`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Title: "Begin the Conversation".
3. **Split Layout:**
   - **Left:** Contact details (Address, Phone, Email, Hours).
   - **Right:** An elegant, multi-step inquiry form.
4. **Form - Step 1:** Basic Info (Names, Contact).
5. **Form - Step 2:** Event Details (Dates, Estimated Guest Count, Location).
6. **Form - Step 3:** Vision (Budget range, aesthetic preferences).
7. **Global Clock:** Small, elegant world clocks showing current time in Chandigarh, London, NY, Dubai (appealing to NRIs).
8. **Office Location:** High-quality image of the Sector 21C office exterior/interior, emphasizing it's a real, premium space.
9. **Map Embedded:** A styled, custom-color Google Map.
10. **Response Time Promise:** Text guaranteeing a response within 24 hours.
11. **Direct Department Emails:** Specific emails for press, vendor inquiries, or careers.
12. **Social Media Links:** Elegant icon list linking to Instagram/Pinterest.
13. **Consultation FAQ:** Brief 3-question accordion on what to expect in the first call.
14. **Alternative Contact:** WhatsApp business link.
15. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
