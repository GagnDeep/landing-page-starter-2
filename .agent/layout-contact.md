# Layout Intent: Contact Page

**Goal:** Make reaching out feel like an exclusive, white-glove concierge service rather than a standard web form.

## 1. Hero: Get in Touch
- **Visual Rhythm:** Split, intimate intro.
- **Layout:** Large typography on the left. A beautiful, evocative image (e.g., hands arranging flowers or styling tools) on the right.

## 2. Main Contact Form
- **Visual Rhythm:** Spacious, elegant form.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned or centered, utilizing the established high-end input styles. Fields for Name, Email, Phone, Service Interest, and Message.

## 3. Direct Contact Details
- **Visual Rhythm:** Typographic list.
- **Layout:** Placed alongside or directly below the form. Includes Email address and Phone number, styled beautifully.

## 4. Operating Hours
- **Visual Rhythm:** Clean, tabular data.
- **Layout:** A neat list of days and times. Distinction between "In-Salon" hours and "At-Home" availability.

## 5. Location / Service Radius Concept
- **Visual Rhythm:** Visual map/graphic.
- **Layout:** A highly stylized, monochromatic map of Chandigarh showing the primary service area.

## 6. Salon Address (If applicable)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Formal block.
- **Layout:** Full address details with a subtle link for directions.

## 7. Immediate Assistance Callout
- **Visual Rhythm:** Highlight box.
- **Layout:** A smaller, contrasting box emphasizing a phone number for urgent changes or day-of inquiries.

## 8. Social Media Links
- **Visual Rhythm:** Icon row.
- **Layout:** Elegant, minimal icons linking to Instagram, WhatsApp, etc.

## 9. Press & Media Inquiries
- **Visual Rhythm:** Secondary link block.
- **Layout:** Smaller text directing PR inquiries to a specific email address.

## 10. Careers / Hiring Link
- **Visual Rhythm:** Secondary link block.
- **Layout:** Subtle pointer to the careers page for interested stylists.

## 11. Visual Break: Brand Image
- **Visual Rhythm:** Full-width image.
- **Layout:** A cinematic shot to break up the dense information.

## 12. Response Time Expectation
- **Visual Rhythm:** Reassuring text.
- **Layout:** A centered, italicized paragraph explaining when clients can expect a reply (e.g., "Within 24 hours").

## 13. FAQ Teaser
- **Visual Rhythm:** Quick links.
- **Layout:** 2-3 of the most common questions linked directly, just in case the user can find their answer without emailing.

## 14. "Book Now" Alternative
- **Visual Rhythm:** High contrast CTA.
- **Layout:** A reminder that if they just want to book, they can skip the form and go straight to the booking engine.

## 15. Newsletter Signup
- **Visual Rhythm:** Restrained form.
- **Layout:** Same as the homepage newsletter block.

## 16. Privacy Assurance
- **Visual Rhythm:** Micro-copy.
- **Layout:** Tiny, muted text under the contact form assuring data privacy.

## 17. Brand Values Reminder
- **Visual Rhythm:** Typographic sign-off.
- **Layout:** A short, elegant phrase or motto before the footer.

## 18. Aesthetic Divider
- **Visual Rhythm:** Line art.
- **Layout:** A thin, sophisticated horizontal rule to separate content from the footer.

## 19. Back to Top Button
- **Visual Rhythm:** Functional UI.
- **Layout:** A subtle sticky or bottom-aligned button to scroll back to the form.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
