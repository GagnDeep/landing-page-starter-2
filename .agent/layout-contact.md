# Contact Page Layout Blueprint

## 1. Hero
- **Layout:** Minimal header.

## 2. The Form
- **Layout:** 2-column layout. Left side: The inquiry form (Name, Event Date, Guest Count, Message). Right side: Direct contact info (Phone, Email, Office Address).

## 3. Map/Location
- **Layout:** A full-width or large embedded map of the Patiala office/kitchen.

## 4. Office Hours
- **Layout:** A small, clean table or list of operational hours.
