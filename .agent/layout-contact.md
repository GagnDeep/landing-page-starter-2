# Layout: Contact Page

1. **Hero Header:** "Get in Touch"
2. **Primary Contact Method:** Very prominent WhatsApp button and number.
3. **Secondary Contact Methods:** Email address and direct phone line.
4. **Location Details:** Full address, hours of operation, and an embedded Google Map.
5. **Contact Form:** Simple form (Name, Email, Phone, Message) as an alternative to WhatsApp.
6. **Social Links:** Links to Instagram, Facebook, etc.
