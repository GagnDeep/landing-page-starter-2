# A2Zee Events - Contact Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To make the inquiry process feel exclusive and highly personalized. It should feel like requesting a private audience rather than submitting a generic form.

## Structural Sections
1.  **`contact-hero`:** "Begin the Conversation." Minimal imagery, high focus on typography.
2.  **`the-inquiry-form`:** A highly curated, multi-step or elegantly styled form asking for necessary details (Date, Location, Guest Count, Vision). Use clean inputs (`border-b` only, or very subtle boxes).
3.  **`direct-contact-info`:** Studio locations, direct phone numbers, and general email.
4.  **`what-to-expect`:** A brief timeline of how quickly the team responds and what the initial consultation entails.
