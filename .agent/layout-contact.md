# Layout: Contact
**URL**: `/contact`
**Intent**: Make it effortless for potential clients to reach out for inquiries or proposals.

## Structure
1. **Hero**: Title: "Get in Touch".
2. **Split Layout**:
   - **Left**: Contact Details (Phone, Email, Address in Patiala), Social Links.
   - **Right**: Inquiry Form (Name, Email, Event Date, Guest Count, Event Type, Message).
3. **Map/Location**: A stylized map showing their service area or headquarters.
4. **Response Time Expectation**: A small note indicating when they can expect a reply.
