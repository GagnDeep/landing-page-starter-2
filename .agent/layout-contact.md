# Layout: Contact Page

The Contact page must be inviting and highly functional, encouraging detailed inquiries.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** "Let's Tell Your Story". A welcoming image or split layout.
2.  **Contact Form (Primary):** A comprehensive form. Includes fields for Names, Date, Venue, Services Interested In, Budget Range, and "Tell us about your wedding".
3.  **Direct Contact Info:** Studio address (if public), phone number, general email address.
4.  **Office Hours / Response Time:** Managing expectations (e.g., "We aim to respond within 48 hours").
5.  **Social Links:** Links to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube.
