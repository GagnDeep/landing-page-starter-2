# Layout Intent: Contact Page

## Core Intent
Make it incredibly easy for clients to reach the boutique, find the physical location in Patiala, and book appointments.

## Key Sections
1. **Hero Header:** "Let's Create Something Beautiful."
2. **Contact Information Grid:** Clear presentation of Phone Number, Email, and Physical Address.
3. **Interactive Map:** An embedded map showing the precise location in Patiala, perhaps styled to match the brand colors.
4. **Opening Hours:** Clearly stated, including any holiday variations or appointment-only days.
5. **Inquiry / Booking Form:** An elegant, easy-to-use form with fields for name, contact details, service interested in, and preferred date.
6. **What to Expect:** A brief note on response times (e.g., "We aim to reply to all inquiries within 24 hours").
7. **Social Links:** Direct links to Instagram, Facebook, or Pinterest for further engagement.
