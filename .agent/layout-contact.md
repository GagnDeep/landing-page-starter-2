# Contact Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Contact Hero**: A welcoming header ("We'd love to hear from you").
2. **Primary Contact Information**: Clear, large display of phone numbers and general email.
3. **Store Location & Map**: An embedded, styled map showing the exact location.
4. **Operating Hours**: A clear table of open days, hours, and holiday exceptions.
5. **General Inquiry Form**: A clean form for standard questions (Name, Email, Message).
6. **Department Selector**: A dropdown in the form to route to Support, Catering, or Wholesale.
7. **Wedding/Catering Specific Form**: A distinct, more detailed form for large events (Date, Guest Count, Venue).
8. **Corporate Gifting Form**: Specific fields for company name and volume requirements.
9. **WhatsApp Direct Link**: A highly visible button to open a WhatsApp chat.
10. **Social Media Links**: Icons leading to Instagram, Facebook, etc.
11. **Store Front Image**: A high-quality image of the shop to help with physical navigation.
12. **Parking & Accessibility Info**: Practical details for visitors driving to the store.
13. **Response Time Expectation**: A small note setting expectations (e.g., "We reply within 24 hours").
14. **Careers/Hiring Link**: Rerouting job seekers away from the general form.
15. **Press/Media Contact**: A specific email for PR inquiries.
16. **Feedback/Complaints Route**: A dedicated, empathetic channel for resolving issues.
17. **FAQ Teaser**: A quick link to the FAQ page to deflect common questions.
18. **Newsletter Signup**: An alternate way to stay in touch without sending a direct message.
19. **Manager/Owner Direct Line**: (If applicable) An emergency contact for large unfulfilled orders.
20. **Visual Divider**: An elegant ornamental graphic separating the content from the footer.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
