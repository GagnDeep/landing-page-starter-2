# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page

## Intent
To provide clear, accessible ways for potential clients to reach out, request quotes, or schedule tastings, with a focus on high-touch customer service.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** "Let's Plan Your Feast" with a welcoming image of the team or an event setup.
3. **Contact Information Grid:** A 3-column layout.
4. **Phone Box:** Primary and secondary numbers.
5. **Email Box:** General inquiries and booking emails.
6. **Location Box:** Physical address of the kitchen/office.
7. **Business Hours:** A clean list of operating hours for office and kitchen.
8. **Primary Contact Form (Left Column):** The main inquiry form.
9. **Form Field: Name:** Input.
10. **Form Field: Email & Phone:** Inputs.
11. **Form Field: Event Date & Guest Count:** Specialized inputs.
12. **Form Field: Event Type:** Dropdown (Wedding, Corporate, etc.).
13. **Form Field: Message/Special Requests:** Textarea.
14. **Form Submit Button:** Prominent CTA.
15. **Office Location Map (Right Column):** Embedded Google Map or styled static map image beside the form.
16. **WhatsApp Quick Connect:** A floating or distinct button to start a WhatsApp chat immediately.
17. **Tasting Session CTA:** A specific section encouraging visitors to book a tasting.
18. **Social Media Links:** Beautifully styled icons linking to Instagram, Facebook, etc.
19. **Response Time Promise:** A small note guaranteeing a reply within 24 hours.
20. **FAQ Teaser:** Link to the full FAQ page for immediate answers.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
A highly functional, split-layout design. The top section is for quick scanning of direct contact methods, while the lower section features a well-structured, user-friendly form balanced by visual map elements to build local trust.
