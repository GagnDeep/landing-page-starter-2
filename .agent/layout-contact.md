# Layout Architecture: Contact Page

## Structural Intent
The contact page must feel like an invitation rather than a form. It should be elegant, easy to use, and highly reassuring.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Global Notice Bar:** (Consistent with global layout)
2. **Main Navigation (Header):** (Consistent with global layout)
3. **Hero Section (Let's Begin):** Warm, inviting headline with soft background imagery.
4. **The Invitation Text:** A short paragraph expressing excitement to hear their story.
5. **Contact Information Grid (Intro):**
6. **Info Block 1 (Phone):** Formatted elegantly.
7. **Info Block 2 (Email):** Direct email link.
8. **Info Block 3 (Studio Address):** If applicable, or general location (Chandigarh).
9. **Info Block 4 (Office Hours):** Setting expectations for response times.
10. **The Inquiry Form (Header):** "Tell us about your vision."
11. **Form Field 1 (Name):** Standard text input.
12. **Form Field 2 (Email):** Standard email input.
13. **Form Field 3 (Phone - Optional):** Standard phone input.
14. **Form Field 4 (Event Type):** Dropdown (Wedding, Corporate, Private).
15. **Form Field 5 (Estimated Date):** Date picker.
16. **Form Field 6 (Venue / Location):** Text input.
17. **Form Field 7 (The Vision / Message):** Text area.
18. **Form Submit Button:** Elegant, wide button with hover state.
19. **What Happens Next? (Expectation Setting):** Brief text explaining the response timeline (e.g., "We will review your details and respond within 48 hours to schedule a consultation.").
20. **Global Footer:** (Consistent with global layout)
