# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page

## Intent
Provide clear, accessible ways for clients to reach out, find the salon, and book appointments.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** Elegant imagery with "Get in Touch" or "Visit Us".
3. **Primary Contact Info Grid:** Phone, Email, WhatsApp quick links.
4. **Location Details:** Full address for Sector 9, Chandigarh.
5. **Interactive Map:** Embed of Google Maps showing the salon location.
6. **Operating Hours:** Clear list of days and times open/closed.
7. **Directions & Parking Info:** Specific instructions for finding the salon and parking.
8. **Contact Form:** Clean, elegant form (Name, Email, Phone, Service Interest, Message).
9. **Form Success Message (Hidden state):** Design for post-submission.
10. **Booking Widget Integration:** Direct embed of the booking system if applicable.
11. **Social Media Links:** Prominent icons for Instagram, Facebook, etc.
12. **Response Time Expectation:** Note on how quickly inquiries are answered.
13. **Press Inquiries Contact:** Specific email for media.
14. **Careers Inquiries Contact:** Specific email for job applications.
15. **Accessibility Info:** Details on wheelchair access, etc.
16. **Public Transit Info:** Nearest stops or stations.
17. **Virtual Tour Teaser:** Link to a video or 3D tour of the space.
18. **FAQ Teaser:** Quick link to the FAQ page to deflect common questions.
19. **Secondary CTA:** "Not ready to book? Join our newsletter."
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
