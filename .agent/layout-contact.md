# Contact Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Provide an elegant, friction-free way for clients to reach out, emphasizing a high-touch, concierge-like service.

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (Connect with Us):** A serene, welcoming image alongside the page title.
3. **Contact Options Grid:** A 2-column or 3-column layout highlighting different ways to connect (Email, WhatsApp/Phone, Studio Visit).
4. **The Inquiry Form:** A beautifully designed form. Floating labels or refined borders, ample padding between fields. Focus on the focus states (e.g., gold ring).
5. **Studio Location Details:** For the Chandigarh studio. Address, hours of operation, and perhaps a stylish, desaturated map or an exterior photo of the boutique.
6. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
