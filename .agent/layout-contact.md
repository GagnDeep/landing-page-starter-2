# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page

## Structural Rhythm
A blend of practical information and welcoming aesthetics.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:** "Get in Touch"
2. **Contact Form:** Elegant, spacious input fields for inquiries.
3. **Location Details:** Address in Chandigarh, embedded map, and boutique hours.
4. **Direct Contact:** Phone numbers and email addresses.
5. **Social Links:** Links to Instagram, Pinterest, etc.
6. **FAQ Link:** "Looking for quick answers? Check our FAQ."
