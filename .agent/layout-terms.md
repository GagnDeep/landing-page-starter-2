# Layout Blueprint: Terms and Conditions Page (`/terms`)

## Goal
To provide a clean, readable legal document outlining the terms of service, cancellation policies, and liability waivers.

## Section Architecture (15 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Page Header:** "Terms and Conditions." Last updated date clearly visible.
3.  **Table of Contents (Optional but helpful):** Jump links to specific legal sections.
4.  **Section 1: Acceptance of Terms:** Standard legal boilerplate.
5.  **Section 2: Booking and Payments:** Rules around deposits, full payments, and accepted methods.
6.  **Section 3: Cancellation and Refund Policy:** Crucial section detailing notice periods required to avoid fees.
7.  **Section 4: Lesson Protocols:** Rules for students (e.g., punctuality, being sober, proper footwear).
8.  **Section 5: Instructor Rights:** The right to refuse service if safety is compromised.
9.  **Section 6: Liability and Insurance:** Explanation of the school's insurance coverage and student liabilities.
10. **Section 7: RTO Test Disclaimers:** Guaranteeing instruction, but not guaranteeing passing the government test.
11. **Section 8: Vehicle Damage:** Policies regarding accidents during training.
12. **Section 9: Privacy Overview:** Short link out to the full Privacy Policy.
13. **Section 10: Changes to Terms:** How updates are communicated.
14. **Contact Info for Legal Queries:** Specific email address.
15. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
