# Layout Blueprint: Terms and Conditions

## Intent
To provide legal protection and clearly state the terms of service, payment policies, and operational agreements in a clean, legible format.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** Minimalist header "Terms and Conditions".
3. **Last Updated Date:** Text indicating the revision date.
4. **Introduction:** General agreement statement.
5. **Section 1: Booking & Confirmations:** Detailed text.
6. **Section 2: Payment Terms:** Advance requirements, final payments.
7. **Section 3: Cancellations & Refunds:** Policies for client cancellation.
8. **Section 4: Guest Count Adjustments:** Rules for changing the number of plates.
9. **Section 5: Menu Substitutions:** Policy on seasonal ingredient changes.
10. **Section 6: Venue Requirements:** What the catering team needs on-site (water, power).
11. **Section 7: Damages & Liability:** Protection clauses for equipment.
12. **Section 8: Force Majeure:** Unforeseen circumstances clause.
13. **Section 9: Leftover Food Policy:** Health and safety rules regarding unconsumed food.
14. **Section 10: Photography & Media:** Rights to use event photos for marketing.
15. **Section 11: Dispute Resolution:** Jurisdiction and arbitration details.
16. **Section 12: Severability:** Standard legal clause.
17. **Section 13: Entire Agreement:** Standard legal clause.
18. **Section 14: Contact for Legal Queries:** Email address for the legal department.
19. **Print Button:** Utility to print the document.
20. **Back to Home Link:** Navigation utility.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
A classic "prose" layout. Narrower content column for optimal reading length, using the `@tailwindcss/typography` plugin (prose) for consistent heading sizes, list styling, and paragraph spacing. Highly legible sans-serif focus.
