# Layout Documentation: Terms of Service

## Intent
Standard legal text, but formatted to remain readable and consistent with the brand's typographic standards.

## Sections
1. **Header:** "Terms of Service" with last updated date.
2. **Content Block:** Single column, narrow max-width for optimal reading length. Use `prose` (Tailwind Typography plugin) configured to use the brand's fonts and colors. Deep charcoal text, ample line height.
