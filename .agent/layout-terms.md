# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - Terms of Service Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Solid header.

2. **Hero Section (Terms & Conditions)**
   - *Layout intent:* Very minimal, text-only hero.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Clinical but elegant.

3. **Last Updated Date**
   - *Layout intent:* A small text block at the top of the document.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Muted text.

4. **Table of Contents (Optional but good)**
   - *Layout intent:* A sticky sidebar or a list at the top linking to sections.
   - *Visual rhythm:* highly functional, clean links.

5. **Section 1: Agreement to Terms**
   - *Layout intent:* Standard long-form text block.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Good typography (line height and width constrained for readability).

6. **Section 2: Intellectual Property**
   - *Layout intent:* Standard long-form text block.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Section 1.

7. **Section 3: User Representations**
   - *Layout intent:* Standard long-form text block.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Section 1.

8. **Section 4: Limitation of Liability**
   - *Layout intent:* Standard long-form text block.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Section 1.

9. **Contact Information for Legal**
   - *Layout intent:* A final block detailing where to send legal notices.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Distinct from the main text.

10. **Global Footer**
    - *Layout intent:* Consistent footer.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Standard layout.
