# Terms of Service & Privacy Policy Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
These utility pages must be perfectly legible, strictly functional, and legally sound, while still existing within the brand's typographic system.

## Sections (Applies to both pages)

1.  **Header**
    *   *Intent:* Clear identification of the document.
    *   *Visuals:* Stark, simple title. Last updated date clearly visible.

2.  **Table of Contents (Optional but recommended)**
    *   *Intent:* Ease of navigation.
    *   *Visuals:* A sticky sidebar on desktop, inline list on mobile.

3.  **The Content Body**
    *   *Intent:* Readability.
    *   *Visuals:* A single, narrow column of text (optimal measure for reading). Use the sans-serif font for maximum clarity. Use distinct hierarchical headings (H2, H3) to break up the dense text. No imagery or distractions.
