# Terms of Service Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Provide legally necessary information in a clean, legible, and un-intimidating format.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation (often simplified for legal pages).
2. **Hero Section (Legal)**: "Terms of Service."
3. **Last Updated Date**: Clear timestamp.
4. **Introduction**: Acceptance of terms.
5. **Services Overview**: Legal definition of what Oasis Caterers provides.
6. **Booking & Deposits**: Terms regarding securing a date.
7. **Payment Schedules**: Legal obligations for final balances.
8. **Cancellations & Refunds**: The formal policy.
9. **Guest Count Adjustments**: Deadlines for final headcounts.
10. **Menu Substitutions**: Clauses allowing for seasonal availability changes.
11. **Liability & Indemnification**: Standard legal protections.
12. **Force Majeure**: Acts of God clause.
13. **Venue Requirements**: Client responsibilities regarding the event location.
14. **Allergies & Dietary Restrictions Liability**: Crucial health and safety disclaimers.
15. **Intellectual Property**: Ownership of recipes, photos, and branding.
16. **Dispute Resolution**: Jurisdiction (e.g., Patiala, Punjab).
17. **Severability**: Standard legal clause.
18. **Changes to Terms**: Right to update the agreement.
19. **Contact for Legal Inquiries**: Dedicated email or address.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
