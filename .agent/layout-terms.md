# Terms & Conditions Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **Legal Hero**: A minimal, text-focused hero section.
3. **Last Updated Date**: Small text indicating the date of the last revision.
4. **Table of Contents**: A sticky left sidebar for easy navigation.
5. **Section 1: Agreement to Terms**: Standard legal preamble.
6. **Section 2: Services Provided**: Definition of the floral and decor services.
7. **Section 3: Booking & Payments**: Terms regarding deposits, payment schedules, and late fees.
8. **Section 4: Cancellations & Refunds**: Clear policy on what happens if an event is canceled.
9. **Section 5: Changes to Order**: How modifications to the floral design are handled leading up to the event.
10. **Section 6: Venue Restrictions**: Liability regarding what venues allow (e.g., hanging installations).
11. **Section 7: Force Majeure**: Acts of God, weather, or unforeseen circumstances.
12. **Section 8: Photography & Usage**: Rights to use photos of the decor for marketing.
13. **Section 9: Rental Items**: Responsibility for damages to rented props or vases.
14. **Section 10: Liability**: Limitations of liability during the event.
15. **Section 11: Governing Law**: Jurisdiction (e.g., Patiala, Punjab).
16. **Section 12: Contact Information**: Where to send legal notices.
17. **Print Button**: A utility button to print the document.
18. **Back to Home Link**: Easy navigation back to the main site.
19. **Decorative Footer Break**: A simple, elegant dividing line.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
