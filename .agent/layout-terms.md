# Terms & Conditions Layout Structure

**Goal:** Present legal information clearly, legibly, and elegantly.

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (Terms of Service):** Minimal text, last updated date clearly visible.
3. **Table of Contents (Optional):** Sticky sidebar for easy navigation through long legal text (desktop only).
4. **Legal Content:** A narrow, highly readable single column of text. Excellent typographic hierarchy (H2s, H3s, bullet points) to make the text scannable.
5. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
