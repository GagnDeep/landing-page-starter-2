# Layout Intent: Terms & Conditions Page

**Goal:** Present legal information clearly, cleanly, and legally soundly, without breaking the site's high-end aesthetic.

## 1. Hero: Legal Header
- **Visual Rhythm:** Stark, minimalist.
- **Layout:** Simple, small heading. No images. Lots of whitespace.

## 2. Last Updated Notice
- **Visual Rhythm:** Micro-copy.
- **Layout:** Small, italicized date right below the title.

## 3. Table of Contents (Sticky Sidebar)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Functional navigation.
- **Layout:** On desktop, a sticky left or right sidebar linking to specific sections to aid readability.

## 4. Section 1: Introduction
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned, highly readable body copy.

## 5. Section 2: Definitions
- **Visual Rhythm:** Definition list.
- **Layout:** Bold terms followed by clear definitions.

## 6. Section 3: Use of Services
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned body copy.

## 7. Section 4: Booking & Cancellations
- **Visual Rhythm:** Highlighted text block.
- **Layout:** Perhaps a slightly different background or subtle border to emphasize the importance of this section.

## 8. Section 5: Payments & Fees
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned body copy.

## 9. Section 6: User Conduct
- **Visual Rhythm:** Bulleted list.
- **Layout:** Clear bullet points outlining acceptable behavior during at-home or in-salon visits.

## 10. Section 7: Intellectual Property
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned body copy.

## 11. Section 8: Limitation of Liability
- **Visual Rhythm:** ALL CAPS or Bold emphasis (if legally required).
- **Layout:** Dense text block, maintaining readability through appropriate line-height.

## 12. Section 9: Dispute Resolution
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned body copy.

## 13. Section 10: Changes to Terms
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned body copy.

## 14. Section 11: Contact Information (Legal)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Distinct contact block.
- **Layout:** Clear email and physical address for legal correspondence.

## 15. Aesthetic Divider
- **Visual Rhythm:** Line art.
- **Layout:** Thin horizontal rule to separate sections clearly if the sidebar isn't used.

## 16. Back to Top Button
- **Visual Rhythm:** Functional UI.
- **Layout:** Necessary for long text pages.

## 17. Link to Privacy Policy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Cross-link.
- **Layout:** Prominent link to the companion legal document.

## 18. Print / Download Option
- **Visual Rhythm:** Utility button.
- **Layout:** A subtle icon/button allowing the user to print the terms easily.

## 19. Final Acknowledgment Statement
- **Visual Rhythm:** Closing text.
- **Layout:** A final sentence before the footer.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
