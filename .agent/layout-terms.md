# Terms & Conditions Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Legal Header**: Clean, serious, and easily readable typography.
2. **Last Updated Date**: Prominently displayed at the top.
3. **Table of Contents**: Anchor links for easy navigation through the document.
4. **Introduction/Acceptance of Terms**: Standard legal preamble.
5. **Definitions**: Clarifying terms like "User", "Site", "Products".
6. **Use of the Website**: Rules regarding user behavior, scraping, and IP.
7. **Product Descriptions & Availability**: Disclaimer that items are handmade and may vary slightly from photos.
8. **Pricing & Payment Terms**: Right to change prices, handling of payment errors.
9. **Order Acceptance & Cancellation**: The bakery's right to refuse or cancel orders.
10. **Shipping & Risk of Loss**: When liability transfers from the bakery to the courier/customer.
11. **Allergen & Health Disclaimer**: Crucial legal protection regarding severe allergies.
12. **Intellectual Property**: Protecting the recipes, branding, and imagery.
13. **User Content/Reviews**: Rules about what customers can post and the bakery's right to use it.
14. **Third-Party Links**: Disclaimer regarding external sites linked from the bakery.
15. **Indemnification**: Standard legal protection clause.
16. **Limitation of Liability**: Capping financial responsibility.
17. **Governing Law/Jurisdiction**: Specifying the local laws that apply.
18. **Changes to Terms**: Right to update the document.
19. **Severability**: Standard clause ensuring if one part is invalid, the rest remains.
20. **Contact Information for Legal Inquiries**: Where to send official notices.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
