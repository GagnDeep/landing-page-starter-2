# Terms & Conditions Page Layout Blueprint

## 1. Header
- **Layout:** Simple, authoritative header. Last updated date clearly visible.

## 2. Content
- **Layout:** A single, narrow column of text with clear, hierarchical headings (H2, H3).
- **Typography:** Highly readable sans-serif, standard sizing.

## 3. Contact for Legal
- **Layout:** A brief section at the bottom directing queries to the appropriate email.
