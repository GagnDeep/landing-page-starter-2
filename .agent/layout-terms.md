# Terms & Conditions Layout Architecture

## 1. Header (`TermsHeader`)
- **Intent:** Legal but branded.
- **Rhythm:** Simple left-aligned text.

## 2. Content Body (`TermsBody`)
- **Intent:** Readability.
- **Rhythm:** Narrow column (`max-w-3xl`). Clear typography hierarchy (H2, H3, paragraphs).

## 3. Table of Contents (`TermsTOC`)
- **Intent:** Navigation.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky sidebar on the left for desktop.
