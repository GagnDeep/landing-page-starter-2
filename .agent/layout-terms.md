# Layout Intent: Terms Page (`/terms`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Extremely minimal. Title: "Terms of Service".
3. **Last Updated Date:** Clear timestamp.
4. **Navigation Sidebar:** Sticky table of contents for long-form text.
5. **Section: Introduction & Acceptance:** Standard legal text.
6. **Section: Services Provided:** Legal definition of the planning services.
7. **Section: Client Responsibilities:** Information required, approvals.
8. **Section: Payment Terms:** Non-refundable retainers, schedules.
9. **Section: Cancellations & Force Majeure:** Crucial for event planning.
10. **Section: Vendor Liability:** Limiting liability for third-party vendors.
11. **Section: Intellectual Property:** Rights to photos/designs.
12. **Section: Dispute Resolution:** Jurisdiction (Chandigarh).
13. **Print Button:** Utility to print the terms.
14. **Contact for Legal:** Specific email for legal inquiries.
15. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
