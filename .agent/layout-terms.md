# Layout Blueprint: Terms and Conditions Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Terms and Conditions").
4.  `LastUpdated`: Date indicating when the terms were last revised.
5.  `Introduction`: General agreement text.
6.  `Eligibility`: Requirements to take classes (age, learner's license).
7.  `BookingPolicy`: Rules regarding scheduling and confirming classes.
8.  `CancellationPolicy`: Rules and fees regarding canceling or rescheduling classes.
9.  `PaymentTerms`: Accepted methods, installment rules, and late fees.
10. `RefundPolicy`: Detailed conditions under which refunds are provided.
11. `StudentConduct`: Expected behavior during lessons (sobriety, following instructions).
12. `InstructorRights`: The instructor's right to terminate a lesson for safety reasons.
13. `VehicleDamage`: Liability regarding accidents or damage during training.
14. `LicenseGuaranteeDisclaimer`: Clarification that the school does not *guarantee* passing the government test.
15. `IntellectualProperty`: Ownership of website content and materials.
16. `LimitationOfLiability`: Standard legal protection clause.
17. `GoverningLaw`: Jurisdiction clause (Chandigarh, India).
18. `ChangesToTerms`: Statement on how users are notified of changes.
19. `ContactLegal`: Specific contact info for legal inquiries.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
