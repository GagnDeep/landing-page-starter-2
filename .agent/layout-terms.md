# Layout Blueprint: Terms & Conditions Page

**Goal:** Clearly state the legal and operational terms of the service in a readable format.

## Sections

1. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
2. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* Simple, authoritative title: "Terms & Conditions".
3. **Last Updated Date**
   - *Intent:* Clear timestamp of the document's currency.
4. **Table of Contents (Sticky sidebar)**
   - *Intent:* Easy navigation through long legal text.
5. **Section 1: General Provisions**
   - *Intent:* Introduction and definitions.
6. **Section 2: Booking and Cancellation Policy**
   - *Intent:* Clear rules on rescheduling or canceling lessons.
7. **Section 3: Payment Terms**
   - *Intent:* Details on fees, installments, and refunds.
8. **Section 4: Code of Conduct**
   - *Intent:* Expectations for both student and instructor behavior.
9. **Section 5: Liability and Insurance**
   - *Intent:* Legal disclaimers regarding accidents or damages during lessons.
10. **Section 6: RTO / Licensing Assistance**
    - *Intent:* Clarify that the school assists but cannot guarantee the RTO passing the student.
11. **Section 7: Dispute Resolution**
    - *Intent:* Legal jurisdiction details.
12. **Contact Information for Legal Inquiries**
    - *Intent:* How to reach out with questions about the terms.
13. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
