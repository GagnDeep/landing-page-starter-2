# Layout Blueprint: Terms of Service Page

## Intent
Provide legally sound, clearly formatted terms of service for website usage and salon policies.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** "Terms of Service" - simple, clean typography.
3. **Last Updated Date:** Prominent display of the last revision date.
4. **Introduction:** General agreement terms.
5. **Definitions:** Clarification of terms used (e.g., "Salon", "Client", "Website").
6. **Use of Website:** Rules regarding intellectual property and acceptable use.
7. **Booking Policies:** Binding terms regarding appointment creation.
8. **Cancellation & No-Show Policy (Legal):** Formal statement of the policy.
9. **Pricing & Payment Terms:** Rights to change prices, payment obligations.
10. **Service Guarantees:** Limitations of liability regarding hair outcomes.
11. **Allergy & Health Disclaimers:** Client responsibility to disclose medical conditions.
12. **Product Returns Policy:** Rules for returning retail items.
13. **Gift Card Terms:** Expiration dates, non-transferability.
14. **User-Generated Content:** Rights regarding photos taken in-salon or shared online.
15. **Third-Party Links:** Disclaimer regarding external websites.
16. **Limitation of Liability:** Standard legal protections for the business.
17. **Indemnification:** Client agreement to hold the salon harmless.
18. **Governing Law:** Jurisdiction (Chandigarh, India).
19. **Contact Information for Legal Inquiries:** Where to send formal notices.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
