# Layout Documentation: Terms Page (`/terms`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
A purely utilitarian page. It must be highly readable, cleanly structured, and devoid of distracting elements. Typography is paramount here.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Terms-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`Terms-02-SimpleHero`**: Minimalist header "Terms of Service".
3.  **`Terms-03-LastUpdated`**: Small, italicized text indicating the date of the last revision.
4.  **`Terms-04-ContentContainer`**: A narrow, single-column layout optimized for long-form reading (approx 65-75 characters per line).
5.  **`Terms-05-TableOfContents`**: A sticky or static list of links jumping to specific sections.
6.  **`Terms-06-SectionHeader`**: H2 level serif headers for major clauses.
7.  **`Terms-07-SubSectionHeader`**: H3 level sans-serif headers for minor clauses.
8.  **`Terms-08-StandardTextParagraph`**: Standard body text, high contrast, readable line height.
9.  **`Terms-09-NumberedList`**: Cleanly styled ordered lists for sequential terms.
10. **`Terms-10-BulletedList`**: Cleanly styled unordered lists.
11. **`Terms-11-ImportantClauseBox`**: A slightly shaded box to highlight critical information (e.g., Liability).
12. **`Terms-12-OrderingTerms`**: Specific section related to placing orders and availability.
13. **`Terms-13-PaymentTerms`**: Specific section covering pricing, taxes, and payment methods.
14. **`Terms-14-CancellationPolicy`**: Highly visible section detailing when and how orders can be cancelled.
15. **`Terms-15-AllergenDisclaimer`**: Crucial legal text regarding cross-contamination.
16. **`Terms-16-IntellectualProperty`**: Standard clause regarding site content and imagery.
17. **`Terms-17-ContactForLegal`**: Details on who to contact regarding these terms.
18. **`Terms-18-PrintButton`**: A simple utility button to print the document.
19. **`Terms-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`Terms-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
