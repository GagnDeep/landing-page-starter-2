# Layout Intent: Terms & Conditions Page

A purely functional, legal document. It must be highly legible and cleanly structured, maintaining the brand's typographic elegance even in dense text.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`LegalHeader`**
    *   **Intent:** Simple title and "Last Updated" date.
    *   **Rhythm:** Left-aligned, straightforward.
3.  **`DocumentContent`**
    *   **Intent:** The body of the legal text.
    *   **Rhythm:** A single, narrow reading column (max-w-prose) centered on the page. Heavy use of `H2` and `H3` tags for section breaks, and standard `p` tags for clauses. Ordered lists for precise legal enumeration.
4.  **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
