# Layout Intent: Terms Page

## Core Intent
Outline the terms of service, booking policies, and guarantees clearly and professionally, maintaining the brand's trustworthy tone.

## Key Sections
1. **Simple Header:** Title and last updated date.
2. **Policy Sections:** Clear divisions for topics like "Booking Terms," "Alteration Guarantee Specifics," "Cancellation Policy," and "Payment Terms."
3. **Text Content Block:** Readable, well-spaced typography to ensure the information is accessible without feeling like a dense legal contract.
