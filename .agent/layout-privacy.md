# Layout Intent: Privacy Policy Page

Identical in structure and intent to the Terms page—functional, highly legible, and elegantly typeset.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`LegalHeader`**
    *   **Intent:** Simple title and "Last Updated" date.
    *   **Rhythm:** Left-aligned, straightforward.
3.  **`DocumentContent`**
    *   **Intent:** The body of the legal text detailing data handling and privacy practices.
    *   **Rhythm:** A single, narrow reading column (max-w-prose) centered on the page. Heavy use of `H2` and `H3` tags, standard `p` tags, and bulleted lists.
4.  **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
