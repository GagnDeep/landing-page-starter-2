   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.

2. **Hero Section (Privacy Policy)**
   - *Layout intent:* Very minimal, text-only hero.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Clinical but elegant.

3. **Last Updated Date**
   - *Layout intent:* A small text block at the top of the document.

4. **Table of Contents**

5. **Section 1: Information We Collect**

   - *Visual rhythm:* Good typography, clear lists.

6. **Section 2: How We Use Your Information**

7. **Section 3: Sharing Your Information**
