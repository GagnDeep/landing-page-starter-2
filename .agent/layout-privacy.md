# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy

## Intent
To clearly explain how client data (contact info, event details, payment data) is collected, used, and protected, ensuring compliance and trust.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** Minimalist header "Privacy Policy".
3. **Last Updated Date:** Text indicating the revision date.
4. **Introduction:** Commitment to data privacy.
5. **Section 1: Information We Collect:** Personal, event, and technical data.
6. **Section 2: How We Collect Information:** Forms, cookies, direct contact.
7. **Section 3: Use of Your Information:** Service delivery, marketing, improvement.
8. **Section 4: Disclosure of Information:** Third-party vendors (e.g., payment processors).
9. **Section 5: Data Security:** How data is stored and protected.
10. **Section 6: Data Retention:** How long records are kept.
11. **Section 7: Your Data Rights:** Access, correction, and deletion requests.
12. **Section 8: Cookies Policy:** Explanation of site tracking.
13. **Section 9: Third-Party Links:** Disclaimer regarding external websites.
14. **Section 10: Children's Privacy:** Standard COPPA compliance clause.
15. **Section 11: Changes to This Policy:** How updates are communicated.
16. **Section 12: Contact Us Regarding Privacy:** Specific DPO or privacy email.
17. **Cookie Consent Adjustment Link:** Utility to change cookie preferences.
18. **Data Request Form Link:** Link to request data deletion.
19. **Print Button:** Utility to print the document.
20. **Back to Home Link:** Navigation utility.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
Identical to the Terms page—a classic "prose" layout. Narrow column, high contrast, clean typography designed strictly for legibility without distraction.
