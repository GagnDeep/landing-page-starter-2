# Layout Intent: Privacy Page (`/privacy`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Extremely minimal. Title: "Privacy Policy".
3. **Last Updated Date:** Clear timestamp.
4. **Navigation Sidebar:** Sticky table of contents.
5. **Section: Data Collection:** What is collected (names, budgets, guest lists).
6. **Section: Use of Data:** How it's used (solely for event execution).
7. **Section: Data Sharing:** Who it's shared with (only necessary vendors/hotels).
8. **Section: International Transfers:** Important for NRI clients (data crossing borders).
9. **Section: Data Security:** Reassurance on the security of sensitive client info.
10. **Section: Client Rights:** Right to access, delete, or modify data.
11. **Section: Cookies:** Standard cookie policy.
12. **Section: Third-Party Links:** Disclaimer regarding external links.
13. **Opt-Out Mechanism:** Instructions on how to opt-out of marketing.
14. **Contact for Privacy:** Specific email for Data Protection Officer/Inquiries.
15. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
