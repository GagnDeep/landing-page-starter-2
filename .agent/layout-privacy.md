# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy Page

## Intent
Detail how client data is collected, used, and protected, ensuring trust and compliance.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** "Privacy Policy" - simple, clean typography.
3. **Last Updated Date:** Prominent display of the last revision date.
4. **Introduction:** Commitment to protecting client privacy.
5. **Information We Collect:** Details on personal data (name, email, phone).
6. **Health & Hair History Data:** Specifics on storing chemical history or allergies.
7. **How We Collect Data:** Forms, cookies, booking systems, in-person consultations.
8. **Use of Your Information:** Why data is needed (appointments, marketing, service quality).
9. **Data Sharing & Disclosure:** Clarifying that data is not sold; details on third-party processors (e.g., booking software).
10. **Data Security Measures:** How client files and digital records are protected.
11. **Data Retention:** How long information is kept on file.
12. **Your Rights:** Client ability to access, correct, or delete their data.
13. **Cookies & Tracking Technologies:** Explanation of website analytics.
14. **Marketing Communications:** How to opt-in or opt-out of newsletters/SMS.
15. **Children's Privacy:** Policy regarding minors' data.
16. **Changes to This Policy:** How updates will be communicated.
17. **Third-Party Links (Privacy Context):** Disclaimer for linked sites.
18. **Governing Law (Privacy Context):** Jurisdiction for data disputes.
19. **Contact Information for Privacy Concerns:** Specific email or address for data requests.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
