# Privacy Policy Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **Legal Hero**: A minimal, text-focused hero section.
3. **Last Updated Date**: Small text indicating the date of the last revision.
4. **Table of Contents**: A sticky left sidebar for easy navigation.
5. **Section 1: Introduction**: Statement on commitment to privacy.
6. **Section 2: Information We Collect**: Details on personal data gathered (names, emails, dates).
7. **Section 3: How We Use Your Information**: Explanation of using data for booking, communication, and service delivery.
8. **Section 4: Cookies & Tracking**: Information on website analytics and cookies used.
9. **Section 5: Third-Party Services**: Mention of payment processors or CRM tools used.
10. **Section 6: Data Retention**: How long client information is kept.
11. **Section 7: Data Security**: Measures taken to protect client information.
12. **Section 8: Your Data Rights**: Information on how clients can request data deletion or modification.
13. **Section 9: Children's Privacy**: Standard clause regarding minors.
14. **Section 10: Changes to Policy**: How updates to the privacy policy will be communicated.
15. **Section 11: Contact Us regarding Privacy**: Specific email for privacy concerns.
16. **Print Button**: A utility button to print the document.
17. **Cookie Consent Management**: Link to reopen the cookie consent banner.
18. **Back to Home Link**: Easy navigation back to the main site.
19. **Decorative Footer Break**: A simple, elegant dividing line.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
