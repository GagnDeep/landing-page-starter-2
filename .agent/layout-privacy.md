# Layout Intent: Privacy Policy Page

**Goal:** Present data privacy information transparently and cleanly, maintaining trust through clear communication and aesthetic consistency.

## 1. Hero: Privacy Header
- **Visual Rhythm:** Stark, minimalist.
- **Layout:** Simple, centered or left-aligned title. No distracting imagery.

## 2. Last Updated / Effective Date
- **Visual Rhythm:** Micro-copy.
- **Layout:** Small, muted text directly beneath the main heading.

## 3. Table of Contents (Sticky Sidebar)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Functional navigation.
- **Layout:** On desktop, a sticky sidebar listing the policy sections for easy jumping.

## 4. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Reassuring text block.
- **Layout:** A clear, readable paragraph stating the brand's respect for client data.

## 5. Section 1: Information We Collect
- **Visual Rhythm:** Categorized list.
- **Layout:** Distinct subheadings for "Personal Data", "Usage Data", and "Cookies".

## 6. Section 2: How We Use Your Data
- **Visual Rhythm:** Bulleted list.
- **Layout:** Clean, easily scannable points detailing usage (booking, marketing, improvements).

## 7. Section 3: Data Sharing & Third Parties
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Left-aligned body copy explaining partners (e.g., payment processors, booking software).

## 8. Section 4: Data Security
- **Visual Rhythm:** Highlighted text block.
- **Layout:** Emphasized section outlining the steps taken to protect client information.

## 9. Section 5: Your Data Rights
- **Visual Rhythm:** Bulleted list.
- **Layout:** Clear points outlining the user's rights (access, deletion, modification).

## 10. Section 6: Cookies Policy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Explanation of cookie usage and how to opt-out.

## 11. Cookie Preferences Trigger
- **Visual Rhythm:** Utility button.
- **Layout:** A button embedded in the text to re-open the cookie consent banner/modal.

## 12. Section 7: Children's Privacy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Brief legal disclaimer.

## 13. Section 8: Changes to Privacy Policy
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Layout:** Explanation of how users will be notified of updates.

## 14. Section 9: Contact the Data Protection Officer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Contact block.
- **Layout:** Specific email address for privacy-related inquiries.

## 15. Aesthetic Divider
- **Visual Rhythm:** Line art.
- **Layout:** Subtle separator lines between major sections.

## 16. Back to Top Button
- **Visual Rhythm:** Functional UI.
- **Layout:** Floating or bottom-aligned button for easy navigation of the long document.

## 17. Link to Terms & Conditions
- **Visual Rhythm:** Cross-link.
- **Layout:** Prominent link back to the main terms page.

## 18. Print / Download Option
- **Visual Rhythm:** Utility button.
- **Layout:** Subtle icon/button to print the policy.

## 19. Region-Specific Disclaimers (e.g., GDPR, CCPA)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Muted text block.
- **Layout:** If applicable, smaller text at the bottom addressing specific jurisdictional rights.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
