# Privacy Policy Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Privacy Policy Header**: Clear, trustworthy presentation.
2. **Last Updated Date**: Prominently displayed.
3. **Table of Contents**: Anchor links for navigation.
4. **Introduction & Commitment to Privacy**: A plain-English summary of respect for customer data.
5. **Information We Collect - Personal**: Detailing name, address, payment info collected during checkout.
6. **Information We Collect - Automated**: Detailing cookies, IP addresses, and analytics.
7. **How We Use Your Information**: Fulfilling orders, marketing, improving the site.
8. **Information Sharing & Disclosure**: Clarifying that data is not sold, but shared with necessary partners (couriers, payment processors).
9. **Payment Processing Security**: Details on third-party secure gateways (PCI compliance).
10. **Cookie Policy**: Explanation of what cookies are used and how to disable them.
11. **Data Retention**: How long customer data is kept on file.
12. **Your Rights & Choices**: How users can request data deletion or opt-out of marketing.
13. **Security Measures**: How the bakery protects digital data.
14. **Children's Privacy**: Standard COPPA compliance clause (if applicable).
15. **International Data Transfers**: Relevant if the site serves or hosts data outside the home country.
16. **Third-Party Services**: Privacy practices of embedded tools (e.g., Google Maps, Analytics).
17. **Changes to This Policy**: How users will be notified of updates.
18. **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Contact details if required by local law.
19. **Plain English Summary**: A quick tl;dr at the bottom for easy understanding.
20. **Contact Us About Privacy**: Dedicated email for privacy concerns.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
