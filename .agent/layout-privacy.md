# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Privacy Policy").
4.  `LastUpdated`: Date indicating when the policy was last revised.
5.  `Introduction`: General statement about commitment to privacy.
6.  `DataCollection_Direct`: What data is collected directly (Name, Phone, ID documents for licensing).
7.  `DataCollection_Automated`: What data is collected automatically (Cookies, Analytics).
8.  `UseOfData`: How the collected data is used (Scheduling, Licensing, Marketing).
9.  `DataSharing`: Circumstances under which data is shared (e.g., with Government RTO for licensing).
10. `DataSecurity`: Measures taken to protect user data.
11. `DataRetention`: How long data is kept on file.
12. `UserRights`: Rights regarding accessing, correcting, or deleting personal data.
13. `CookiePolicy`: Detailed explanation of cookie usage and management.
14. `ThirdPartyLinks`: Disclaimer regarding links to external websites.
15. `ChildrensPrivacy`: Statement on data collection regarding minors.
16. `InternationalTransfers`: (If applicable) Data storage locations.
17. `ChangesToPolicy`: Statement on how updates to the policy are communicated.
18. `OptOutInstructions`: How to stop receiving marketing communications.
19. `ContactPrivacy`: Specific contact info for privacy concerns.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
