# Privacy Policy Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Reassure high-net-worth clients that their personal and event data is handled with the utmost discretion and security.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation (simplified).
2. **Hero Section (Privacy)**: "Privacy Policy."
3. **Last Updated Date**: Clear timestamp.
4. **Introduction**: Commitment to client confidentiality.
5. **Information We Collect**: Breakdown of data points (Name, Contact, Event Details, Payment Info).
6. **How We Use Your Information**: Execution of services, communication, internal analytics.
7. **Information Sharing & Disclosure**: When data is shared (e.g., with trusted partner vendors or venues).
8. **Data Security**: Measures taken to protect client information.
9. **Cookies and Tracking Technologies**: Explanation of website analytics.
10. **Third-Party Links**: Disclaimer regarding external websites.
11. **Client Rights regarding Data**: Access, correction, and deletion requests.
12. **Retention of Information**: How long event records are kept.
13. **Children's Privacy**: Standard COPPA compliance clause if applicable.
14. **International Data Transfers**: If handling NRI clients, how data crosses borders.
15. **Marketing Communications**: Opt-in/Opt-out procedures.
16. **Changes to This Policy**: Right to update.
17. **Contact for Privacy Concerns**: Dedicated email or address (e.g., privacy@oasiscaterers.com).
18. **Table of Contents (Sidebar)**: Sticky navigation for easy reading.
19. **Downloadable PDF Version**: Link to a formal document.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
