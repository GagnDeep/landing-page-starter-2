# Layout Documentation: Privacy Page (`/privacy`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
Similar to the Terms page, this is a utilitarian, text-heavy layout focused entirely on readability and clear legal communication.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Privacy-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`Privacy-02-SimpleHero`**: Minimalist header "Privacy Policy".
3.  **`Privacy-03-LastUpdated`**: Small text indicating the date of the last revision.
4.  **`Privacy-04-ContentContainer`**: Narrow, single-column reading layout.
5.  **`Privacy-05-TableOfContents`**: Links to jump to sections.
6.  **`Privacy-06-SectionHeader`**: H2 level serif headers.
7.  **`Privacy-07-SubSectionHeader`**: H3 level sans-serif headers.
8.  **`Privacy-08-StandardTextParagraph`**: Standard body text.
9.  **`Privacy-09-DataCollectionGrid`**: A clear table or grid showing what data is collected and why.
10. **`Privacy-10-CookiesPolicy`**: Specific section detailing the use of cookies and tracking.
11. **`Privacy-11-ThirdPartySharing`**: Section explaining if/how data is shared with partners (e.g., payment processors).
12. **`Privacy-12-UserRights`**: Section detailing the user's right to access or delete their data.
13. **`Privacy-13-SecurityMeasures`**: Brief explanation of how data is protected.
14. **`Privacy-14-ChildrensPrivacy`**: Standard legal clause regarding minors.
15. **`Privacy-15-DataRetention`**: Details on how long data is kept.
16. **`Privacy-16-InternationalTransfers`**: (If applicable) Information on data moving across borders.
17. **`Privacy-17-DataProtectionOfficer`**: Contact details for the person responsible for privacy.
18. **`Privacy-18-OptOutInstructions`**: Clear steps on how to unsubscribe from marketing.
19. **`Privacy-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`Privacy-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
