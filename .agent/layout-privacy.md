# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy Page (`/privacy`)

## Goal
To explain how user data (forms, cookies) is collected, stored, and protected in compliance with standard privacy laws.

## Section Architecture (15 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Page Header:** "Privacy Policy." Last updated date clearly visible.
3.  **Introduction:** Commitment to protecting student data.
4.  **Section 1: Information We Collect:** Breaking down personal info (names, numbers) vs. technical info (cookies).
5.  **Section 2: How We Use Your Information:** Explaining that data is used for booking, communication, and service improvement.
6.  **Section 3: Information Sharing:** Clarifying that data is NOT sold, but may be shared with RTO authorities if required.
7.  **Section 4: Data Security:** How digital records and physical forms are protected.
8.  **Section 5: Cookies and Tracking:** Explanation of site analytics (e.g., Google Analytics).
9.  **Section 6: Third-Party Links:** Disclaimer regarding external links (e.g., to payment gateways or RTO sites).
10. **Section 7: Your Rights:** How users can request data deletion or modification.
11. **Section 8: Children's Privacy:** Policies regarding learners under 18 (parental consent).
12. **Section 9: Changes to Policy:** Notification process for updates.
13. **Contact for Privacy Concerns:** Dedicated email or address.
14. **Back to Home Button:** Simple navigation aid.
15. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
