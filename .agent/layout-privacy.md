# Privacy Policy Layout Architecture

## 1. Header (`PrivacyHeader`)
- **Intent:** Legal but branded.
- **Rhythm:** Simple left-aligned text.

## 2. Content Body (`PrivacyBody`)
- **Intent:** Readability.
- **Rhythm:** Narrow column (`max-w-3xl`). Clear typography hierarchy.

## 3. Table of Contents (`PrivacyTOC`)
- **Intent:** Navigation.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky sidebar on the left for desktop.
