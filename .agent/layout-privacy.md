# Layout Architecture: Privacy Policy Page

## Structural Intent
Standard legal page. Focus on transparency regarding data collection, especially concerning inquiry forms and analytics.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Global Notice Bar:** (Consistent with global layout)
2. **Main Navigation (Header):** (Consistent with global layout)
3. **Hero Section (Privacy Policy):** Simple, clear headline.
4. **Last Updated Date:** Prominently displayed.
5. **Table of Contents (Optional but recommended):** Jump links.
6. **Section 1: Information We Collect:** Overview.
7. **Personal Information:** Details on name, email, phone collected via forms.
8. **Derivative Data:** Analytics, IP addresses, browser types.
9. **Section 2: How We Use Your Information:** Purpose of collection (e.g., to contact regarding inquiries).
10. **Section 3: Disclosure of Your Information:** Third-party sharing (e.g., vendor partners, only with consent).
11. **Section 4: Tracking Technologies:** Cookies, web beacons.
12. **Section 5: Third-Party Websites:** Links to external sites.
13. **Section 6: Security of Your Information:** Measures taken to protect data.
14. **Section 7: Policy for Children:** Standard COPPA boilerplate.
15. **Section 8: Controls for Do-Not-Track Features:** Browser settings.
16. **Section 9: Specific Privacy Rights:** Depending on jurisdiction.
17. **Section 10: Updates to This Notice:** How users are notified of changes.
18. **Section 11: Contact Us About This Policy:** Specific email or address.
19. **Data Deletion Request Link/Info:** How users can request data removal.
20. **Global Footer:** (Consistent with global layout)
