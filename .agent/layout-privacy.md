# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy Page

**Goal:** Transparently explain how user data is collected, used, and protected.

## Sections

1. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
2. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* Simple title: "Privacy Policy".
3. **Last Updated Date**
   - *Intent:* Clear timestamp.
4. **Table of Contents (Sticky sidebar)**
   - *Intent:* Easy navigation.
5. **Section 1: Information We Collect**
   - *Intent:* Detail personal info (name, phone) and technical info (cookies) collected.
6. **Section 2: How We Use Your Information**
   - *Intent:* Explain usage for scheduling, communication, and RTO paperwork.
7. **Section 3: Information Sharing and Disclosure**
   - *Intent:* Clarify that data is not sold, but may be shared with RTO authorities as needed.
8. **Section 4: Data Security**
   - *Intent:* Steps taken to protect user information.
9. **Section 5: Your Rights**
   - *Intent:* Options for users to access or delete their data.
10. **Section 6: Cookies Policy**
    - *Intent:* Brief explanation of tracking for analytics.
11. **Contact for Privacy Concerns**
    - *Intent:* Dedicated email or contact method for data requests.
12. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
