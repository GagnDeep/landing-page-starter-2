# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

## Intent
To attract top culinary and hospitality talent in the region by showcasing the company culture, benefits, and current openings.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** "Join Our Kitchen Family" with an engaging photo of the staff at work.
3. **Culture Statement:** A paragraph about the values of respect, teamwork, and culinary excellence.
4. **Why Work With Us Title:** Section header.
5. **Benefit 1: Competitive Pay:** Icon and description.
6. **Benefit 2: Training & Growth:** Icon and description.
7. **Benefit 3: Meals Provided:** Icon and description.
8. **Benefit 4: Family Environment:** Icon and description.
9. **Employee Testimonial:** A quote from a long-standing chef or staff member.
10. **Open Positions Title:** Section header with a filter (Kitchen, Front of House, Admin).
11. **Job Listing 1 (e.g., Sous Chef):** Card with title, type (Full-time), and short description.
12. **Job Listing 2 (e.g., Waitstaff):** Card with details.
13. **Job Listing 3 (e.g., Event Coordinator):** Card with details.
14. **Application Process Steps:** A visual 1-2-3 guide (Apply -> Interview -> Trial).
15. **General Application Form Title:** For speculative applications.
16. **Form Field: Name & Contact:** Inputs.
17. **Form Field: Role of Interest:** Dropdown.
18. **Form Field: Resume Upload:** File input area.
19. **Submit Application Button:** CTA.
20. **Equal Opportunity Statement:** A brief legal/ethical note.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
Energetic and professional. Uses behind-the-scenes photography to build excitement, followed by a clean, organized list of benefits and job openings, culminating in a straightforward application form.
