# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

## Intent
Attract top talent in the Chandigarh area by showcasing the salon's culture, benefits, and prestige.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Standard sticky nav.
2. **Hero:** "Join Our Team" or "Elevate Your Career".
3. **Culture Statement:** Why Villa Hair Sense is a great place to work.
4. **Benefits Overview:** Grid of perks (e.g., continuous education, premium tools, health benefits).
5. **The Villa Standard:** Expectations of excellence and artistry.
6. **Career Paths:** How a junior stylist can grow into a master.
7. **Current Openings (Header):** Section title for job listings.
8. **Job Listing: Senior Stylist:** Expandable card with details.
9. **Job Listing: Color Specialist:** Expandable card with details.
10. **Job Listing: Salon Coordinator:** Expandable card with details.
11. **Job Listing: Assistant/Apprentice:** Expandable card with details.
12. **Application Process:** Step-by-step guide on how to apply.
13. **Employee Testimonials:** Quotes from current staff.
14. **Education & Training Program:** Details on the internal academy.
15. **Salon Life Gallery:** Images of team events, training sessions, and daily operations.
16. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement:** Commitment to a welcoming workplace.
17. **General Application Form:** Form for speculative applications if no roles fit.
18. **FAQ (Careers Specific):** Questions about trials, portfolios, etc.
19. **Follow Our Journey:** Link to the salon's professional/team Instagram.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
