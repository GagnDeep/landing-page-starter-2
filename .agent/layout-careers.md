# Careers Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To attract high-tier culinary and hospitality talent. The tone should be demanding but rewarding, highlighting the prestige of working for the brand.

## Sections

1.  **Hero: Join the Legacy**
    *   *Intent:* Establish the prestige of the workplace.
    *   *Visuals:* A dynamic shot of the team in action. Bold typography.

2.  **Our Culture & Standards**
    *   *Intent:* Explain what it takes to work here.
    *   *Visuals:* A text-heavy section detailing the rigorous standards and the rewarding environment.

3.  **Benefits of Excellence**
    *   *Intent:* Why work here?
    *   *Visuals:* A clean grid listing benefits (training, compensation, environment).

4.  **Open Positions**
    *   *Intent:* The functional core of the page.
    *   *Visuals:* An accordion or a simple list layout. Each item expands to show the job description and a link to apply.

5.  **General Application CTA**
    *   *Intent:* Catch talent even if a specific role isn't listed.
    *   *Visuals:* A simple form or email link for general submissions.
