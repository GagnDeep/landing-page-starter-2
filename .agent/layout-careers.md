# Careers Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Attract top-tier culinary and hospitality talent. Convey that Oasis Caterers is a prestigious institution, not just a job.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation.
2. **Hero Section (Join the Craft)**: "A legacy built by master artisans."
3. **Culture Overview**: Text block detailing the high standards and supportive environment.
4. **Why Oasis?**: 3-column feature grid (e.g., Growth, Prestige, Environment).
5. **A Day in the Life Video**: High-quality video of the kitchen and event execution.
6. **Open Roles Header**: "Current Opportunities."
7. **Department Filters**: Culinary, Service, Logistics, Management.
8. **Job Listing 1 (Executive Chef)**: Detailed accordion or linked card.
9. **Job Listing 2 (Event Captain)**: Detailed accordion or linked card.
10. **Job Listing 3 (Prep Cook)**: Detailed accordion or linked card.
11. **Employee Testimonials**: Quotes from current staff about their experience.
12. **Training & Development**: Information on the Oasis Academy or onboarding process.
13. **Benefits & Perks**: Clean list of what is offered to the team.
14. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement**: Clear, formal commitment.
15. **The Application Process**: Timeline of what to expect after applying.
16. **General Application Form**: For those whose roles aren't currently listed.
17. **Apprenticeship Program**: Information for culinary students.
18. **Gallery (The Team in Action)**: High-energy, professional behind-the-scenes photos.
19. **Primary Call to Action**: "Apply Now."
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
