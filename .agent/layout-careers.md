# Layout Intent: Careers Page

## Core Intent
Attract top tailoring talent and administrative staff who share the boutique's dedication to craftsmanship and customer service.

## Key Sections
1. **Hero Header:** Focus on joining a team of master craftspeople.
2. **Culture & Environment:** A section detailing the positive, professional, and supportive work environment at Stitching Solutions.
3. **Open Positions Grid:** A list of current openings (e.g., Master Tailor, Pattern Maker, Customer Experience Associate).
4. **Application Process:** Clear steps on how to apply and what to expect.
5. **Employee Testimonials (Optional):** Short quotes from current staff about working at the boutique.
