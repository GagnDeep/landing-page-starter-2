# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Join Our Team").
4.  `CultureIntro`: Why work at Azad Driving School (Focus on safety, community, and respect).
5.  `BenefitsGrid`: List of employee benefits (competitive pay, flexible hours, training).
6.  `Role_Instructor`: Specific details on what makes a great instructor here.
7.  `Role_Admin`: Details for office/administrative roles.
8.  `CurrentOpenings`: Dynamic list or section showing available jobs.
9.  `ApplicationProcess`: 1-2-3 guide on how to apply and the interview process.
10. `ApplicationForm`: Embedded form to submit a resume and cover letter.
11. `Requirements_Instructor`: Hard requirements for instructors (valid license, clean record, teaching cert).
12. `EmployeeTestimonial`: Quote from a current instructor about the work environment.
13. `DiversityStatement`: Commitment to equal opportunity employment.
14. `TrainingProgram`: How the school trains its new instructors.
15. `DayInTheLife`: A brief narrative of a typical day for an instructor.
16. `ValuesReiteration`: Connecting the job back to the core values of the school.
17. `LocationHighlight`: Working in the Burail, Chandigarh area.
18. `FAQ_Careers`: Questions specific to employment (e.g., "Do I need my own car?").
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Encourage general applications even if no positions are open.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
