# Layout Documentation: Careers Page (`/careers`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
This page is about selling the bakery's culture. It should be dynamic, showing the passion of the team. It uses a mix of lifestyle imagery and clear, structured lists for job openings.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Careers-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`Careers-02-HeroImageText`**: Vibrant image of the kitchen team with a large overlay "Join Our Family".
3.  **`Careers-03-CultureIntro`**: A text block explaining the core values and what it means to work at Verma's.
4.  **`Careers-04-PerksGrid`**: A 3-column grid of icons/text highlighting benefits (e.g., Free Bakes, Training, Health).
5.  **`Careers-05-TeamTestimonial`**: A quote from a current employee about their experience.
6.  **`Careers-06-OpenPositionsHeader`**: Clear heading introducing the job list.
7.  **`Careers-07-JobFilter`**: Simple buttons to filter by department (Kitchen, Front of House, Management).
8.  **`Careers-08-JobList`**: A vertical layout of available roles.
9.  **`Careers-09-JobListItem`**: Flex row showing Job Title, Location, and Type (Full-time/Part-time), with an "Apply" link.
10. **`Careers-10-JobDetailsModal`**: (Or separate page) Detailed view of the job requirements and responsibilities.
11. **`Careers-11-ApprenticeshipBanner`**: A distinct section highlighting opportunities for training or internships.
12. **`Careers-12-DiversityStatement`**: A text block outlining the commitment to an inclusive workplace.
13. **`Careers-13-HiringProcess`**: A simple 3 or 4 step timeline explaining what happens after applying.
14. **`Careers-14-DayInTheLifeGallery`**: A small grid of candid photos showing a typical day at the bakery.
15. **`Careers-15-GeneralApplication`**: Text and button for people to submit a resume even if no suitable role is listed.
16. **`Careers-16-ContactHR`**: Direct email link for recruitment questions.
17. **`Careers-17-BrandVideo`**: (Optional) Embed of a short documentary-style video about the bakery.
18. **`Careers-18-FollowOurJourney`**: CTA to follow the bakery on LinkedIn or Instagram.
19. **`Careers-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`Careers-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
