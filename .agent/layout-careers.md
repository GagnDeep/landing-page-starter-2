# Careers Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **Careers Hero**: A dynamic hero showing the team in action, creating floral arrangements.
3. **Culture Statement**: A strong heading about joining a team of artisans.
4. **Why Work With Us**: A 3-column grid highlighting company culture.
5. **Benefit 1**: Creative freedom.
6. **Benefit 2**: Learning traditional crafts.
7. **Benefit 3**: Being part of significant life moments.
8. **Open Positions Title**: "Current Opportunities".
9. **Job Card 1 (Senior Florist)**: Title, location, type (Full-time), and "View Details" button.
10. **Job Card 2 (Event Setup Crew)**: Title, location, type, and button.
11. **Job Card 3 (Design Consultant)**: Title, location, type, and button.
12. **Internship/Apprenticeship Block**: Information for young designers wanting to learn the craft.
13. **Day in the Life Gallery**: A masonry grid of candid team photos.
14. **Team Testimonial**: A quote from a current employee about the work environment.
15. **Application Process**: A 3-step list explaining how to apply and interview.
16. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement**: A note on welcoming all backgrounds.
17. **General Application**: A callout for people to send their portfolio even if no specific job is listed.
18. **Contact for HR**: Direct email for career inquiries.
19. **Decorative Elements**: Subtle floral illustrations breaking up the sections.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
