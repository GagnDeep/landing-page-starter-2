# Layout Intent: Careers Page

Attracting top culinary and hospitality talent. The design should convey that working for Crown Caterers is a prestigious, demanding, and rewarding artistic endeavor.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`CareersHero`**
    *   **Intent:** "Join the Art of Hospitality." An energetic, behind-the-scenes image of a bustling, high-end kitchen or a beautifully coordinated service team.
3.  **`CultureStatement`**
    *   **Intent:** Defining the high standards and the supportive, elite environment.
    *   **Rhythm:** A centered, medium-width text block with a strong serif headline.
4.  **`BenefitsGrid`**
    *   **Intent:** Highlighting perks (Global travel, elite training, competitive compensation).
    *   **Rhythm:** A 3-column grid with subtle icons and concise text.
5.  **`OpenPositionsList`**
    *   **Intent:** The functional core of the page.
    *   **Rhythm:** A clean, expanding accordion or a table-like list categorized by department (Culinary, Operations, Management). Each row should have a sleek "Apply" button.
6.  **`EmployeeTestimonial`**
    *   **Intent:** A quote from a long-standing chef or manager.
    *   **Rhythm:** A large pull quote with an accompanying portrait, similar to the main testimonial section but focused on internal culture.
7.  **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
