# Layout Documentation: Careers Page

## Intent
Attract individuals who respect the craft. The tone should be about joining a legacy, not just getting a job.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Join the Atelier." Image of apprentices or tailors working together.
2. **Culture/Values:** Why work here? Focus on mastery, tradition, and passing on knowledge.
3. **Open Positions:** An elegant list of roles (e.g., "Master Cutter," "Apprentice Tailor," "Client Advisor").
4. **Application Process:** Step-by-step text explanation.
