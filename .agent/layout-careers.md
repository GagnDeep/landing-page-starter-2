# Layout Intent: Careers Page (`/careers`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Title: "Join the Architects of Celebration". Image of the team.
3. **Culture Statement:** Text block on the high standards and fast-paced luxury environment.
4. **Why Guide Events?:** 3-column grid on benefits (e.g., Working with elite clients, travel, growth).
5. **Day in the Life:** Short video or photo gallery showing behind-the-scenes work.
6. **Core Values:** Reiteration of company values specifically for applicants.
7. **Open Positions Listing:** A clean list/table of available roles.
8. **Role Detail View (Modal/Expand):** Layout for job description, requirements, and responsibilities.
9. **Application Process:** A 4-step visual (Apply -> Portfolio Review -> Interview -> Trial).
10. **Internship Program:** Specific section for the "Guide Events Apprenticeship".
11. **Testimonials from Staff:** Quotes from current employees.
12. **Diversity & Inclusion:** Statement on hiring practices.
13. **Freelance/Vendor Roster:** A distinct CTA for vendors wanting to join the preferred list.
14. **General Application Form:** Form for unsolicited applications.
15. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
