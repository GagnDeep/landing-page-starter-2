# Careers Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Attract top artisanal and design talent in Chandigarh.

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (Join the Atelier):** Inspiring imagery of artisans at work.
3. **Culture & Values:** A text-driven section detailing the respect for craft, work environment, and growth opportunities.
4. **Day in the Life (Gallery):** A small cluster of images showing the studio environment.
5. **Open Roles:** A clean, list-based presentation of available positions (e.g., Master Tailor, Hand Embroiderer, Client Concierge). Clickable rows that expand or lead to details.
6. **Spontaneous Application:** A section for talent to submit portfolios even if a role isn't listed.
7. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
