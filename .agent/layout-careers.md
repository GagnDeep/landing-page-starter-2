# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

## Structural Rhythm
Professional yet infused with the brand's passion for craftsmanship.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:** "Join Our Atelier"
2. **Culture & Values:** Why work with Designlane Heena?
3. **Life at the Boutique:** Image gallery of the workspace.
4. **Open Positions:** List or grid of available roles (e.g., Master Tailor, Sales Associate).
5. **Application Process:** How to apply and what to expect.
6. **General Inquiry:** "Don't see a fit? Send us your portfolio."
