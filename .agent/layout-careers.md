# Careers Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Careers Hero**: Inspiring header focused on joining a heritage family.
2. **Culture & Values**: A brief section on what it means to work at Verma's (respect, quality, tradition).
3. **Why Join Us?**: Benefits highlight (competitive pay, meals, family environment).
4. **Day in the Life**: A short narrative or image gallery showing the kitchen and storefront atmosphere.
5. **Current Openings Filter**: A way to sort jobs by 'Kitchen/Production', 'Front of House', 'Management'.
6. **Open Position List**: A clean, expanding list or grid of available jobs.
7. **Role Detail: Master Halwai**: Specific focus on recruiting skilled traditional sweet makers.
8. **Role Detail: Bakery Chef**: Focus on modern cake and pastry roles.
9. **Role Detail: Retail Staff**: Customer service and packaging roles.
10. **Apprenticeship Program**: Information for young chefs wanting to learn traditional methods.
11. **Application Form**: A straightforward form to upload a resume and cover letter.
12. **Interview Process Explanation**: What a candidate should expect after applying.
13. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement**: A commitment to fair hiring practices.
14. **Employee Testimonials**: Quotes from long-standing staff members.
15. **Location Requirements**: Clarifying if the role requires physical presence at specific branches.
16. **Seasonal Hiring Notice**: Information specifically for temporary festival staff.
17. **Future Opportunities (General Submission)**: A way to apply even if a specific role isn't listed.
18. **HR Contact Info**: Direct email for recruitment queries.
19. **Visual Break - Kitchen Action**: A dynamic image of the team at work.
20. **Back to Home CTA**: Easy navigation away from the page.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
