# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

**Goal:** Attract patient, professional driving instructors to join the Capital Driving School team.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* Standard global announcement.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* "Join Our Team" or "Teach the Next Generation of Safe Drivers".
4. **Why Work With Us**
   - *Intent:* Grid highlighting benefits (Competitive pay, flexible hours, modern fleet, respectful environment).
5. **Our Culture**
   - *Intent:* Emphasize the core values: patience and professionalism over rushing.
6. **Instructor Testimonials**
   - *Intent:* Quotes from current instructors about why they love working there.
7. **Open Positions List**
   - *Intent:* Dynamic list of current openings (e.g., Senior Instructor, Weekend Instructor, Customer Support).
8. **Job Detail Accordion/Modal**
   - *Intent:* Clickable roles that expand to show requirements and responsibilities.
9. **The Hiring Process**
   - *Intent:* Step-by-step guide (Application, Interview, Background Check, Training).
10. **Application Form**
    - *Intent:* Simple form to upload a resume and contact details.
11. **FAQ (Careers)**
    - *Intent:* Questions about required certifications or part-time availability.
12. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
