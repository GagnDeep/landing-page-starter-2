# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page (`/careers`)

*Intent: Attract top-tier talent who understand luxury service.*

## 1. Careers Hero
- **Intent**: Showcase the internal culture.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large hero image of the team working at an event.

## 2. Culture Statement
- **Intent**: What it means to work at Guide Events.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A single, powerful text column.

## 3. Benefits & Perks
- **Intent**: Why work here.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A simple 3-column grid.

## 4. Open Positions
- **Intent**: List available roles.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A clean list layout with hover effects that expand to show details.

## 5. Spontaneous Application
- **Intent**: Catch great talent even without open roles.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A subtle, elegant callout box.
