# Layout: Careers
**URL**: `/careers`
**Intent**: Attract top culinary and hospitality talent.

## Structure
1. **Hero**: Title: "Join Our Team".
2. **Culture Section**: Why work at Sangam Caterer? Highlighting growth and environment.
3. **Benefits**: A grid showing employee perks.
4. **Open Positions**: A clean list of current openings categorized by department (Kitchen, Service, Admin).
5. **Application Process**: Brief explanation of next steps.
6. **General Application**: An option to submit a resume even if no specific role fits.
