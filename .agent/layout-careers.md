# A2Zee Events - Careers Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To attract top-tier talent in hospitality, design, and production. Must convey the high standards and prestigious nature of the firm.

## Structural Sections
1.  **`careers-hero`:** "Join the Masters of Celebration."
2.  **`culture-overview`:** A section highlighting the demanding yet rewarding nature of the work.
3.  **`open-positions-list`:** A clean list or simple cards of current openings.
4.  **`benefits-grid`:** Highlighting perks, travel opportunities, and professional growth.
5.  **`general-application-cta`:** For when a specific role isn't listed.
