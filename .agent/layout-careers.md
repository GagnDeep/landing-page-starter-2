# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - Careers Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Solid header.

2. **Hero Section (Join the Team)**
   - *Layout intent:* An energetic but refined image of the team at work.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Inspiring typography.

3. **Culture & Values**
   - *Layout intent:* A 2-column text block explaining the work environment.
   - *Visual rhythm:* High readability, focus on the human element.

4. **Benefits & Perks**
   - *Layout intent:* A grid of icons and short text (e.g., travel opportunities, health, continuous learning).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Clean, scannable list.

5. **Open Roles Header**
   - *Layout intent:* A prominent title indicating current availability.
   - *Visual rhythm:* High contrast, clear division from the culture section.

6. **Job Listing 1 (e.g., Senior Planner)**
   - *Layout intent:* An accordion or card with the job title, location, and a "View Role" button.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Hover effects to indicate interactivity.

7. **Job Listing 2 (e.g., Design Assistant)**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent with Listing 1.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Clean separation between listings.

8. **General Application**
   - *Layout intent:* A block for people to submit their resume even if a specific role isn't open.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A distinct card, perhaps with a softer background (`--secondary`).

9. **Day in the Life (Gallery/Video)**
   - *Layout intent:* A small carousel or video showing what it's like to work at an event.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Engaging and dynamic.

10. **Global Footer**
    - *Layout intent:* Consistent footer.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Standard layout.
