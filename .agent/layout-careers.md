# Layout Intent: Careers Page

**Goal:** Attract top-tier talent by presenting the salon as an elite, supportive, and highly desirable place to work.

## 1. Hero: Join the Elite
- **Visual Rhythm:** Aspirational, dynamic image.
- **Layout:** Full-width hero image showing stylists collaborating or working passionately. Large, bold heading overlay.

## 2. Our Philosophy on Talent
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial manifesto.
- **Layout:** Centered, large serif text block explaining the standard of excellence expected and supported.

## 3. Why Work With Us (Benefits Grid)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Structured, icon-led grid.
- **Layout:** 2x2 or 3x2 grid detailing benefits (e.g., competitive pay, flexible hours, continuous education, premium tools).

## 4. The "At-Home" Stylist Life
- **Visual Rhythm:** Split feature.
- **Layout:** Image of a stylist on the go on one side, text explaining the independence and premium nature of the mobile service on the other.

## 5. The "In-Salon" Environment
- **Visual Rhythm:** Split feature (reverse).
- **Layout:** Text on the left, image of the beautiful salon interior on the right.

## 6. Continuous Education & Growth
- **Visual Rhythm:** Highlight block.
- **Layout:** A distinct section emphasizing training. Might include a pull quote from a senior stylist.

## 7. Current Openings Header
- **Visual Rhythm:** Clean section break.
- **Layout:** Simple, large heading indicating available roles.

## 8. Role 1 (e.g., Senior Colorist)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Expandable list item or card.
- **Layout:** Title of role, location (Mobile/Salon), and a button to view details or expand the job description.

## 9. Role 2 (e.g., Bridal Specialist)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Expandable list item.
- **Layout:** Matches established role item style.

## 10. Role 3 (e.g., Salon Coordinator)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Expandable list item.
- **Layout:** Matches established role item style.

## 11. What We Look For
- **Visual Rhythm:** Bulleted, clean list.
- **Layout:** A text block detailing soft skills and aesthetic alignment required, beyond just technical ability.

## 12. Visual Break: Team Culture
- **Visual Rhythm:** Candid photography.
- **Layout:** A large, warm image of the team.

## 13. Application Process
- **Visual Rhythm:** Timeline/Steps.
- **Layout:** A horizontal 3-step timeline (e.g., 1. Submit Portfolio, 2. Technical Interview, 3. Trial Run).

## 14. General Application Callout
- **Visual Rhythm:** Text block.
- **Layout:** Instructions on how to apply if a specific role isn't listed but they believe they are a fit.

## 15. Portfolio Requirements
- **Visual Rhythm:** Informational block.
- **Layout:** Clear instructions on what to include in the application (Instagram handles, specific types of work).

## 16. Testimonial from a Stylist
- **Visual Rhythm:** Quote block.
- **Layout:** Large serif quote emphasizing the supportive work environment.

## 17. Diversity & Inclusion Statement
- **Visual Rhythm:** Muted, respectful text.
- **Layout:** A centered paragraph affirming equal opportunity.

## 18. Contact for Hiring Inquiries
- **Visual Rhythm:** Direct link.
- **Layout:** An email link for specific HR/hiring questions.

## 19. Aesthetic Divider
- **Visual Rhythm:** Line art.
- **Layout:** Thin horizontal rule.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Standard dense typography.
- **Layout:** Consistent with global footer design.
