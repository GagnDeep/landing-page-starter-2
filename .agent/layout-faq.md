# Layout Intent: FAQ Page (`/faq`)

1. **Header:** Standard global nav.
2. **Hero:** Minimal, typography-centric. Title: "Questions & Clarity".
3. **Search Bar:** A prominent search input specifically for the FAQ.
4. **Category Navigation:** Sticky sidebar or top tabs (e.g., General, Logistics, NRI Specific, Financials).
5. **Section: General Planning:** Accordion group for basic questions (How far in advance should we book?).
6. **Section: NRI & Destination:** Accordion group addressing time zones, remote viewing of venues, etc.
7. **Section: Financials & Contracts:** Accordion group for payment schedules and cancellation policies.
8. **Section: Decor & Catering:** Accordion group covering tastings, custom menus, and decor mockups.
9. **Section: On the Day:** Accordion group explaining how the team handles the event day.
10. **The "Unasked" Questions:** A unique section addressing things clients don't know they should ask.
11. **Direct Contact Prompt:** "Didn't find your answer?" block.
12. **Video FAQ Teaser:** Thumbnails linking to short video explanations by the founder.
13. **Downloadable Guides:** Link to download a PDF "First Steps" guide.
14. **Client Portal Login:** A subtle link for existing clients to log in.
15. **CTA:** "Book a Discovery Call".
