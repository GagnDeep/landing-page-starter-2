# Layout Documentation: FAQ Page (`/faq`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
The FAQ page prioritizes clarity and ease of finding information. It uses a structured layout, primarily relying on clear typography and elegant accordion components, to provide answers without cluttering the screen.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`FAQ-01-GlobalHeader`**: Standard elegant navigation bar.
2.  **`FAQ-02-FAQHero`**: Simple title "Frequently Asked Questions" and a brief welcoming subtitle.
3.  **`FAQ-03-SearchInput`**: A prominent, elegantly styled search bar to quickly find specific answers.
4.  **`FAQ-04-CategorySidebar`**: A left-hand sticky sidebar listing categories (Ordering, Delivery, Dietary, Custom Cakes).
5.  **`FAQ-05-MainContentArea`**: The right-hand column where the questions and answers reside.
6.  **`FAQ-06-CategorySection`**: A block grouping related questions, starting with an elegant serif category header.
7.  **`FAQ-07-AccordionList`**: The container for the interactive question/answer blocks.
8.  **`FAQ-08-AccordionItem`**: The individual interactive component. Question is bold sans-serif; expands to reveal serif body text.
9.  **`FAQ-09-PopularQuestions`**: A highlighted section at the top of the content area for the most common inquiries.
10. **`FAQ-10-OrderingFAQ`**: Specific section dedicated to the logistics of placing an order.
11. **`FAQ-11-DeliveryFAQ`**: Specific section covering areas served, costs, and timing.
12. **`FAQ-12-StorageFAQ`**: Important section detailing how to store different baked goods for maximum freshness.
13. **`FAQ-13-AllergenFAQ`**: Prominent, highly visible section regarding nuts, gluten, and dairy cross-contamination.
14. **`FAQ-14-CancellationFAQ`**: Clear, unambiguous section detailing the refund and cancellation policy.
15. **`FAQ-15-StillHaveQuestions`**: A distinct block at the bottom for users who couldn't find their answer.
16. **`FAQ-16-ContactSupportCTA`**: Button within the 'Still Have Questions' block leading to the contact page.
17. **`FAQ-17-LiveChatPrompt`**: (Optional) A small banner indicating typical response times for emails or suggesting a phone call.
18. **`FAQ-18-FeedbackWidget`**: Small "Was this helpful?" thumbs up/down icons below answers.
19. **`FAQ-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Standard footer structure.
20. **`FAQ-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Standard legal footer strip.
