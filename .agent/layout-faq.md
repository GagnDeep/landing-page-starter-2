# FAQ Layout Blueprint

**Intent**: Provide clear, accessible answers to common questions without cluttering the main service pages. Maintain the elegant, helpful tone of a high-end concierge.

## Sections (20)
1. **Global Header**: Standard elegant navigation.
2. **Hero Section (Inquiries)**: "Guiding your event planning journey."
3. **Search the FAQ**: Large, prominent search input.
4. **Category Navigation**: Links to jump to sections (e.g., Menus, Logistics, Payments).
5. **Section 1: The Menu & Tasting**: Accordion group.
6. **Section 2: Service & Staffing**: Accordion group.
7. **Section 3: Logistics & Venues**: Accordion group.
8. **Section 4: Pricing & Contracts**: Accordion group.
9. **Section 5: Dietary & Allergies**: Accordion group.
10. **Highlighted Question**: A pulled-out "most asked" question designed as a feature block.
11. **The Tasting Process in Detail**: A step-by-step infographic or text flow.
12. **What to Expect on the Day**: A brief narrative of event-day operations.
13. **Partner Vendors**: Information on florists, decorators, etc.
14. **Sustainability Practices**: Brief FAQ on waste management and sourcing.
15. **Emergency Protocols**: Reassurance on backup plans and contingencies.
16. **Still Have Questions?**: Transition text to contact options.
17. **Direct Email Link**: "Concierge@oasiscaterers.com"
18. **Phone Support Details**: Operating hours and contact number.
19. **Primary Call to Action**: "Submit an Inquiry."
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
