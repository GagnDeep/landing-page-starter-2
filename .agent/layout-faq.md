# Layout Blueprint: FAQ

**Purpose**: Answer common questions quickly to reduce customer service load and increase conversion.
**Vibe**: Helpful, organized, concise.

## Sections
1. **Global Header**
2. **Hero: How Can We Help?**
   - Search bar prominently displayed.
3. **Quick Links / Categories**
   - Delivery, Orders, Custom Cakes, 24/7 Service.
4. **Top 5 Most Asked Questions**
   - Open by default or highly visible.
5. **Category: Ordering & Customization**
   - Accordion list.
6. **Category: Delivery & 24/7 Service**
   - Accordion list (focus on midnight policies).
7. **Category: Ingredients & Dietary**
   - Accordion list (Eggless options, allergies).
8. **Category: Payments & Cancellations**
   - Accordion list.
9. **Still Need Help?**
   - Contact box (Phone, WhatsApp, Email).
10. **Live Chat Teaser**
    - "We're online 24/7."
11. **Global Footer**
