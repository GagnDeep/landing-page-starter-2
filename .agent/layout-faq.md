# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo, primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `PageHeader`: Title area ("Frequently Asked Questions").
4.  `SearchFAQ`: Dedicated search bar to quickly find answers.
5.  `CategoryNav`: Jump links to different sections (General, Classes, Licensing, Pricing).
6.  `Section_General`: Accordion for general questions about the school.
7.  `Section_Classes`: Accordion for questions about training methods, automatic vs manual, timings.
8.  `Section_Licensing`: Accordion for questions regarding the driving test and getting a license in Chandigarh.
9.  `Section_Pricing`: Accordion for payment, refund, and package questions.
10. `Section_Vehicles`: Accordion for questions about the cars used and safety features.
11. `Section_Instructors`: Accordion for questions about instructor qualifications.
12. `Section_Location`: Accordion regarding the Burail location and pickup areas.
13. `StillHaveQuestions`: A prominent block bridging FAQs to direct contact.
14. `ContactForm_Mini`: A small inline form to ask a question directly.
15. `LiveChatTeaser`: (If applicable) Mention of WhatsApp or live chat support.
16. `HelpfulLinks`: Quick links to Pricing, Contact, or specific policy pages.
17. `DownloadableGuides`: Links to PDF guides (e.g., "Checklist for your Driving Test").
18. `VideoAnswers`: (Optional) Short video clips answering the top 3 most common questions.
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Call to action to book a class now that questions are answered.
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
