# FAQ Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Standard sticky navigation.
2. **FAQ Hero**: A simple, clean hero section focused on finding answers.
3. **Search Bar**: A prominent input to filter questions.
4. **Category Navigation**: Sticky sidebar or horizontal scroll of FAQ categories.
5. **Section: Booking & Availability**: Heading for the first category.
6. **Accordion Item 1**: How far in advance should we book?
7. **Accordion Item 2**: Do you travel outside Patiala?
8. **Accordion Item 3**: What is the deposit required?
9. **Section: Design & Floral Details**: Heading for the second category.
10. **Accordion Item 4**: Can we request specific seasonal flowers?
11. **Accordion Item 5**: Do you provide artificial flowers?
12. **Accordion Item 6**: Can we keep the flowers after the event?
13. **Section: Logistics & Setup**: Heading for the third category.
14. **Accordion Item 7**: How long does setup take?
15. **Accordion Item 8**: Do you handle the teardown?
16. **Accordion Item 9**: Are you insured for venue requirements?
17. **Still Have Questions?**: A friendly text block.
18. **Direct Contact Links**: Email and phone number presented clearly.
19. **Call to Action Banner**: "Let's discuss your specific needs."
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
