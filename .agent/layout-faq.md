# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page

**Goal:** Provide quick, clear answers to common questions, reducing friction and customer service inquiries.

## Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - *Intent:* Standard global announcement.
2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - *Intent:* Standard sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**
   - *Intent:* "Frequently Asked Questions" heading with a search bar.
4. **Category Navigation**
   - *Intent:* Jump links to specific FAQ sections (e.g., "Getting Started", "Pricing", "The Lessons").
5. **Category 1: Getting Started**
   - *Intent:* Accordion list for questions like "Do I need a learner's license?".
6. **Category 2: Packages & Pricing**
   - *Intent:* Accordion list for payment, refunds, and package selection.
7. **Category 3: The Lessons & Schedule**
   - *Intent:* Accordion list for timing, cancellations, and the learning process.
8. **Category 4: RTO & Licensing**
   - *Intent:* Accordion list detailing the test process and paperwork assistance.
9. **Category 5: Vehicle & Safety**
   - *Intent:* Accordion list regarding the cars, dual controls, and safety measures.
10. **Still Have Questions? (Contact CTA)**
    - *Intent:* Direct link to WhatsApp, phone, or the contact page.
11. **Quick Links**
    - *Intent:* Links to Pricing, About, or specific features.
12. **Testimonial Block**
    - *Intent:* Reassurance near the bottom of the page.
13. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Standard footer.
