# Layout Intent: FAQ Page

The FAQ page must remain elegant while delivering dense, practical information. It should alleviate anxieties for high-net-worth clients planning complex events.

## Structural Map

1.  **`GlobalNavigation`** (Persistent)
2.  **`FAQHero`**
    *   **Intent:** A simple, calming header. "Questions & Guidance".
    *   **Rhythm:** Minimalist, relying on typography.
3.  **`CategoryJumpLinks`**
    *   **Intent:** Quick navigation to specific topics (Logistics, Menus, Venues, Payments).
    *   **Rhythm:** A horizontal row of pill-shaped buttons or simple text links.
4.  **`AccordionSection: Culinary & Menus`**
    *   **Intent:** Questions regarding tastings, dietary requirements, and sourcing.
    *   **Rhythm:** A sleek, expanding accordion list. When closed, only the question is visible in a bold sans-serif. When open, the answer reveals with a smooth, sweeping animation.
5.  **`AccordionSection: Destinations & Logistics`**
    *   **Intent:** Questions regarding travel, staff accommodation, and equipment shipping.
    *   **Rhythm:** Continuation of the accordion pattern.
6.  **`AccordionSection: Booking & Financials`**
    *   **Intent:** Questions regarding deposits, cancellation policies, and minimum spends.
    *   **Rhythm:** Continuation of the accordion pattern.
7.  **`StillNeedHelp`**
    *   **Intent:** A gentle prompt to contact the team if a question isn't answered.
    *   **Rhythm:** A small, centered block of text with an elegant link to the contact page.
8.  **`GlobalFooter`** (Persistent)
