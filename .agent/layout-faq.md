# FAQ Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
To provide clear, authoritative answers to common questions without cluttering the main narrative pages. The design should be strictly functional but maintain the luxury aesthetic.

## Sections

1.  **Hero: Inquiries & Information**
    *   *Intent:* Clear page title.
    *   *Visuals:* Very minimal. A stark, clean header against a muted background.

2.  **Category Navigation (Optional)**
    *   *Intent:* Help users jump to relevant sections (e.g., Booking, Logistics, Menu).
    *   *Visuals:* A simple sticky sidebar or a horizontal jump-link menu.

3.  **The Accordions (The core content)**
    *   *Intent:* Present information densely but cleanly.
    *   *Visuals:* A series of elegantly styled accordion components. The borders should be very thin, and the typography should ensure high readability. Use smooth, subtle animations for the expand/collapse action.

4.  **Still Have Questions?**
    *   *Intent:* Catch users whose questions weren't answered.
    *   *Visuals:* A small, centered block with a direct link to the contact page or a specific email address.
