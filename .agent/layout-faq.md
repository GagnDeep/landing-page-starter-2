# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - FAQ Page

1. **Global Header**
   - *Layout intent:* Consistent navigation.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Solid header.

2. **Hero Section (Questions & Curiosities)**
   - *Layout intent:* A simple, text-focused hero. No massive background image required, just clean typography.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Minimalist and calming.

3. **Search Bar**
   - *Layout intent:* A large, centrally placed input field to search questions.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Elegant input styling, subtle focus ring (`--ring`).

4. **Category Jump Links**
   - *Layout intent:* Links to scroll down to specific sections (e.g., Logistics, Pricing, Destinations).
   - *Visual rhythm:* A horizontal list or sidebar on larger screens.

5. **Section 1: The Planning Process**
   - *Layout intent:* An accordion group for process-related questions.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Distinct section header followed by clean accordion items.

6. **Section 2: Destinations & Travel**
   - *Layout intent:* An accordion group for travel/location questions.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Section 1.

7. **Section 3: Budget & Investment**
   - *Layout intent:* An accordion group for financial questions.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Section 1.

8. **Section 4: Vendor Relations**
   - *Layout intent:* An accordion group for questions about florists, photographers, etc.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Consistent with Section 1.

9. **Still Have Questions? (Contact Teaser)**
   - *Layout intent:* A small card or text block offering direct contact.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Placed alongside or at the end of the lists, drawing the eye with a distinct background (`--muted`).

10. **Global Footer**
    - *Layout intent:* Consistent footer.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Standard layout.
