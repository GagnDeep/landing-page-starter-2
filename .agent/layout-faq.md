# FAQ Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **FAQ Hero/Search**: A clean header with a prominent search bar to find answers quickly.
2. **Category Navigation**: Quick links (e.g., Shipping, Orders, Ingredients, Events) that smooth-scroll to sections.
3. **Ordering & Payments**: Accordion section covering how to order, modify, or pay.
4. **Shipping & Delivery (Local)**: Accordion for local logistics, times, and zones.
5. **Shipping (National)**: Accordion for out-of-state shipping rules and timelines.
6. **Product Shelf Life & Storage**: Crucial information on how to keep sweets fresh.
7. **Ingredients & Allergens**: Detailed answers about ghee, nuts, vegan options, and gluten.
8. **Bulk & Wedding Orders**: Specific questions regarding lead times, tastings, and customization.
9. **Cancellations & Refunds**: Clear policy outline for different types of orders.
10. **Packaging Details**: Questions about what materials are used and if they are travel-safe.
11. **Corporate Gifting FAQ**: Invoicing, multi-address shipping, and branding options.
12. **Festival Rush Protocol**: Explaining how the store operates during peak times (e.g., Diwali).
13. **Loyalty & Accounts**: How to manage passwords, points, and saved addresses.
14. **Custom Cakes**: Specific rules for bakery side (fondant vs cream, sizing).
15. **In-Store Experience**: Questions about parking, hours, and pre-ordering for pickup.
16. **Still Have Questions? (Contact Teaser)**: A soft CTA leading to the contact page.
17. **Live Chat/WhatsApp Prompt**: A floating or inline button for immediate assistance.
18. **Downloadable Guides**: Links to PDF menus or storage instruction sheets.
19. **Recent Updates/Notices**: A small bulletin for temporary policy changes (e.g., weather delays).
20. **Return to Shop CTA**: A clear button to go back to the catalog.
21. **Standard Footer**: Consistent site footer.
