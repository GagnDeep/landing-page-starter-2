# FAQ Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Provide clear, comforting answers to potential clients about the bespoke process, shipping, and returns.

1. **Header (Global):** Same as home.
2. **Hero Section (Client Care):** "How may we assist you?" Minimalist, airy header.
3. **Category Jump Links:** Elegant links to jump to specific sections (e.g., "Sizing", "Shipping", "Returns").
4. **FAQ Section 1 (The Bespoke Process):** An accordion-style list. The design of the accordion must be refined (e.g., just a subtle line separator and a delicate '+' icon).
5. **FAQ Section 2 (Shipping & Delivery):** Continuing the accordion format.
6. **FAQ Section 3 (Returns & Alterations):** Continuing the accordion format.
7. **Still Need Help?:** A visually distinct section encouraging direct contact if the question isn't answered.
8. **Footer (Global):** Same as home.
