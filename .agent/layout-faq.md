# Layout: FAQ Page

A functional, easy-to-navigate page addressing common questions to save time for both the client and the studio.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Simple, text-focused hero. Title: "Frequently Asked Questions".
2.  **Category Navigation:** Jump links to specific sections (e.g., Booking, Logistics, Deliverables).
3.  **FAQ Accordions:** Divided into logical sections.
    - **Booking & Investment:** Retainers, payment plans, securing dates.
    - **Logistics & Travel:** NRI considerations, destination weddings, team size.
    - **The Process:** Timeline, shot lists, family formals.
    - **Deliverables:** Editing timelines, raw files policy, album design.
4.  **Still have questions?:** A friendly closing block directing to the Contact page.
