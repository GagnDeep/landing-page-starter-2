# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page (`/faq`)

## Goal
To reduce customer service overhead by answering common questions regarding licensing, training formats, and pricing, acting as a final hurdle-clearer before booking.

## Section Architecture (15-20 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Standard global component.
2.  **Hero Section:** "Frequently Asked Questions." Clear, supportive subheadline.
3.  **Search Bar:** To quickly find specific questions (e.g., "license").
4.  **Category Navigation (Sticky/Sidebar or Tabs):** Jump links to sections (Licensing, Classes, Pricing, Vehicles).
5.  **Category: Getting Started & Licensing:** Accordions covering learner's license requirements, RTO process.
6.  **Category: The Training Process:** Accordions covering lesson duration, pick-up/drop-off, instructor assignments.
7.  **Category: Vehicles & Safety:** Accordions about dual-controls, insurance, car models.
8.  **Category: Pricing & Payments:** Accordions on refunds, installments, hidden fees.
9.  **Category: Post-Training (The Test):** Accordions on how the final RTO test works and the school's involvement.
10. **"Still have questions?" Block:** A prominent, friendly block for questions not covered.
11. **Contact Information Summary:** Quick display of phone, WhatsApp, email.
12. **Location/Map Refresher:** Reminder of the Sector 47 location.
13. **Ask a Question Form:** A simple form specifically to submit a new question.
14. **Final CTA:** "Clear on the details? Book your first lesson."
15. **Global Footer:** Standard global component.
