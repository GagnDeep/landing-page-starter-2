# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page

## Intent
To proactively answer common questions, reducing friction in the booking process and saving time for the customer service team.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Standard site header.
2. **Hero Header:** "Frequently Asked Questions" with a supportive subheadline ("Everything you need to know before your big day").
3. **Search Bar:** A prominent search input to filter questions quickly.
4. **Quick Links/Jump Menu:** Links to skip to specific categories (Booking, Menu, Logistics).
5. **Category 1: Booking & Payments:** Section header.
6. **FAQ 1.1:** Accordion item.
7. **FAQ 1.2:** Accordion item.
8. **FAQ 1.3:** Accordion item.
9. **FAQ 1.4:** Accordion item.

16. **FAQ 3.1:** Accordion item.
17. **FAQ 3.2:** Accordion item.
18. **FAQ 3.3:** Accordion item.
19. **Still Have Questions? Banner:** A visually distinct section.
20. **Direct Contact Methods:** Icons and links for Phone, WhatsApp, and Email.
21. **Footer:** Standard site footer.

## Visual Rhythm
Function over form, but elegantly styled. Relies heavily on the Shadcn Accordion component for a clean, expanding/collapsing list that prevents information overload, utilizing generous whitespace and clear typography.
