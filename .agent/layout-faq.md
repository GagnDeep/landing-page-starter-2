# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page (`/faq`)

*Intent: Answer common questions gracefully and efficiently.*

## 1. FAQ Hero
- **Intent**: Clear page purpose.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Extremely minimal, text-only header.

## 2. Category Anchors
- **Intent**: Quick jump to specific sections (Process, Pricing, Destinations).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Sticky sidebar or top horizontal menu.

## 3. Accordion Section 1: The Process
- **Intent**: Questions about how things work.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Clean lines, smooth slow expansion. No harsh boxes.

## 4. Accordion Section 2: Destinations & Travel
- **Intent**: Questions about logistics.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Continuation of previous style.

## 5. Accordion Section 3: Investment
- **Intent**: Financial questions.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Continuation of previous style.

## 6. Still Have Questions?
- **Intent**: Catch-all leading to contact.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Centered, small text block with an elegant link.
