# Layout Documentation: FAQ Page

## Intent
Provide clear, easily accessible answers to common questions, particularly focusing on the logistics for NRI clients.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Simple, text-focused. "Frequently Asked Questions."
2. **Search/Filter:** A very clean, minimal search bar.
3. **Categorized Accordions:** Group questions logically (e.g., "The Process," "Pricing & Payment," "International Orders," "Care & Maintenance"). The accordions should animate smoothly and have subtle border lines.
4. **Contact CTA:** "Still have questions? Reach out to our master tailor."
