# Homepage Layout Map

## 1. Hero (`hero`)
**Intent:** Immediately captivate the user with an immersive, full-bleed visual of a stunning event space and establish the brand's luxury positioning.
**Rhythm:** Full-screen (`min-h-svh`), heavy emphasis on high-quality background imagery or video loop, central or offset elegant typography (H1), minimal UI interference, subtle scroll-down indicator.

## 2. Introduction/Philosophy (`intro`)
**Intent:** Set the tone and state the core philosophy of "exceeding expectations."
**Rhythm:** Generous top and bottom padding (`py-32`), large central typography emphasizing the brand's mission, significant white space, slow fade-in on scroll.

## 3. The Experience Overview (`experience-overview`)
**Intent:** Broadly introduce the catering experience—taste, service, and atmosphere.
**Rhythm:** Asymmetric 2-column layout; overlapping image block on one side and a refined text block on the other to create editorial flair.

## 4. Signature Services Grid (`services-grid`)
**Intent:** Highlight the 3-4 core catering services (e.g., Weddings, Corporate, Private Dining).
**Rhythm:** Elegant grid (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3`); each card features a tall aspect ratio image, subtle hover effect (slow zoom), and minimal typography below.

## 5. Featured Venue Highlight (`venue-highlight`)
**Intent:** Showcase a premier partner venue or typical luxury setting.
**Rhythm:** Full-width image break, parallax effect on the background, text box overlaid with a slight frosted glass or solid color block.

## 6. Culinary Artistry (`culinary-artistry`)
**Intent:** Focus strictly on the food—presentation, sourcing, and chef expertise.
**Rhythm:** Split layout; dominant, high-resolution close-up of a plated dish juxtaposed with a poetic description and a link to the menu/features.

## 7. The Process (`process`)
**Intent:** Outline the bespoke planning and tasting process.
**Rhythm:** Vertical or horizontal timeline, minimalist icons or numbers, alternating text placement to keep the eye moving down the page.

## 8. Testimonial Carousel (`testimonials`)
**Intent:** Build trust through words of high-profile past clients.
**Rhythm:** Clean, typography-focused slider. Large quote marks, elegant serif italics for the quote, and a simple attribution. No bulky card borders.

## 9. Visual Gallery Preview (`gallery-preview`)
**Intent:** A visual feast showing the breadth of their events.
**Rhythm:** Masonry layout or a dynamic, interlocking grid of 5-7 images of varying sizes. Images reveal via slow masking on scroll.

## 10. The Team / Chef Spotlight (`team-spotlight`)
**Intent:** Personalize the brand by highlighting the head chef or lead planner.
**Rhythm:** Portrait-style image on the left, bio and philosophy on the right. High contrast, possibly utilizing a secondary background color.

## 11. Custom Menu Curation (`menu-curation`)
**Intent:** Emphasize that every menu is unique and tailored.
**Rhythm:** Centralized text block flanked by two floating, smaller imagery slots. Subtle floating animation for the images.

## 12. Sustainability & Sourcing (`sourcing`)
**Intent:** Communicate modern luxury values—local, sustainable, premium ingredients.
**Rhythm:** Full-width section with a muted background color. 3-column icon and text layout, very spacious.

## 13. Press & Recognition (`press`)
**Intent:** Showcase awards, magazine features, or high-end partnerships.
**Rhythm:** A simple, horizontal band of monochromatic logos. Opacity at 50%, changing to 100% on hover.

## 14. Event Enhancements (`enhancements`)
**Intent:** Tease additional services like mixology, floral partnerships, or styling.
**Rhythm:** Accordion or interactive tab layout. Clicking a tab elegantly crossfades the adjacent large image.

## 15. The "Exceeds Promise" Guarantee (`guarantee`)
**Intent:** Reinforce the core value proposition one last time before the main CTA.
**Rhythm:** Dark mode block (even in light theme) for stark contrast. Large serif text, gold/accent color highlights.

## 16. Recent Journal/Blog Entries (`journal-preview`)
**Intent:** Show active, ongoing expertise and recent stunning events.
**Rhythm:** 3-column grid of "cards," but styled like magazine covers—image top, date, serif title, and a discrete "read more" line.

## 17. FAQ Teaser (`faq-teaser`)
**Intent:** Address immediate logistical questions for high-end clients.
**Rhythm:** 2-column layout: heading and context on the left, 3-4 key accordion items on the right.

## 18. Pre-Footer CTA (`pre-footer-cta`)
**Intent:** The primary conversion point to book a consultation or tasting.
**Rhythm:** Massive text, full width. Often a solid primary color background or a very dark, moody image overlay. Single, prominent button.

## 19. Newsletter/Exclusive List (`newsletter`)
**Intent:** Capture emails for those not yet ready to book.
**Rhythm:** Minimalist input field with a bottom border only, elegant submit arrow. Centered within a spacious block.

## 20. Global Footer (`footer`)
**Intent:** Provide navigation, legal links, and contact details.
**Rhythm:** Clean grid structure. Top separator line. Small, readable sans-serif text. Social icons aligned right.
