# Home Page Layout Map

**Goal:** Establish Boho Salon as the premier destination for high-end beauty, chrome gel nails, and bridal-NRI services in Sector 17, Chandigarh. Must feel like flipping through a luxury editorial magazine.

## Section 1: Top Announcement Bar
- **Visuals:** Thin, muted primary background with contrasting light text.
- **Content:** Scrolling or static text highlighting current offers (e.g., "Exclusive Bridal Packages | Book Early for Season") or location.

## Section 2: Main Navigation Header
- **Visuals:** Transparent background initially, turning solid white/cream on scroll. Elegant, spaced-out sans-serif links.
- **Structure:** Logo center (or left), navigation links, and a prominent "Book Appointment" CTA button on the right.

## Section 3: Hero Video/Image Header
- **Visuals:** Full-bleed, high-quality, slow-motion ambient video or carousel of stunning nail art and salon ambiance.
- **Structure:** Large serif headline overlay (e.g., "The Art of Elegance"), subtle subheading, primary CTA to services.

## Section 4: Brand Philosophy / Introduction
- **Visuals:** Large, airy spacing. Soft background color (e.g., muted sage).
- **Structure:** Centered serif quote or statement about the boho-modern ethos. "More than a salon, a sanctuary."

## Section 5: Signature Services Highlights (Grid/Carousel)
- **Visuals:** Masonry grid or smooth carousel. High-res imagery focusing on textures (chrome nails, curly hair).
- **Structure:** Cards for Nails, Hair, Bridal, Skin. Hover effects reveal "View Details."

## Section 6: The Chrome Gel Nail Specialty
- **Visuals:** Split screen. Left: Close-up macro shot of chrome nails. Right: Editorial text block.
- **Structure:** Highlighting the specific expertise in advanced nail art. Secondary CTA "Explore Nail Menu."

## Section 7: Curly Hair Advanced Care
- **Visuals:** Split screen, reversed layout from Section 6.
- **Structure:** Focus on specialized curly haircuts. "Embrace your natural texture."

## Section 8: The Bridal & NRI Experience
- **Visuals:** Full-width parallax image of a bridal preparation or finished look.
- **Structure:** Overlay text box detailing the bespoke, stress-free NRI/Bridal packages.

## Section 9: Featured Artists / Stylists
- **Visuals:** Circular or arched portrait frames.
- **Structure:** Meet the master stylists. Name, specialty, and a link to their portfolio/booking.

## Section 10: Client Testimonials / "Love Notes"
- **Visuals:** A clean, horizontal scrolling marquee or fading carousel of text.
- **Structure:** Short, impactful quotes from high-profile or regular clients.

## Section 11: Image Gallery / Lookbook (Row 1)
- **Visuals:** Asymmetrical grid of latest salon work.
- **Structure:** Purely visual inspiration. Click to enlarge.

## Section 12: Image Gallery / Lookbook (Row 2)
- **Visuals:** Continuation of the grid, perhaps featuring behind-the-scenes ambiance.
- **Structure:** "Follow us on Instagram" CTA interspersed.

## Section 13: Product Lines Used (Partners)
- **Visuals:** Monochome logos of premium brands used (e.g., Olaplex, Dyson, premium nail gels).
- **Structure:** Simple horizontal strip. "Crafted with the best."

## Section 14: Promotional Banner / Special Offer
- **Visuals:** Deep, rich background (dark mode color) with gold/light accents.
- **Structure:** Time-sensitive offer or seasonal package highlight.

## Section 15: The Sector 17 Location (Visit Us)
- **Visuals:** Split layout. Stylized map or photo of the salon exterior/interior on one side.
- **Structure:** Address details, opening hours, and a "Get Directions" button.

## Section 16: Newsletter / VIP List Signup
- **Visuals:** Soft terracotta background. Minimalist input field.
- **Structure:** "Join the Boho List" for exclusive invites and tips.

## Section 17: Latest from the Blog / Journal
- **Visuals:** 3-column grid of recent articles.
- **Structure:** Thumbnails, titles (e.g., "Summer Nail Trends"), and "Read More" links.

## Section 18: FAQ Snippets
- **Visuals:** Clean accordion style.
- **Structure:** Top 3-4 most asked questions (e.g., "Do I need an appointment for nails?"). Link to full FAQ.

## Section 19: Pre-Footer Instagram Feed
- **Visuals:** Full-width strip of 5-6 square Instagram images. No gaps.
- **Structure:** Hover reveals Instagram handle `@bohosalon.chd`.

## Section 20: Comprehensive Footer
- **Visuals:** Dark background. Organized columns.
- **Structure:** Logo, quick links, contact info, social icons, newsletter secondary signup, copyright, and legal links.
