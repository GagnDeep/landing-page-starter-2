# Layout Intent: Home Page

1. **Global Navigation (Header):** Sticky header, dark charcoal background fading in on scroll, containing logo (left) and clean primary navigation (right) with a CTA button for booking.
2. **Hero Section (The Arrival):** Full-screen video/image background of a grand luxury wedding setup. High-contrast serif headline centered. Secondary CTA to explore menus.
3. **Brand Promise (The Ethos):** Clean, soft cream background. A central large typography statement about pure vegetarian luxury catering. Minimalist, breathing space.
4. **Signature Experiences (Marquee/Grid):** A staggered 3-column grid showing off specific culinary setups (e.g., Live Counters, Traditional Sit-down, Global Fusion). Image-heavy with subtle text overlays.
5. **Cultural Heritage (Story Snippet):** Asymmetrical layout. Large image on the left showing traditional cooking/prep. Right side text explaining the Patiala heritage and NRI trust.
6. **Statistic/Scale Indicator (The Numbers):** Dark mode strip. Three key metrics (e.g., weddings catered, years of heritage, guest capacity) displayed with large serif numbers and small sans-serif labels.
7. **Featured Menu Spotlight (The Taste):** A horizontal scrolling section or carousel showcasing signature dishes. Each item features a rich, high-quality image with a minimal caption and price/tier hint.
8. **Testimonial 1 (The Client Voice):** Large serif pull-quote on a muted sage background. Minimal attribution. Emphasizes luxury and flawless execution.
9. **Service Tiers (The Offerings):** 3-card layout (Standard, Premium, Maharaja). Subtle borders, soft shadows. Highlighting the differences in scale and service.
10. **Visual Interlude (The Ambiance):** Parallax image of a beautifully lit dining hall. No text, purely atmospheric to establish mood.
11. **Event Types (The Occasions):** Grid of 4 soft-edged image cards (Engagement, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception). Hover effects reveal short descriptions.
12. **The Culinary Team (The Masters):** A clean, editorial-style section introducing the head chefs/directors. Black and white or desaturated imagery for a timeless feel.
13. **Process/Journey (The Execution):** A vertical timeline or step-by-step layout detailing the booking to execution phase (Consultation, Tasting, Planning, The Big Day).
14. **Testimonial 2 (The NRI Perspective):** Another pull-quote, this time focusing on trust and long-distance coordination. Different visual treatment than Testimonial 1 (e.g., image accompanying the quote).
15. **Sustainability/Purity (The Commitment):** A text-heavy section with a subtle background texture (warm sand). Discussing sourcing of ingredients, pure veg commitment, and hygiene standards.
16. **Gallery Teaser (The Visual Proof):** A masonry grid of 5-6 high-quality images showcasing varied setups and details. A "View Full Gallery" button at the bottom.
17. **FAQ Teaser (The Assurances):** Accordion style. 3-4 of the most common questions (e.g., "Do you travel outside Patiala?"). Clean, minimal borders.
18. **Final CTA (The Invitation):** Deep charcoal background, rich gold text. A compelling call to book a consultation or tasting. Large primary button.
19. **Newsletter/Updates (The Connection):** Simple input field on a muted background. Clean typography encouraging sign-ups for seasonal menus or wedding tips.
20. **Global Footer (The Anchor):** Comprehensive footer. Logo, multi-column links (About, Menus, Legal, Careers), contact information (address, phone, email), and social icons. Very structured and organized.
