# Layout Documentation: Home Page (`/`)

## Structural Intent & Visual Rhythm
The Home Page acts as the grand entrance to the heritage bakery. It should utilize generous vertical rhythms, large high-quality imagery, and alternating asymmetrical content blocks to convey an editorial, high-end, and bespoke feel.

## Sections (20 Named Layout Blocks)
1.  **`Home-01-GlobalAnnouncementBar`**: Thin, high-contrast banner at the top for seasonal announcements or shipping updates.
2.  **`Home-02-HeritageHeader`**: Main navigation with centered logo (Playfair Display), elegant links, and subtle hover states.
3.  **`Home-03-HeroCinematic`**: Full-bleed hero section. Soft fade-in motion. Focus on a stunning, warm image of a fresh bake.
4.  **`Home-04-HeroTypographyOverlay`**: Absolute positioned text block within the hero. Large serif headline, brief sans-serif subhead, primary CTA button.
5.  **`Home-05-BrandTrustBanner`**: Horizontal strip below hero. Subtle logos of local press or a short heritage statement (e.g., "Baking in Patiala since 1985").
6.  **`Home-06-IntroEditorialBlock`**: Two-column asymmetrical grid. One side large serif text detailing the family history, the other a portrait-oriented image.
7.  **`Home-07-SignatureSweetsShowcase`**: Full-width section with a muted background. Carousel or grid of the top 3-4 signature items (e.g., Swiss Rolls, Biscoff).
8.  **`Home-08-SignatureItemCard`**: Reusable component layout for the showcase. Image top, elegant title below, brief description, "Order" link.
9.  **`Home-09-BespokeCakesParallax`**: Large banner section with subtle parallax background image of cake frosting or decoration.
10. **`Home-10-BespokeCakesContent`**: Floating card over the parallax background detailing custom wedding and celebration cakes.
11. **`Home-11-ProcessTimeline`**: Vertical or horizontal step-by-step layout showing the artisanal baking process (Mix, Bake, Decorate).
12. **`Home-12-TestimonialSlider`**: Clean, centered typography section featuring rotating quotes from loyal customers.
13. **`Home-13-LocalIngredientsHighlight`**: Split screen (50/50). Left: rustic image of wheat/jaggery. Right: text on sourcing local ingredients.
14. **`Home-14-FeaturedCollectionGrid`**: A bento-box style grid showing different categories (Breads, Pastries, Custom Cakes, Savories).
15. **`Home-15-VisitUsBlock`**: Centered block with map graphic or photo of the storefront, address details, and opening hours.
16. **`Home-16-InstagramFeedGallery`**: A tight grid of 4-6 square images highlighting recent bakes, conveying community and freshness.
17. **`Home-17-NewsletterSignupPrompt`**: Soft background section. Centered text inviting users to join the "Bakery Club" for exclusive updates.
18. **`Home-18-NewsletterInputForm`**: Inline form layout (input + button) matching the brand's soft radii and border colors.
19. **`Home-19-GlobalFooterMain`**: Multi-column footer. Brand summary, quick links, contact info, and social icons.
20. **`Home-20-GlobalFooterLegal`**: Bottom-most strip. Copyright text, link to Terms and Privacy Policy. Minimalist sans-serif.
