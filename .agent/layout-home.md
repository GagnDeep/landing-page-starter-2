# Layout: Home Page

## Structural Blueprint (High Conversion & WhatsApp-First)

1. **Top Announcement Bar**
   - Subtle banner for current lead time or special offers (e.g., "Bespoke tailoring lead time: 2 weeks. Book early for weddings.").

2. **Global Navigation (Header)**
   - Sticky header. Logo center. Hamburger menu left (mobile). Quick WhatsApp CTA right ("Book Consultation").

3. **Hero Section (First Impression)**
   - Full-bleed hero image/video (editorial style of a finished garment or tailoring process).
   - High-contrast serif headline (e.g., "Master Tailors of Patiala").
   - Primary CTA: "Design Your Pathani" (Links to WhatsApp).
   - Secondary CTA: "Explore Our Craft" (Scrolls to About).

4. **Trust Bar (Social Proof)**
   - Minimalist logos or text indicating "5.0-Star Rated", "Locally Trusted", "Decades of Heritage".

5. **Signature Services (Grid)**
   - 3-column grid highlighting core offerings:
     - Bespoke Pathani Kurtas
     - Nehru Jackets
     - Custom Trousers/Shirts

6. **The Purba Difference (Value Proposition)**
   - Editorial layout (text left, image right). Highlighting the artisanal process, hand-selected fabrics, and attention to detail.

7. **Featured Fabric Collections (Carousel)**
   - High-quality fabric swatches with names and composition (e.g., "Irish Linen", "Pure Silk").

8. **WhatsApp Ordering Process (Step-by-Step)**
   - Clear, simple 3-step guide: 1. Consult, 2. Measure (in-store or guide), 3. Craft & Deliver.

9. **Customer Testimonials (Carousel)**
   - Elegant quote cards highlighting 5-star reviews and specific positive experiences.

10. **Bespoke Gallery (Masonry Grid)**
    - High-end photography of finished garments on clients or mannequins.

11. **Video/Process Highlight**
    - A short, auto-playing (muted) video showing the cutting or sewing process to emphasize craftsmanship.

12. **Gifting & Occasions (Banner)**
    - Section promoting gift cards or special wedding packages.

13. **Featured In / Press (Optional)**
    - Any local press or notable mentions to build authority.

14. **In-Store Experience (Location Highlight)**
    - Photo of the physical shop in Patiala, address, and an invitation to visit for a fitting.

15. **FAQ Snippet (Accordion)**
    - Top 3 frequently asked questions (e.g., "Do you ship internationally?", "How long does it take?").

16. **Newsletter/Community Opt-in**
    - "Join our inner circle for exclusive fabric releases and tailoring tips."

17. **Final High-Impact CTA**
    - Large, centered section: "Ready for your perfect fit?" with a prominent WhatsApp button.

18. **Global Footer (Navigation Links)**
    - Links to all secondary pages (About, Services, Contact, Legal).

19. **Global Footer (Contact & Socials)**
    - Phone number, email, physical address, and elegant social media icons.

20. **Global Footer (Legal & Copyright)**
    - Copyright notice, Terms, Privacy Policy.
