# Home Page Layout Structure

The Home page aims to act as a digital storefront, drawing visitors into the world of Kohinoor Trends.
Below are 20 distinct sections designed to pace the user journey beautifully from top to bottom.

1. **Header / Navigation Bar**: Fixed at the top, transparent transitioning to solid on scroll. Elegant serif logo, clean navigation links, and a subtle "Book Consultation" primary action.
2. **Hero Section**: Full viewport height. A breathtaking, high-quality video or image cross-fade of bridal wear, overlayed with a sophisticated serif headline ("Elegance Woven in Tradition") and a delicate call-to-action.
3. **Introduction / Brand Promise**: A generous white-space area featuring a short, poetic paragraph about Kohinoor Trends' legacy since 1998.
4. **Heritage-Timeline Teaser**: A horizontally scrolling or step-by-step visual snapshot of the boutique's journey over the decades.
5. **Bridal-Spotlight (Lehengas)**: Large, high-resolution imagery showcasing bridal lehengas, with an asymmetrical layout and slow-scale hover effects.
6. **Groom-Matching (Sherwanis)**: Complementary section highlighting groom attire, emphasizing the synergy between bride and groom collections.
7. **Daily Wear / Cotton Suits Grid**: A 3-column elegant grid showcasing the everyday premium cotton suits, focusing on textures and colors.
8. **Featured Collections Carousel**: A graceful, slow-moving carousel of the current season's highlighted collections.
9. **The Fabric of Us (Craftsmanship)**: A dark-themed, immersive section focusing on the close-up details of embroidery, Zari work, and silk textures.
10. **Testimonial-Grid (Generations of Brides)**: A refined masonry or grid layout of quotes and photos from real brides, establishing deep trust.
11. **Boutique Experience / Virtual Tour**: A wide banner inviting users to experience the physical Chandigarh boutique, featuring a parallax interior shot.
12. **Stylist Consultation Call-out**: A muted-background section with a warm invitation to book a personal styling appointment.
13. **Trending Now**: A curated list of 4-6 currently trending pieces, presented in elegant, tall portrait cards.
14. **Behind the Seams (Artisan Spotlight)**: A split layout (image/text) honoring the weavers and artisans who create the garments.
15. **Celebrity / Influencer Lookbook**: A subtle ribbon or gallery showing notable figures wearing Kohinoor Trends.
16. **Gifting & Trousseau**: A specialized section highlighting bridal trousseau packaging and premium gifting options.
17. **FAQ Teaser**: An accordion with 3-4 of the most common questions (e.g., shipping, custom sizing), styled cleanly to avoid visual clutter.
18. **Newsletter / Insider Access**: A sophisticated input form to join the mailing list for exclusive previews and heritage stories.
19. **Instagram Feed Gallery**: A seamless, borderless grid of the latest social media posts, blending organic reach with the site's aesthetic.
20. **Footer**: Expansive, multi-column footer containing deep links, legal pages, contact details, and a final, elegant brand sign-off.
