# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

1.  `TopBar`: Mini notification bar for immediate contact info (Phone/Email) & 6AM-11PM availability.
2.  `MainNavigation`: Sticky header with logo (Azad Driving School), primary links, and a high-contrast "Book a Class" CTA.
3.  `HeroSection`: Immersive background with a strong value proposition, primary "Start Learning" CTA, and a secondary "View Pricing" CTA.
4.  `TrustBanner`: Quick hit of trust signals (Govt. Registered, 4.7★ Rating, Years of Experience, Kadar Khan mention).
5.  `IntroSection`: Brief welcome message emphasizing safety and modern automatic car training in Burail, Chandigarh.
6.  `ServiceHighlights`: Grid layout highlighting key services (Automatic Training, Manual Training, Refresher Courses).
7.  `Feature_RealTraffic`: Section specifically dedicated to the "Real-Traffic & Highway Curriculum" advantage.
8.  `Feature_SafetyFirst`: Emphasis on safety protocols, dual-control vehicles, and instructor qualifications.
9.  `InstructorProfile`: Spotlight on Kadar Khan, his expertise, and teaching philosophy.
10. `VideoTestimonial`: Embedded video or a prominent featured quote from a successful student.
11. `ProcessSteps`: 1-2-3 guide on how to get started (Book -> Train -> Get License).
12. `PricingTeaser`: A quick look at the most popular packages (e.g., 15-day course, weekend crash course).
13. `VehicleFleet`: Carousel or grid showcasing the modern, well-maintained fleet (especially automatic cars).
14. `TargetAudience`: Highlighting suitability for beginners, nervous drivers, and those needing a refresher.
15. `ReviewCarousel`: Sliding or grid layout of Google/Facebook reviews highlighting the 4.7★ rating.
16. `LocationMap`: Embedded map showing the Burail, Chandigarh location and service areas.
17. `FAQ_Accordion`: 3-4 most common questions answered directly on the home page.
18. `BlogTeaser`: Latest 2-3 articles from the blog for SEO and educational value (e.g., "Tips for passing the test").
19. `PreFooterCTA`: Final, strong call to action before the footer (e.g., "Ready to take the wheel?").
20. `Footer`: Comprehensive footer with links, detailed contact info, social icons, and legal disclaimers.
