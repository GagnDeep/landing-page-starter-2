# Home Page Layout Blueprint
Verma's Bakery & Sweets

## Sections & Intent

1. **Top Announcement Bar**: A very thin strip at the very top for seasonal updates or operational hours.
2. **Main Navigation**: Full horizontal header with logo (centered or left), links to categories, and cart/action buttons.
3. **Hero Section (Full Bleed)**: A grand, cinematic introduction to the bakery. Evokes heritage and warmth immediately.
4. **Heritage Intro**: A textual section with elegant typography briefly telling the family story.
5. **Featured Category 1 - Traditional Sweets**: A spotlight on signature items (e.g., Ladoo, Barfi).
6. **Featured Category 2 - Fresh Bakery**: Highlighting freshly baked goods (cakes, breads).
7. **Bestsellers Carousel**: A horizontally scrollable list of the most popular items.
8. **Artisanal Process Spotlight**: A split-screen section (image + text) explaining the hand-made quality.
9. **Seasonal Specials Banner**: A vibrant, visually distinct banner for current holiday/seasonal offerings.
10. **Testimonials/Reviews**: A carousel or grid of customer love and legacy stories.
11. **Gifting & Occasions**: A section dedicated to wedding boxes, corporate gifting, and festivals.
12. **Ingredient Quality Highlight**: Focusing on pure ghee, fresh ingredients, no preservatives.
13. **Local Community/Storefront**: A look at the physical shop and its connection to the neighborhood.
14. **Dietary & Specialty Options**: Callouts for sugar-free, vegan, or gluten-free traditional sweets.
15. **Newsletter Signup**: An elegant form to capture emails for updates and offers.
16. **Awards & Recognition**: Badges or mentions of local press, awards, or heritage status.
17. **Quick Order/Catering Call to Action**: A bold section driving users to order for large events.
18. **Instagram/Social Grid**: A beautifully laid out visual feed of recent baked goods.
19. **Visit Us (Map & Hours)**: Clear, practical information for foot traffic.
20. **Pre-Footer Links**: Secondary navigation grouped clearly.
21. **Footer**: Copyright, legal links, and minimal branding.
