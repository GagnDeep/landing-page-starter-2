# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

## Intent
To serve as the welcoming grand entrance for Harbhajan's Catering Patiala, converting visitors by showcasing the heritage, high quality (4.8 stars), and mouth-watering Punjabi cuisine.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Header/Navigation:** Sticky transparent-to-solid navigation with brand logo, links, and "Get a Quote" CTA.
2. **Hero Section:** Full viewport height, background video or rich image of a grand catering setup, large 'Playfair Display' headline ("Authentic Punjabi Flavors for Your Grand Celebrations"), subheadline, and primary CTA.
3. **Trust Banner:** A horizontal strip showcasing the 4.8-star rating, years in business, and "Family Owned" badge.
4. **Introduction/Our Story (Brief):** A warm, inviting section with a photo of the founders/family, expressing hospitality and tradition.
5. **Signature Dishes Carousel:** A sliding gallery highlighting key dishes (Amritsari Kulcha, Dal Makhani) with rich imagery and descriptions.
6. **Services Overview Title:** Section header for catering services offered.
7. **Service Card 1 (Weddings):** Visual card for grand wedding catering.
8. **Service Card 2 (Corporate Events):** Visual card for professional event catering.
9. **Service Card 3 (Private Parties):** Visual card for intimate family functions.
10. **The "Generous Portions" Promise:** A visual break or banner emphasizing their commitment to abundant, satisfying servings.
11. **Testimonial Slider:** Cards featuring glowing reviews from past clients, using elegant serif quotes.
12. **Gallery Grid (Teaser):** A masonry or balanced grid showing vibrant photos of food, staff, and event setups.
13. **Why Choose Us Features:** A 3-column or 4-column grid with HugeIcons illustrating key benefits (Authentic Taste, Professional Service, Pure Veg, Custom Menus).
14. **Video Highlight:** A prominent section featuring an embedded video of a live catering event, capturing the ambiance and sizzle.
15. **Pricing Teaser:** A soft-sell section pointing towards transparent package pricing for different scales of events.
16. **Chef's Philosophy:** A beautifully styled quote block or section about the head chef's dedication to quality ingredients.
17. **FAQ Teaser:** Accordion-style preview of 3-4 top questions (e.g., booking advance, tasting sessions).
18. **Final CTA/Booking Banner:** High-contrast section urging visitors to check availability or request a menu.
19. **Newsletter/Offers Signup:** A simple input form for staying updated on seasonal menus or tasting events.
20. **Pre-Footer Links:** Quick navigation, contact info, social links, and business hours.
21. **Footer:** Copyright, legal links, and a subtle traditional motif.

## Visual Rhythm
Flows from high-impact visuals (Hero) down to structured, trust-building content (Reviews, Services), breaking the grid with full-width sensory sections (Video, Carousel), and ending with clear conversion paths.
