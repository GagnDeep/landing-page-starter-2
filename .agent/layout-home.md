# Home Page Layout & Structural Blueprint

**Goal:** Establish immediate authority, showcase specialization in paralysis and chronic recovery, and build trust particularly with NRI patients seeking elite care.

## Rhythm & Structure

1. **Global Header (Navigation)**
   - Sticky, translucent background with blur (glassmorphism).
   - Left: Sophisticated typographic logo.
   - Right: Primary navigation links and a prominent "Book Consultation" CTA button.

2. **Hero Section (The Authority Arrival)**
   - Full viewport height (min-h-screen).
   - Large, slow-moving or static premium background image (clinical, modern, abstract).
   - Bold, large serif headline communicating elite recovery.
   - Clean sans-serif subheadline.
   - Two CTAs: Primary (Solid) and Secondary (Outline/Ghost).

3. **Trust Indicators (The Anchor Bar)**
   - Immediately below the hero.
   - Horizontal scrolling or neat grid of logos/accreditations, years of experience, and "Trusted by NRI families."
   - Subtle background tint (Muted Surface).

4. **Introduction / Welcome Statement**
   - Centered alignment.
   - Generous top and bottom padding (Sectional spacing).
   - Brief, powerful statement from Dr. Iftikhar Ali Khan about the philosophy of care.
   - Script or handwritten signature element for a personal touch.

5. **Core Specializations Overview**
   - Grid layout (1 col mobile, 3 cols desktop).
   - Three focus areas: Paralysis Rehab, Post-Op Care, Chronic Pain.
   - Each card features a custom, subtle iconography (Hugeicons), a serif title, and short descriptive text.
   - Hover state: Gentle elevation lift and subtle border glow.

6. **The Clinic Environment (Visual Showcase)**
   - 50/50 split layout (Image left, Text right).
   - Image: A beautifully framed shot of the modern clinic or equipment.
   - Text: Highlighting the premium, hygienic, and advanced nature of the facility.

7. **NRI Patient Focus (Dedicated Section)**
   - Dark mode background block to break up the flow and draw attention.
   - Focus on remote coordination, trusted care while family is abroad, and premium concierge-style service.
   - Testimonial pull-quote specific to an NRI client.

8. **Dr. Iftikhar Ali Khan Profile Snippet**
   - 50/50 split layout (Text left, Image right).
   - Image: Professional, high-quality portrait of the doctor in clinical setting.
   - Text: Credentials, years of experience, specific focus on complex neuro-rehab.
   - CTA to full "About" page.

9. **Patient Success Stories (Testimonials Slider)**
   - Soft background color.
   - Carousel or grid of elegant testimonial cards.
   - Focus on transformation and recovery journeys.
   - Includes patient name, condition treated, and quote.

10. **Detailed Service: Paralysis Rehabilitation Deep-Dive**
    - Large full-width banner or distinct structural block.
    - Deep dive into the signature service.
    - Bulleted list of approaches (e.g., Bobath concept, PNF).

11. **Detailed Service: Sports & Orthopedic Recovery**
    - Alternating layout from the previous section.
    - Focus on returning to peak performance.

12. **The "Why Choose Us" Grid**
    - 2x2 or 3x2 grid of key differentiators.
    - E.g., One-on-one attention, state-of-the-art equipment, evidence-based practice, pure hygienic environment.

13. **Technology & Equipment Highlight**
    - Horizontal scrolling strip of advanced equipment used in the clinic.
    - Shows investment in modern recovery tools.

14. **Process / Patient Journey Steps**
    - Vertical timeline or horizontal step-by-step layout.
    - Steps: Consultation -> Assessment -> Custom Plan -> Recovery.
    - Connecting lines or arrows indicating progression.

15. **Insurance & Payment Information**
    - Simple, clear section addressing practical concerns.
    - List of accepted insurance or payment methods.

16. **Recent Articles / Health Insights (Blog Teaser)**
    - 3-column grid of recent blog posts.
    - Image, category tag, title, and "Read More" link.
    - Encourages SEO engagement and shows ongoing thought leadership.

17. **FAQ Accordion (Quick Answers)**
    - Narrow, centered layout for easy reading.
    - 5-6 common questions (e.g., "Do I need a referral?", "How long is a session?").
    - Clean, modern accordion behavior.

18. **Final CTA / Booking Section**
    - Strong, high-contrast block.
    - Compelling headline: "Start Your Recovery Journey Today."
    - Prominent booking form or button.
    - Contact details (Phone, Address) explicitly listed alongside.

19. **Location & Map Integrated Section**
    - Map visualization (static elegant image or interactive if requested later).
    - Address details, opening hours prominently displayed.
    - Directions for easy access in Patiala.

20. **Global Footer**
    - Multi-column layout.
    - Brand logo, brief summary.
    - Quick links to all pages.
    - Legal links (Privacy, Terms).
    - Social media icons.
    - Copyright information.
