# Home Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Establish a rhythmic, editorial, premium journey from initial impact to conversion, spanning at least 20 distinct sections. The flow must breathe, alternating between large visual moments and focused textual narratives.

1. **Global Navigation (Header):** Transparent initial state, transitioning to a solid blurred backing on scroll. Minimalist logo, elegant typographic links, subtle cart/search icons.
2. **Hero Section (The Arrival):** Full-bleed atmospheric background (video or subtle parallax image). A single, large, elegant serif headline (Playfair Display) with a delicate sans-serif subtitle and a refined ghost button ("Explore the Collection").
3. **Brand Mantra / Introduction:** Ample padding (fluid-xl). A centered, large-type statement about the craft of bespoke tailoring in Chandigarh. Minimal text, high impact.
4. **Featured Collection - Introduction:** Left-aligned heading with a short descriptive paragraph. Establishes the rhythm of the upcoming product showcase.
5. **Featured Collection - Look 1:** Asymmetric layout. Large image on the left, descriptive copy and "View Details" link on the right.
6. **Featured Collection - Look 2:** Asymmetric layout reversed. Large image on the right, copy on the left. Maintains visual interest through alternating composition.
7. **Featured Collection - Look 3:** Full-width cinematic image break, with text elegantly overlaid or positioned below in a 2-column grid (Image above, details below).
8. **The Art of Craftsmanship (Video/Visual Divider):** A deeply immersive section showcasing the stitching process. Darker background or rich maroon to shift the mood.
9. **Craftsmanship Detail 1 (Fabric):** Two-column split. Focus on the premium fabrics sourced globally.
10. **Craftsmanship Detail 2 (Embroidery):** Focus on intricate handwork. Image-heavy with delicate captions.
11. **Testimonial / Editorial Quote:** Full width, fluid-lg padding. A single, powerful quote from a client or fashion publication, set in large serif italic, with a subtle gold accent line.
12. **Bespoke Process - Step 1 (Consultation):** A horizontal timeline or staggered card layout. Minimalist cards with refined borders.
13. **Bespoke Process - Step 2 (Design & Tailoring):** Continuing the timeline/staggered layout.
14. **Bespoke Process - Step 3 (Delivery & Fittings):** The final step of the bespoke journey.
15. **Atelier Peek (Parallax Window):** A narrow horizontal slice of an image showing the Chandigarh studio, moving slightly slower than the scroll (parallax effect).
16. **Journal / Editorial Insights (Blog Teaser):** A 3-column grid of recent articles. Large images, elegant serif titles, subtle "Read more" links. No heavy card boxes—just images and text with ample space.
17. **Global Shipping / Service Assurance:** A calm, minimal section detailing worldwide reach and premium packaging. Uses subtle, refined iconography.
18. **Newsletter Signup:** A clean, uncluttered form. "Join the Inner Circle." A single input field with an elegant submit button and a subtle gold ring on focus.
19. **Instagram / Social Grid:** A tightly packed, borderless grid of aesthetic images from social media. A visual mosaic to end the page.
20. **Global Footer:** Deep charcoal or rich background. Multi-column layout for links, contact info, legal, and subtle branding. Elegantly spaced, highly legible.
