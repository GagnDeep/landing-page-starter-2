# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

## Intent
To establish Villa Hair Sense as the premier, stylist-led salon in Chandigarh. Must immediately convey luxury, expertise, and a welcoming, high-end atmosphere.

## Sections (20+)
1. **Global Header:** Sticky navigation with logo, primary links, and a prominent "Book Appointment" CTA.
2. **Hero Section (The Reveal):** Full-bleed cinematic background (image/video placeholder), prominent H1, subtitle, and primary CTA.
3. **Brand Promise (The Ethos):** A short, elegant typography-focused section outlining the salon's philosophy.
4. **Signature Services Overview:** A grid or masonry layout highlighting 3-4 top services (e.g., Butterfly Haircut, Balayage, Spa).
5. **Stylist Spotlight (The Artisan):** Introduction to the lead stylist/owner, building personal connection and authority.
6. **Before & After Gallery (The Transformation):** An interactive slider or grid showing high-quality transformation images.
7. **Client Testimonials (The Praise):** Elegant pull-quotes from satisfied clients, emphasizing the 4.8-star reputation.
8. **The Salon Experience (The Ambiance):** Image-led section showcasing the interior of the Sector 9 location.
9. **Featured Products (The Regimen):** A curated list of high-end hair care products used and sold at the salon.
10. **Membership/Loyalty Teaser:** Information on VIP programs or recurring client benefits.
11. **Bridal & Special Events:** Dedicated section for high-ticket bridal and event styling services.
12. **Educational Content/Blog Teaser:** Latest tips or trends from the salon's blog.
13. **Instagram Feed (The Portfolio):** A stylized grid pulling recent, curated posts from social media.
14. **Location & Contact (The Destination):** Map integration, address (Sector 9, Chandigarh), and opening hours.
15. **FAQ Teaser:** 3-4 common questions with an accordion or simple list, linking to the full FAQ page.
16. **Newsletter Signup (The Insider):** Elegant form for clients to receive updates, exclusive offers, or styling tips.
17. **Press & Mentions:** Logos or quotes from local magazines or influencers, if applicable.
18. **Why Choose Us (The Differentiator):** Iconography + text highlighting unique selling points (e.g., bespoke consultations, premium products).
19. **Final CTA (The Invitation):** A large, compelling section urging the user to book their transformation.
20. **Global Footer:** Comprehensive footer with secondary links, social icons, legal links, and contact info.
