# A2Zee Events - Home Page Layout Blueprint

## Overall Intent
The Home page serves as the digital front door to a luxury destination wedding experience. It must balance breathtaking imagery (hospitality-pro) with clear value propositions and emotional resonance (bespoke tailoring). The layout should feel airy, unhurried, and highly curated.

## Structural Sections (20 Named Sections)

1.  **`site-header`:** Sticky navigation. Transparent at the top, transitioning to solid background (`bg-background/80` with backdrop blur) on scroll. Elegant logo center or left. Minimal links (About, Portfolio, Services, Contact).
2.  **`hero-cinematic`:** Full-screen (`min-h-svh`) hero section. Subtle, slow zoom-in on a breathtaking destination wedding image. Large, elegant serif heading (`font-heading`). Primary CTA button ("Inquire Now").
3.  **`brand-ethos-intro`:** Generous padding (`py-32`). A succinct, poetic paragraph centered. High-contrast serif drop cap. Focuses on the "why" — curating high-end, hospitality-driven experiences.
4.  **`featured-destinations-grid`:** A 3-column asymmetric grid. Images have slight parallax effects. Sharp corners (`rounded-none` or `rounded-sm`). Small, tracking-widest uppercase eyebrow text ("Locations").
5.  **`service-pillars-overview`:** 4-column layout detailing core offerings (Planning, Design, Concierge, Production). Use clean, minimalist icons (HugeIcons). Hover state gently reveals more details.
6.  **`trust-bar-publications`:** A subtle, muted band (`bg-muted/50`). Logos of high-end publications (Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, etc.) in monochrome, low opacity.
7.  **`founder-note-split`:** 50/50 split layout. Left side: High-quality editorial portrait. Right side: A personal message from the founder emphasizing bespoke tailoring and NRI clientele understanding.
8.  **`portfolio-highlight-carousel`:** A slow-moving, infinite carousel of the best work. Edge-to-edge layout. Images are the focus; captions appear gracefully on hover.
9.  **`the-process-steps`:** Vertical timeline or staggered layout. 3 to 4 distinct steps showing the logistical precision from concept to execution. Subtle vertical line connecting them.
10. **`testimonial-quote-hero`:** Full width, deep background (`bg-primary`). A single, powerful quote from a past client. Very large serif font. High-contrast text (`text-primary-foreground`).
11. **`video-mood-reel`:** A cinematic, silent auto-playing video (or static image with a play button overlay) capturing the emotion and scale of the events.
12. **`stats-and-scale`:** A minimalist grid showing key metrics (e.g., "15+ Countries," "100+ Curated Events") to establish credibility and capability for large-scale operations.
13. **`nri-focus-section`:** A dedicated block speaking directly to the NRI clientele. Addressing specific pain points (time zones, local vendor management) with reassuring, professional copy.
14. **`curated-venues-spotlight`:** Highlights of exclusive venue partnerships. Card layout with high-quality imagery and subtle tags indicating location/style.
15. **`aesthetic-gallery-masonry`:** A tight masonry grid of detail shots (table settings, floral arrangements, bespoke lighting). Focus on the "premium-craft" aspect.
16. **`faq-preview-accordion`:** A clean accordion component highlighting 3-4 of the most common high-level questions (e.g., "Do you travel internationally?").
17. **`recent-journal-entries`:** 2 or 3 column layout featuring recent blog posts or real wedding features. Emphasizes storytelling.
18. **`instagram-feed-grid`:** A 4x2 or 6x1 grid of recent social media imagery. Seamless connection to the broader visual brand.
19. **`cta-grand-finale`:** A large, elegant section with a final, compelling call to action. "Begin Your Journey." Simple, sophisticated button.
20. **`site-footer`:** Comprehensive but clean footer. Multi-column layout for links, contact info, newsletter signup, and legal links. Minimalist design.
