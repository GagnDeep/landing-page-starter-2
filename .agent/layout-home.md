# Layout Blueprint: Home Page (`/`)

## Goal
The Home page serves as the primary gateway, establishing immediate trust, highlighting the core methodology, and driving conversions (bookings/inquiries).

## Section Architecture (20 Blocks)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):** Fixed/Sticky header. Logo left, Links center (About, Features, Pricing, Blog, Contact), Primary CTA ("Book a Lesson") right.
2.  **Hero Section:** High-impact. Headline focusing on confidence and safety. Subheadline with government-registered status. Primary CTA. Background: Calm, high-quality image of driving instruction or subtle video loop.
3.  **Trust Bar (Social Proof):** Logos/Text highlighting certifications (Govt. Registered), 4.9-star rating, "Over 10,000+ confident drivers."
4.  **Value Proposition Intro:** Short, authoritative text block. "Why Sector 47 chooses Armaan."
5.  **Core Methodology Spotlight:** 3-column grid. Calm, Methodical, Safe. Icon + short description for each.
6.  **Beginner Focus Feature:** Left: Image of relaxed student and instructor. Right: Copy focusing on overcoming driving anxiety.
7.  **Service Overview - Beginners:** Card/Block linking to beginner packages.
8.  **Service Overview - Refreshers:** Card/Block linking to refresher courses.
9.  **Service Overview - Test Prep:** Card/Block linking to license test preparation.
10. **The Fleet Section:** Highlighting well-maintained, dual-control vehicles. Image gallery or slider.
11. **Instructor Profile Highlight:** Meet the lead instructor(s). Emphasize patience and experience.
12. **Video/Interactive Tour:** A section holding an embed or modal trigger for a video showing a "day in the life" of a lesson.
13. **Pricing Teaser:** 2-3 popular packages displayed clearly. CTA to view full pricing.
14. **Testimonial Carousel:** Highlighting specific stories of overcoming fear. Star ratings included.
15. **Location & Area Covered:** Map visual or list of sectors covered (focusing around Sector 47).
16. **Safety First Guarantee:** A dedicated block emphasizing the dual-control cars and strict safety protocols.
17. **FAQ Teaser:** 3-4 common questions (e.g., "Do I need a learner's license first?"). Accordion style. Link to full FAQ.
18. **Recent Blog/Advice Posts:** 2-3 cards linking to driving tips (e.g., "How to parallel park").
19. **Final CTA (Bottom of Page):** Large, inviting block. "Ready to take the wheel?" Secondary text and prominent button.
20. **Global Footer:** Links, Contact Info, Address, Socials, Legal links (Terms, Privacy), Copyright.
