# Layout Architecture: Home Page

## Structural Intent
The home page serves as the grand entrance to Blinking Eye Events. It must immediately convey luxury, warmth, and full-service capability. The rhythm should alternate between visually arresting imagery and clear, elegant typography.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Global Notice Bar:** Subtle banner at the top for urgent announcements or current booking season status.
2. **Main Navigation (Header):** Transparent, becoming solid on scroll. Logo centered, links distributed elegantly.
3. **Hero Section (The Grand Entrance):** Full-bleed cinematic image or subtle background video. Large H1, understated CTA.
4. **Brand Essence (The Philosophy):** Minimalist text section introducing Kavita and the core philosophy of Blinking Eye Events.
5. **Services Overview Intro:** Section header introducing the spectrum of services.
6. **Service Card 1 (Weddings):** Image-led card detailing wedding planning.
7. **Service Card 2 (Corporate Events):** Image-led card for corporate galas/retreats.
8. **Service Card 3 (Private Celebrations):** Image-led card for anniversaries, milestones.
9. **The "Full-Service" Promise:** Text-heavy section explaining what full-service truly means (24/7 reliability, zero stress).
10. **Portfolio/Gallery Teaser:** Horizontal scroll or masonry grid of top event photography.
11. **Testimonial 1 (The Emotional Impact):** A standout, glowing review focusing on the emotional relief of hiring the firm.
12. **The Process (How We Work) Intro:** Brief introduction to the methodology.
13. **Process Step 1 (Discovery):** Detail on the initial consultation.
14. **Process Step 2 (Design & Logistics):** Detail on planning and sourcing.
15. **Process Step 3 (Execution):** Detail on day-of management.
16. **Meet Kavita (The Founder):** A personal note and portrait of the founder, establishing trust.
17. **As Featured In / Partners:** Logos of premium vendors or publications (subtle, desaturated).
18. **Secondary CTA (Book a Consultation):** A strong, elegant block encouraging the next step.
19. **Newsletter / Digest Signup:** "Join our circle" for event inspiration.
20. **Global Footer:** Extensive footer with navigation, contact details, social links, and legal requirements.
