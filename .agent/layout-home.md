# Layout Intent: Home Page

**Goal:** Establish trust, luxury, and convenience immediately. The flow must read like an editorial feature introducing a high-end service.

## 1. Hero Section
- **Visual Rhythm:** Full viewport height. Deep, cinematic background image (or video) taking up the entire screen.
- **Layout:** Text overlaid elegantly in the center or left-aligned. High contrast between text and background. Subtle down-arrow indicator for scroll.

## 2. Trust Indicators (As Seen In / Badges)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Minimalist, monochromatic logo strip.
- **Layout:** Horizontal scrolling or evenly spaced row. Generous padding above and below.

## 3. Brand Philosophy Statement
- **Visual Rhythm:** Stark, text-heavy, massive typography.
- **Layout:** Centered, oversized serif text acting as a pull-quote. Lots of negative space. No images.

## 4. Service Categories Grid
- **Visual Rhythm:** Structured, architectural grid.
- **Layout:** 2x2 or 3x2 grid of tall, portrait-oriented image cards. Each card has a subtle text overlay at the bottom.

## 5. The "At-Home" Advantage (Split Feature 1)
- **Visual Rhythm:** High contrast asymmetry.
- **Layout:** Left side: Large, bleed-to-edge image of a relaxing home setup. Right side: Ample white space, refined heading, and descriptive paragraph.

## 6. The "In-Salon" Experience (Split Feature 2)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Reverse of the previous section.
- **Layout:** Right side: Large image of a pristine salon interior. Left side: Text block vertically centered.

## 7. Signature Treatments Highlight
- **Visual Rhythm:** Carousel or horizontal scroll.
- **Layout:** Cards that peek off the edge of the screen to encourage swiping/scrolling. Delicate typography for pricing details.

## 8. Testimonial Showcase
- **Visual Rhythm:** Soft, text-focused, floating layout.
- **Layout:** A single, large, rotating testimonial dominating the viewport. Subtle fade transitions.

## 9. Meet the Lead Stylist / Founder
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial portrait profile.
- **Layout:** Split layout. An intimate, high-quality portrait on one side, a personal letter-style text block on the other.

## 10. Process: How It Works
- **Visual Rhythm:** Step-by-step linear progression.
- **Layout:** Vertical timeline or 1x3 horizontal grid. Minimalist icons or elegant numerals (1, 2, 3) leading the eye.

## 11. Highlight Video Section
- **Visual Rhythm:** Immersive, breaking the grid.
- **Layout:** 16:9 large video player spanning the width of the container. Minimal UI controls.

## 12. Featured Products Used
- **Visual Rhythm:** Product showcase, clean and clinical but warm.
- **Layout:** Horizontal band of product bottles. Subtle hover effects that lift the products.

## 13. Location & Availability
- **Visual Rhythm:** Informational but elegant.
- **Layout:** Split section. A stylized, monochromatic map on one side, operating hours and service radius on the other.

## 14. Membership / Subscription Callout
- **Visual Rhythm:** High emphasis, using primary accent colors.
- **Layout:** A distinct block with a different background color. Bold typography announcing the VIP/membership tier.

## 15. FAQ Teaser
- **Visual Rhythm:** Accordion style, very clean.
- **Layout:** A list of 3-4 top questions. Thin, elegant border dividers between items.

## 16. Journal / Blog Recent Posts
- **Visual Rhythm:** Magazine index.
- **Layout:** 3-column grid of recent articles. Each card features an image, a small category label, and a serif headline.

## 17. Newsletter Signup
- **Visual Rhythm:** Restrained, form-focused.
- **Layout:** Centered block. A single elegant input field with a sharp, rectangular submit button.

## 18. Instagram / Social Proof Feed
- **Visual Rhythm:** Image-heavy mosaic.
- **Layout:** A tight grid of square images showing real clients or behind-the-scenes.

## 19. Final Call to Action (Booking)
- **Visual Rhythm:** Dramatic, high-contrast block.
- **Layout:** Large heading, generous padding, and a prominent, highly styled booking button.

## 20. Footer
- **Visual Rhythm:** Dense but organized typography.
- **Layout:** Multi-column layout for navigation, legal links, and social icons. Clean lines, small sans-serif text.
