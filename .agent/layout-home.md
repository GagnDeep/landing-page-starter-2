# Home Page Layout Structure

**Goal:** Establish Sardaar G. Tailors as the premier bespoke mens tailoring house.

## Sections (20+)

1.  **Header/Navigation:** Elegant, sticky, transparent to solid transition. Logo centered, navigation links flanking.
2.  **Hero/Opening Statement:** Full-bleed image/video of craftsmanship (e.g., threading a needle, measuring fabric). High contrast serif typography overlaid. Call to Action: "Book a Fitting".
3.  **Brand Essence (The Sardaar G. Promise):** A quiet, text-heavy section detailing the legacy of Patiala craftsmanship. Generous padding.
4.  **Featured Collection - Sherwanis:** Image grid highlighting intricate details of bespoke sherwanis.
5.  **Featured Collection - Kurta Pajamas:** Minimalist layout showing the crispness of Muktsari kurta-pajamas.
6.  **The Master Tailor:** Profile section of the head craftsman. Image beside a quote about dedication to the art.
7.  **The Process - Step 1: Consultation:** Icon + text explaining the initial design and fabric selection phase.
8.  **The Process - Step 2: Measurements:** Highlighting the precision of the bespoke fit.
9.  **The Process - Step 3: First Baste Fitting:** Showcasing the skeleton of the garment.
10. **The Process - Step 4: Final Delivery:** The finished product and adjustments.
11. **Fabric Gallery:** A horizontal scroll or grid of premium fabrics (silk, linen, velvet) with macro photography.
12. **Client Testimonial 1:** A full-width quote block emphasizing the feeling of wearing the garments.
13. **Editorial Lookbook Highlight:** A sprawling, magazine-style layout of a recent collection.
14. **Bespoke Accessories:** Cufflinks, turbans, matching juttis.
15. **The Patiala Heritage:** A section tying the brand to its royal and cultural roots in Punjab.
16. **Client Testimonial 2:** Focus on a wedding experience.
17. **FAQ Teaser:** Quick accordion of 3 common questions (e.g., timeline, pricing).
18. **Newsletter/Join the Guild:** A refined email capture form for exclusive updates.
19. **Location/Visit the Studio:** Map or stylized image of the Patiala storefront with hours and booking link.
20. **Footer:** Comprehensive links, social icons (Instagram focused), legal links, and a final brand mark.
