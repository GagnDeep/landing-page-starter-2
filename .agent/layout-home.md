# Layout: Home Page

The home page defines the high-touch, professional reputation of Studio Sanjay Arts. It must feature at least 20 distinct sections with a clear visual rhythm, conveying a "magazine-style" elegant studio aesthetic.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Full-screen cinematic video/image background. Large Playfair Display headline ("Capturing the Art of Your Legacy"). Soft gradient overlay to ensure text readability.
2.  **Trust Bar / As Featured In:** Minimalist logo strip (e.g., Vogue Weddings, WedMeGood) with a subtle muted background to establish immediate authority.
3.  **Introduction / The Studio:** A clean, spacious 2-column layout. Left: A striking portrait or studio shot. Right: A serif pull quote and humanist body text introducing the studio's philosophy.
4.  **Signature Style Highlight:** A full-width masonry or irregular grid showcasing 3-4 iconic, high-contrast images. Emphasis on candid storytelling.
5.  **Service Spotlight - Wedding Photography:** Large image card with hover effects (slight zoom/fade). Playfair headline, short description, and "Explore Weddings" button.
6.  **Service Spotlight - Pre-Wedding Films:** Alternating layout from Section 5. Focus on cinematic storytelling.
7.  **Service Spotlight - NRI & Destination Weddings:** Focus on global clientele. Text highlighting seamless experience and family-centered approach.
8.  **Testimonial / The Experience (1):** A full-width section with a dark ambient background ("The Evening Fitting" vibe). A glowing review from a past client in elegant serif typography.
9.  **Featured Real Wedding (Case Study 1):** A highly curated grid of a single wedding. "The Royal Patiala Wedding". Includes a brief story of the couple.
10. **The Team / Meet Sanjay:** A warm, approachable portrait of the lead photographer/founder with a short bio. Fosters trust.
11. **Our Approach / Why Us:** A 3-column icon or small-image grid detailing the process (e.g., Unobtrusive, Family-First, Editorial Quality).
12. **Video Reel Teaser:** A 16:9 container for a short cinematic reel. Soft glow (`ring`) effect on hover.
13. **Featured Real Wedding (Case Study 2):** "The Destination Udaipur Celebration". Alternating layout from Case Study 1 to maintain visual rhythm.
14. **Pricing Teaser:** A clean, structured section hinting at the investment. Emphasizes "Bespoke Packages" and "Full-Service Experience". Link to Pricing page.
15. **Testimonial Grid (2):** A masonry layout of 3-4 shorter testimonials. Cards have subtle borders and soft background colors.
16. **FAQ Snippets:** An accordion featuring the top 3-4 questions (e.g., "Do you travel internationally?", "How long does delivery take?"). Clean and functional.
17. **Blog / Journal Recent Posts:** A 3-column grid of recent blog posts. Magazine-style cards with date, category, and elegant title.
18. **Instagram Feed Teaser:** A sleek row of 5-6 square images. "Follow our journey @StudioSanjayArts".
19. **Call to Action (Pre-Footer):** A large, high-contrast block (using primary or dark background). "Let's Tell Your Story". A prominent contact button.
20. **Footer:** Comprehensive footer. Links to all pages, contact info, social icons, newsletter signup. Clean sans-serif typography.

## Visual Rhythm
- Alternating dark and light sections to create pacing.
- Generous padding (`py-24`) between major sections to allow content to breathe.
- Asymmetrical grids used sparingly to add artistic flair without feeling chaotic.
