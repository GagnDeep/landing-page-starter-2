# Homepage Layout Architecture: A-One Cakes and Gift Zone

This document outlines the structural intent and visual rhythm for the homepage. The goal is a highly immersive, editorial flow—not a standard SaaS grid. Total sections: 20.

## 1. Top Navigation Bar (`HomeNav`)
- **Intent:** Sticky, elegant, and minimal.
- **Rhythm:** Full width, transparent fading to solid cream/espresso on scroll. Centered logo (Fraunces), flanked by clean, typographic links and a prominent "Order Now" CTA.

## 2. Hero Section: The Midnight Bakery (`HomeHero`)
- **Intent:** Immersive, emotional hook. A massive, high-contrast headline overlapping a stunning, screen-filling background image of a decadent cake or gift box.
- **Rhythm:** Full viewport height (`min-h-screen`). Text is vertically centered but horizontally offset (left-aligned) for an editorial, asymmetric feel.

## 3. Urgency & Trust Banner (`HomeUrgencyBanner`)
- **Intent:** Instantly communicate "24/7 Delivery & Last-Minute Perfection".
- **Rhythm:** A narrow, full-width marquee or typographic ribbon directly beneath the hero. Fast reading, high contrast.

## 4. Introduction: The Art of the Occasion (`HomeIntro`)
- **Intent:** A quiet, elegant introduction to the brand's ethos.
- **Rhythm:** Generous macro padding (`py-32`). A narrow, center-aligned text block (`max-w-2xl`) using the serif font, making it feel like an invitation or a magazine excerpt.

## 5. Bestsellers Showcase: Cakes (`HomeBestsellersCakes`)
- **Intent:** Visually rich showcase of the top products.
- **Rhythm:** Asymmetric grid. 1 large featured item spanning 2 columns, surrounded by 2 smaller items. Images bleed to the edges of their containers.

## 6. Category Split: Gifts & Flowers (`HomeCategorySplit`)
- **Intent:** Clear navigation to non-cake offerings.
- **Rhythm:** 50/50 split screen layout. Left side: rich imagery of flowers. Right side: rich imagery of gifts. Hover interactions reveal categories.

## 7. The Midnight Magic: 24/7 Feature (`HomeFeatureMidnight`)
- **Intent:** Highlight the unique value proposition of round-the-clock service.
- **Rhythm:** Dark mode forced section (even in light mode) to evoke night. Large typography on the left, an evocative image on the right.

## 8. Artisanal Quality Detail (`HomeQualityDetail`)
- **Intent:** Prove the premium nature of the products. Focus on ingredients or craftsmanship.
- **Rhythm:** Text-heavy left column with small, overlapping inset images. Right column features a single, massive, macro-photography detail shot.

## 9. Customer Stories / Testimonials (`HomeTestimonials`)
- **Intent:** Build trust through luxury social proof.
- **Rhythm:** A horizontal scrolling carousel. Cards are minimal—just beautiful typography (serif quotes) and small, elegant attribution.

## 10. The Process: Order to Doorstep (`HomeProcess`)
- **Intent:** Demystify the ordering and delivery process.
- **Rhythm:** 3 or 4 column grid. Each step represented by a minimal icon and highly refined, short typography. Lots of negative space.

## 11. Custom Orders: Make It Yours (`HomeCustomOrders`)
- **Intent:** Push high-ticket custom creations.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width background image with a glass/tinted overlay card in the center. High contrast CTA.

## 12. Featured Collection: Weddings (`HomeCollectionWeddings`)
- **Intent:** Showcase premium, high-tier offerings.
- **Rhythm:** A distinct background color change (e.g., to the Champagne/Muted token). Large, sweeping imagery with staggered text blocks.

## 13. Featured Collection: Birthdays (`HomeCollectionBirthdays`)
- **Intent:** Showcase the most common use case.
- **Rhythm:** Playful but elegant grid. Slightly overlapping images, perhaps with subtle parallax on scroll.

## 14. Gifting Guide Teaser (`HomeGiftingGuide`)
- **Intent:** Position the brand as a helpful expert, not just a vendor.
- **Rhythm:** Magazine-style editorial block. Large headline, 3 distinct columns of text teasing different guides (e.g., "For Him", "For Her", "Corporate").

## 15. The Ingredients Philosophy (`HomeIngredients`)
- **Intent:** Reinforce quality.
- **Rhythm:** Dense, typographic section. Large serif heading, smaller sans-serif paragraphs, arranged in an asymmetric masonry style.

## 16. Local Presence & Delivery Zones (`HomeDeliveryZones`)
- **Intent:** Practical information delivered elegantly.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, map-inspired graphic or a sleek typographic list of areas, paired with a solid color block background.

## 17. Newsletter: The Inner Circle (`HomeNewsletter`)
- **Intent:** Capture leads with an exclusive feel.
- **Rhythm:** A sophisticated, minimal form. Just an email input and a subtle submit button. Large, inviting serif headline.

## 18. Instagram / Social Proof (`HomeSocialGrid`)
- **Intent:** Show real-time activity and aesthetic.
- **Rhythm:** A tight, edge-to-edge grid of 4-6 images. No gaps. Hovering reveals a subtle overlay.

## 19. Final CTA: Don't Wait (`HomeFinalCTA`)
- **Intent:** The ultimate push to purchase.
- **Rhythm:** Massive, screen-filling typography. Very little else. A singular, bold button.

## 20. Footer (`HomeFooter`)
- **Intent:** Comprehensive, elegant sign-off.
- **Rhythm:** Multi-column layout for links, contact info, and legal. Uses the muted background color. Clean, small, sans-serif typography.
