# Layout Intent: Home Page (`/`)

The home page serves as the grand entrance to Guide Events. It must immediately establish a sense of premium luxury, trust, and comprehensive service capability, particularly appealing to high-end NRI clients.

## Sections (20 Distinct Layout Intents)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):**
    *   **Intent:** Unobtrusive but accessible access to all key areas.
    *   **Layout:** Sticky top, transparent fading to solid on scroll. Logo centered or left-aligned. Clean typography. Prominent "Book Consultation" CTA.

2.  **Hero Section:**
    *   **Intent:** Breathtaking first impression. High emotional impact.
    *   **Layout:** Full-viewport height (100vh). Slow-zooming background image/video of a spectacular wedding setup. Large, elegant serif typography (H1). Subtle call-to-action to scroll down.

3.  **Introduction / "The Art of Celebration":**
    *   **Intent:** Establish brand voice and philosophy.
    *   **Layout:** Generous whitespace. Centered alignment. A statement paragraph in the primary serif font.

4.  **Trust & Venue Partners Bar:**
    *   **Intent:** Immediate social proof via association with premium venues.
    *   **Layout:** Horizontal scrolling marquee or a neat, evenly spaced grid of desaturated partner logos.

5.  **Core Services Overview (The Orchestration):**
    *   **Intent:** High-level summary of what Guide Events does (Decor, Planning, Catering, Logistics).
    *   **Layout:** 2x2 or 4-column grid. Each item features a subtle icon, a heading, a short description, and a "Learn More" link. Hover effects should be gentle (e.g., slight background shift).

6.  **Feature Highlight: The NRI Experience:**
    *   **Intent:** Speak directly to a core demographic. Emphasize seamless remote planning.
    *   **Layout:** Asymmetric split section. 60% text (emphasizing 24/7 availability and zero-stress coordination) / 40% imagery (e.g., an elegant planning session or a relaxed couple).

7.  **Decor Showcase (Gallery Teaser):**
    *   **Intent:** Visual proof of aesthetic capability.
    *   **Layout:** Masonry or curated asymmetrical grid of high-quality decor images. Clicking an image expands it or links to a full gallery.

8.  **The Signature Approach (Process Steps):**
    *   **Intent:** Demystify the luxury planning process.
    *   **Layout:** Vertical timeline or a horizontal 3-step sequence (Consultation, Design, Execution) connected by a subtle visual line.

9.  **Testimonial / Featured Couple (Deep Dive):**
    *   **Intent:** Emotional connection through a success story.
    *   **Layout:** Full-width section with a muted background color. Large, elegant quote marks. A high-quality photo of the couple alongside a detailed testimonial praising the stress-free experience.

10. **Curated Culinary Experiences (Catering Teaser):**
    *   **Intent:** Highlight the royal Punjabi cuisine aspect (tying back to Bawa Caterers heritage if applicable/relevant).
    *   **Layout:** Image-heavy. Alternating text/image blocks highlighting specific culinary themes (e.g., Live Stalls, Royal Thali).

11. **Venue Selection & Transformation:**
    *   **Intent:** Show capability to transform spaces.
    *   **Layout:** "Before and After" interactive slider, or side-by-side images comparing an empty hall to a fully decorated event.

12. **Destination Weddings (Locations):**
    *   **Intent:** Highlight capability to execute events anywhere.
    *   **Layout:** Grid of cards, each featuring a location (e.g., Chandigarh, Udaipur, Goa) with a characteristic image and brief text.

13. **Behind the Scenes / The Team:**
    *   **Intent:** Build personal trust.
    *   **Layout:** A brief introduction to Mr. Negi and the core team. Clean, professional portraits.

14. **Quick Facts / The Guide Events Difference:**
    *   **Intent:** Rational proof points supporting the emotional appeal (e.g., "500+ Weddings", "4.9★ Rating").
    *   **Layout:** A dark, visually striking horizontal band featuring 3-4 large numbers with brief labels below them.

15. **Featured in / Press Mentions:**
    *   **Intent:** External validation and prestige.
    *   **Layout:** Simple, elegant row of publisher logos (e.g., wedding magazines).

16. **Recent Journal/Blog Entries:**
    *   **Intent:** Show ongoing activity and thought leadership.
    *   **Layout:** A 3-column grid showing the latest blog posts. Cards include a thumbnail, date, title, and a "Read More" link.

17. **FAQ Teaser:**
    *   **Intent:** Address immediate, common objections.
    *   **Layout:** An accordion component featuring the top 3-4 questions, with a link to the full FAQ page.

18. **Call to Action (Pre-Footer):**
    *   **Intent:** Drive the primary conversion (booking a consultation).
    *   **Layout:** Prominent, full-width section, perhaps with a subtle background texture. Clear, commanding typography and a large primary button.

19. **Newsletter / Insider Access:**
    *   **Intent:** Capture leads who aren't ready to book yet.
    *   **Layout:** A clean, minimal form field requesting an email address in exchange for a "wedding planning guide" or exclusive updates.

20. **Global Footer:**
    *   **Intent:** Navigation fallback, contact info, and legal links.
    *   **Layout:** Multi-column layout. Column 1: Brand intro/logo. Column 2/3: Quick links. Column 4: Contact details (Sector 21C, Chandigarh). Bottom bar: Copyright, Terms, Privacy.
