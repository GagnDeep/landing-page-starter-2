# Home Page Layout Intent Map

1. **Global Header**: Sticky top navigation, transparent at first, turns solid on scroll.
2. **Hero Section**: Full-bleed background image with large serif typography overlay, calling out "Anand Karaj & Wedding Decor".
3. **Hero Call to Action**: Subtle primary button urging users to "Book a Consultation".
4. **Brand Essence Banner**: A horizontally scrolling or elegantly fading banner highlighting core values (Tradition, Elegance, Craft).
5. **Introduction Block**: Two-column layout; text on left, staggered floral imagery on right.
6. **Service Highlights Title**: Centered serif title introducing the three main services.
7. **Service Card 1 (Anand Karaj)**: Left-aligned card with a deep, rich background and soft shadow.
8. **Service Card 2 (Reception Decor)**: Right-aligned card mirroring Card 1's rhythm.
9. **Service Card 3 (Pre-wedding)**: Center-aligned card acting as the anchor.
10. **Featured Real Weddings**: A masonry grid of high-quality imagery.
11. **Testimonial Slider**: Soft, buttercream background with large serif quotes fading in and out.
12. **The Artisan Process**: A 3-step vertical timeline showing how they work.
13. **Step 1 (Consultation)**: Detailed description of the initial chat.
14. **Step 2 (Design)**: Exploring the floral choices.
15. **Step 3 (Execution)**: The big day setup.
16. **Why Choose Us (List)**: Icon-heavy grid detailing unique selling points.
17. **Floral Signature Element**: A large, decorative, high-quality image of a signature floral arrangement acting as a visual break.
18. **Call to Action Section**: A velvet cocoa background section urging the final contact.
19. **Instagram Feed**: A clean 4-column grid of recent social posts.
20. **Global Footer**: Symmetrical footer with links, contact info, and newsletter signup.
