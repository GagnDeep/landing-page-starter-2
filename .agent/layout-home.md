# Layout Blueprint: Home Page (`/`)

*Intent: A multi-layered, editorial narrative that introduces Guide Events as the ultimate authority in high-end destination weddings.*

## 1. Minimal Navigation Bar
- **Intent**: A subtle, elegant top navigation bar that fades in. Logo center-aligned, with a hamburger menu or refined text links for an editorial feel.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Very quiet, providing grounding without dominating the viewport.

## 2. Cinematic Hero
- **Intent**: Immediately establish a sense of awe. A full-bleed, slow-moving or static editorial image/video background.
- **Visual Rhythm**: High contrast, with a massive elegant serif headline offset and staggered. Minimalist call-to-action placed unobtrusively.

## 3. The Promise (Philosophy Statement)
- **Intent**: A stark, text-heavy section acting as a deep breath. A single, powerful manifesto paragraph.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Expansive whitespace, large text set in a refined serif, centered or sharply asymmetrical.

## 4. Curated Destinations (Grid Teaser)
- **Intent**: Teasing the international scale. A masonry or asymmetrical grid of location imagery.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Alternating image sizes (portrait vs. landscape). Images overlap slightly or have distinct vertical offsets.

## 5. Trust & Authority Bar
- **Intent**: Subtle logos of high-end publications (Vogue, Harper's Bazaar) or venues.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Monochromatic, muted, horizontally scrolling or statically balanced.

## 6. The Approach (Process Overview)
- **Intent**: Breaking down the complex planning process into elegant, digestible pillars.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Three or four columns with subtle dividers or thin borders (0.5px).

## 7. Featured Real Wedding: The Setup
- **Intent**: Deep dive into a specific event. A full-width image followed by context.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Starts with a visual anchor, leading into narrative text.

## 8. Featured Real Wedding: The Details
- **Intent**: Showcasing meticulous attention to detail.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A cluster of 3-4 close-up detail shots (table settings, florals) arranged organically.

## 9. Founder's Quote (Editorial Break)
- **Intent**: Personal touch and authority from the founder.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Very large pull quote, perhaps spanning two lines, elegantly stylized, set against a muted secondary background color.

## 10. The Guide Difference (Value Proposition)
- **Intent**: Comparing the standard experience to the Guide Events experience.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A refined, understated side-by-side text layout without aggressive checkboxes or tables.

## 11. Immersive Video/Atmosphere Strip
- **Intent**: A break in the scroll. A horizontal slice of moving imagery that captures a mood (e.g., champagne pouring, a slow dance).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Edge-to-edge, fixed height.

## 12. Service Scope: Full Planning
- **Intent**: Outlining the primary offering.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Left-aligned text block heavily anchored next to a large, striking portrait-oriented image.

## 13. Service Scope: Design & Styling
- **Intent**: Outlining the secondary offering.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Alternating layout (right-aligned text, left-aligned image) for cadence.

## 14. Client Testimonial (Social Proof)
- **Intent**: A single, powerful story from a past client.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Text-focused, highly structured typography with elegant attribution.

## 15. The Venues (Exclusivity)
- **Intent**: Hinting at exclusive access to private estates and chateaus.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Horizontal carousel or list with slow interaction reveals.

## 16. Behind the Scenes (Process Transparency)
- **Intent**: Showing the team in action, proving the hard work behind the seamless result.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A collage layout, slightly chaotic but tightly constrained within a grid.

## 17. The Investment (Pricing Teaser)
- **Intent**: Qualifying leads and establishing premium positioning.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Stark, minimalist layout. Large numbers set in elegant typography.

## 18. FAQ Highlights
- **Intent**: Addressing common high-net-worth concerns immediately.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A sleek, minimal accordion. Smooth, slow expand/collapse animations.

## 19. Final Call to Action
- **Intent**: Pushing the user to the inquiry stage.
- **Visual Rhythm**: A massive, full-screen background or deep brand color block. High contrast, large serif headline.

## 20. Expansive Footer
- **Intent**: A comprehensive closing area offering navigation, legal, and subtle branding.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Multi-column, incredibly organized, small elegant typography.
