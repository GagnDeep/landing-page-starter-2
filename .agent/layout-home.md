# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

## Structural Rhythm
The home page flows like a guided tour through a luxury atelier. Sections breathe with expansive vertical padding.

## Sections (20 distinct sections)

1. **Navigation Shell:** Minimalist top nav. Logo left, elegant links right, subtle scroll interaction.
2. **Hero Expression:** Full-screen imagery with a large, editorial serif heading. "Hand-stitched elegance, tailored for you." Primary CTA: "Begin Your Consultation."
3. **Brand Manifesto:** A quiet, text-focused section with asymmetrical alignment detailing the atelier’s philosophy.
4. **Trust Bar / Press:** Subtle logos of features/press without borders or boxes.
5. **The Art of Embroidery (Visual Intro):** A large, high-res image of intricate threadwork, paired with a small caption.
6. **Bespoke Process Steps (1/3):** Consultation.
7. **Bespoke Process Steps (2/3):** The Fitting.
8. **Bespoke Process Steps (3/3):** The Delivery.
9. **Signature Collection Showcase:** A carousel or staggered grid of signature pieces.
10. **The Gift of Bespoke (CTA):** A specialized section focused on gifting custom suits. Deep background color for contrast.
11. **Artisan Highlight:** A profile section on the tailors/embroiderers.
12. **Material Library:** A tactile visual grid showing fabric swatches with hover interactions.
13. **Testimonial Grid:** Asymmetrical grid of client quotes.
14. **Video Tour / Ambience:** A full-width subtle looping video section (or placeholder) showing the boutique interior.
15. **Featured Read / Blog Peek:** Links to the latest editorial piece on styling.
16. **FAQ Accordion (Highlights):** Top 3 questions asked by first-time bespoke clients.
17. **Location & Visit Us:** Maps and address with an image of the Chandigarh storefront.
18. **Secondary CTA:** "Ready to transform your wardrobe?"
19. **Newsletter Signup:** "Join our inner circle for exclusive atelier updates."
20. **Footer:** Comprehensive but clean sitemap, legal links, and subtle social icons.
