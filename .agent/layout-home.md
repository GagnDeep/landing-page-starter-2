# Layout Blueprint: Home Page
**Intent:** Introduce Bawa Caterers Enterprises as a premier, opulent, and highly professional catering service. The layout should flow like a grand feast—starting with an impactful impression, moving through rich offerings, establishing deep trust, and concluding with a clear invitation.

## Section Inventory (20 Sections)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):**
    - Intent: Elegant, transparent-to-solid on scroll. Sticky.
    - Elements: Logo (center or left), Primary Links, "Book a Tasting" CTA.
2.  **Hero Section:**
    - Intent: Immediate grand impression. Warm, rich visual.
    - Elements: Full-bleed background image or subtle video of a royal spread. Prominent Serif H1. Subtitle. Primary CTA.
3.  **Trust Bar (Social Proof):**
    - Intent: Establish immediate credibility.
    - Elements: "Rated 4.6 Stars", "Serving Patiala for X Years", Logos of partnered venues.
4.  **Brand Ethos (Introduction):**
    - Intent: Warm welcome, explaining the "Royal Punjabi" philosophy.
    - Elements: Two-column layout (Text + Arch-framed image). Playfair heading.
5.  **Signature Services Overview:**
    - Intent: High-level overview of what they do.
    - Elements: Grid of 3-4 cards (Weddings, Corporate, Live Stalls, Intimate Events). Hover effects with shadow-glow.
6.  **The Royal Spread Showcase (Gallery Intro):**
    - Intent: Visual feast of their best dishes.
    - Elements: Large marquee or masonry grid of high-quality food photography.
7.  **Live Cooking Stall Spotlight:**
    - Intent: Highlight a unique selling proposition (interactive food).
    - Elements: Full-width dark mode section to contrast the fire/action of live cooking.
8.  **Menu Highlights (Interactive):**
    - Intent: Let users peek at the offerings.
    - Elements: Tabbed interface (Starters, Mains, Desserts, Beverages) showing select premium items.
9.  **The Bawa Experience (How It Works):**
    - Intent: Reassure clients of a seamless process.
    - Elements: 4-step horizontal timeline (Consultation -> Tasting -> Planning -> Execution).
10. **Testimonials (The Royal Court):**
    - Intent: Deep social proof from past clients.
    - Elements: Carousel or staggered grid of reviews. Elegant typography.
11. **Featured Event Case Study:**
    - Intent: Show, don't just tell, a successful large-scale event.
    - Elements: Split screen (Large image + Stats/Story of a 1000+ guest wedding).
12. **Meet the Master Chefs:**
    - Intent: Add a human element and authority.
    - Elements: Profiles of the lead chefs/founders. Circular images, brief bios.
13. **Quality & Sourcing Commitment:**
    - Intent: Assure clients of premium ingredients.
    - Elements: Icon grid (Local sourcing, Fresh spices, Hygiene standards).
14. **Pricing/Packages Teaser:**
    - Intent: Give a hint of pricing structure without overwhelming.
    - Elements: "From Silver to Platinum" conceptual tiers. Link to full pricing page.
15. **Venue Partnerships:**
    - Intent: Show established relationships in Patiala/Punjab.
    - Elements: Logo marquee of famous palaces/banquets they cater to.
16. **FAQ (Top 3 Questions):**
    - Intent: Address immediate objections.
    - Elements: Accordion style. (e.g., "Do you do outstation catering?").
17. **Latest from the Blog/Journal:**
    - Intent: SEO and ongoing engagement.
    - Elements: 3 recent posts (e.g., "Trends in Punjabi Weddings").
18. **Newsletter/Offers Signup:**
    - Intent: Capture leads who aren't ready to book.
    - Elements: Elegant inline form. "Join our table".
19. **Pre-Footer CTA (The Final Invitation):**
    - Intent: The ultimate push to contact.
    - Elements: Deep velvet background, gold text. "Let us craft your perfect menu." Button.
20. **Global Footer:**
    - Intent: Utility and secondary navigation.
    - Elements: Address, Contact Info, Links to Legal/Careers, Social Icons.
