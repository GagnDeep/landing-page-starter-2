# Layout intent for A2Zee Events - Home Page

1. **Global Header (Transparent on top, solid on scroll)**
   - *Layout intent:* Elegant top navigation with the brand logo centered. Links on left and right for balance, reserving the right-most slot for a CTA button.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Minimal height, transparent over the hero to feel immersive, transitioning to a solid blurred background with a subtle shadow on scroll.

2. **Hero Section (Cinematic Introduction)**
   - *Layout intent:* Full-viewport height section dominating the initial load. A full bleed background image or subtle looping video. Text overlay with a large serif headline, a short subtitle, and a primary call-to-action button.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Grand and expansive. The text sits centrally or offset to the left in a constrained width to ensure legibility while letting the imagery breathe.

3. **Brand Manifesto (The "Why")**
   - *Layout intent:* A quiet, text-focused section to establish tone. A central pull quote or manifesto statement in the serif heading font.
   - *Visual rhythm:* High vertical padding (`spacing-4xl`). No background image, just the base background color. Extremely clean, centering the reader's attention on the brand's philosophy.

4. **Featured Services Overview (The Pillars)**
   - *Layout intent:* A staggered or asymmetrical grid highlighting the 3-4 core services (e.g., Destination Planning, Design & Styling, Concierge).
   - *Visual rhythm:* Alternating image and text blocks (zig-zag pattern) or a 3-column layout where the middle column is vertically offset. Subtle fade-in animations on scroll.

5. **Signature Destinations Grid**
   - *Layout intent:* A showcase of top locations (e.g., Italy, France, Bali). A bento-box or masonry grid of high-quality images.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Images have a subtle zoom on hover. Captions are minimal, appearing elegantly at the bottom of each image card or revealed on hover.

6. **The A2Zee Experience (Process Timeline)**
   - *Layout intent:* A vertical or horizontal timeline explaining the planning journey from consultation to the big day.
   - *Visual rhythm:* A delicate, continuous connecting line with small nodal dots. Content blocks alternate sides of the line.

7. **Social Proof / Testimonial Highlight**
   - *Layout intent:* A single, powerful quote from a past client. Accompanied by a subtle, ambient background texture or a soft, monochromatic image of a wedding detail.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Large, sweeping typography. High padding to separate it from surrounding sections.

8. **Portfolio Teaser (Curated Gallery)**
   - *Layout intent:* A horizontal scrolling carousel or a tightly packed 4x2 grid of recent wedding highlights.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Clean edges, uniform gaps (`spacing-sm`). Designed to feel like opening an editorial lookbook.

9. **Founder / Principal Planner Intro**
   - *Layout intent:* A two-column split. One side features a high-end portrait of the founder; the other side contains a welcoming letter/bio.
   - *Visual rhythm:* Asymmetrical balance. The image might slightly overlap the container boundaries to create a layered, custom feel.

10. **Press & Publications Strip**
    - *Layout intent:* A horizontal band featuring greyscale logos of publications where A2Zee has been featured (e.g., Vogue, Harper's Bazaar).
    - *Visual rhythm:* Very subtle, low contrast. Logos are small and evenly spaced.

11. **Interactive Service Selector**
    - *Layout intent:* A section allowing users to toggle between different service tiers (e.g., Full Service vs. Partial) to see changing descriptions or imagery.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Smooth cross-fade transitions between states. Elegant tab styling.

12. **Destination Spotlight (Deep Dive)**
    - *Layout intent:* A full-width section focusing on one specific, highly sought-after destination. Large image on one side, evocative description on the other.
    - *Visual rhythm:* High contrast against the previous section (e.g., switching from a light background to a deep primary background color).

13. **Behind the Scenes / Our Approach**
    - *Layout intent:* A collage of smaller, candid images showing the team at work, combined with brief explanatory text.
    - *Visual rhythm:* A less structured, more organic layout to convey the human element behind the perfection.

14. **FAQ Teaser**
    - *Layout intent:* 3-4 of the most common questions presented in an elegant accordion format.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Clean, horizontal rule separators between questions. Smooth expansion animation.

15. **Newsletter / Magazine Signup**
    - *Layout intent:* A beautifully designed opt-in block. Instead of a generic "subscribe," framed as joining an exclusive list or receiving a digital magazine.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Centered form within a refined, subtly bordered card.

16. **Recent Journal Entries (Blog Teaser)**
    - *Layout intent:* A 3-column grid showing the latest blog posts. Each card has an image, date, title, and a "Read More" link.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Minimalist cards with no borders, relying on spacing (`spacing-lg`) and typography to separate items.

17. **Partners & Vendor Network**
    - *Layout intent:* A section highlighting the caliber of vendors they work with (florists, photographers).
    - *Visual rhythm:* A subtle, slow-moving marquee or a static, elegant grid of names/logos.

18. **Final Call to Action (The Invitation)**
    - *Layout intent:* A grand, full-width section with a striking background image (darkened or blurred). A final, compelling invitation to book a consultation.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Large, centralized typography. The button is the clear focal point. High emotional resonance.

19. **Global Footer - Upper Area (Links & Contact)**
    - *Layout intent:* A multi-column layout containing secondary navigation, contact information, and social links.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Structured and highly legible. Use of the muted text colors.

20. **Global Footer - Lower Area (Legal & Copyright)**
    - *Layout intent:* A single horizontal bar at the very bottom.
    - *Visual rhythm:* Smallest typography. Contains copyright notice and links to Privacy/Terms.
