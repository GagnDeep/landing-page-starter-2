# Layout: Home Page
**URL**: `/`
**Intent**: Establish Sangam Caterer's premium identity, showcase culinary mastery and event expertise, and drive leads for high-end weddings and events.

## Structure (20 Sections)

1. **Hero Header**: Full-screen or large hero image/video background. Playfair Display main headline centered. Navigation is transparent, becoming solid on scroll.
2. **Brand Ethos / Intro**: A clean, high-whitespace section with a short, impactful statement about their heritage and culinary philosophy.
3. **Featured Service: Weddings**: Large evocative image left, text right. Highlighting the grand wedding experience.
4. **Featured Service: Corporate**: Image right, text left. Focus on professional, flawless execution.
5. **Featured Service: Intimate Gatherings**: Image left, text right. Focus on bespoke menus and attention to detail.
6. **The Culinary Experience**: A visually rich grid or masonry layout showcasing food presentation. Minimal text.
7. **Menu Highlights (Starters)**: Horizontal scrolling list or a clean grid of exquisite starter dishes.
8. **Menu Highlights (Mains)**: Similar layout to starters, focusing on rich main courses.
9. **Menu Highlights (Desserts)**: Sweet, elegant presentation of desserts.
10. **Testimonial Slider**: Clean typography-driven slider. Large quotes in Playfair Display.
11. **Statistics/Scale**: A subtly patterned background section highlighting numbers (e.g., years of experience, events catered, guests served).
12. **The Team/Chefs**: A brief introduction to the masterminds behind the food. High-quality portraits.
13. **Venue Partnerships**: A logo cloud or card grid of premium venues they regularly work with.
14. **Process / How We Work**: A vertical timeline or 3-step horizontal process (Consultation -> Tasting -> Execution).
15. **Gallery / Portfolio Teaser**: A 4-6 image grid showing diverse event setups and culinary art.
16. **FAQ Teaser**: 3-4 top questions answered in an elegant accordion format.
17. **Latest Journal/Blog Posts**: A 3-column card layout linking to recent articles or event features.
18. **Awards & Recognition**: Minimalist section displaying badges or mentions in press.
19. **Call to Action (CTA)**: A bold, distinct background section urging visitors to request a consultation.
20. **Footer**: Comprehensive but clean. Links, contact info, newsletter signup, and social icons.
