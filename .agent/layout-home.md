# Layout Documentation: Home Page

## Intent
The Home page serves as the grand entrance to Imran Tailor. It must immediately establish the 20+ year legacy, the bespoke nature of the craft, and the premium service offered to NRIs. The rhythm should be deliberate, alternating between sweeping visual statements and focused, intimate details of the tailoring process.

## Sections (20+ Distinct Layouts)

1.  **Global Navigation Bar:**
    *   *Layout:* Fixed top, transparent fading to solid (bg-background) on scroll. Centered logo (elegant typography), left/right balanced links.
    *   *Intent:* Unobtrusive, sophisticated wayfinding.

2.  **Hero/Atelier Entrance (Section 1):**
    *   *Layout:* Full viewport height (`min-h-screen`). Large background image/video of the workshop or a masterfully tailored suit. Text overlay: Large `font-heading` title (e.g., "Precision. Heritage. Elegance."), small `font-sans` subtitle, single gold-accented primary CTA ("Book a Fitting").
    *   *Intent:* Immediate emotional impact and value proposition.

3.  **The Legacy Statement (Section 2):**
    *   *Layout:* Generous padding (`py-32`). Centered, narrow max-width text block. Large serif drop cap or oversized quotation marks.
    *   *Intent:* Establish the 20+ years of trust and craftsmanship.

4.  **The Master's Touch / Video Teaser (Section 3):**
    *   *Layout:* Asymmetric split. Left: 60% width subtle video loop of chalk on fabric or stitching. Right: 40% width elegant typography describing the process.
    *   *Intent:* Show, don't just tell, the meticulous nature of the work.

5.  **Service Pillars Grid (Section 4):**
    *   *Layout:* 3-column grid (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3`). Minimalist cards. Each card has a subtle icon (tape measure, shears, thread), a serif title, and short sans-serif description. Soft borders.
    *   *Intent:* Clearly define offerings (Bespoke, Alterations, NRI Service).

6.  **The NRI Experience Focus (Section 5):**
    *   *Layout:* Full-width background color (`bg-secondary/10`). Two-column layout alternating. Image of a traveler or a packed, elegant garment bag. Text highlights global shipping, remote consultations.
    *   *Intent:* Directly address a key target demographic and their specific needs.

7.  **Featured Collection/Gallery - Look 1 (Section 6):**
    *   *Layout:* Full width, zero margin image. Parallax effect.
    *   *Intent:* High-impact visual break showing the final product.

8.  **Featured Collection - Details (Section 7):**
    *   *Layout:* "Bento box" style grid (unequal sizes). Close-up shots of lapels, buttonholes, lining. Minimal text on hover.
    *   *Intent:* Highlight the granular quality of the craftsmanship.

9.  **The Fabric Library (Section 8):**
    *   *Layout:* Horizontal scrolling carousel (`overflow-x-auto`). Cards showing different premium fabrics (wool, silk, linen) with their origins (e.g., "Italian Wool", "Irish Linen").
    *   *Intent:* Showcase the material quality.

10. **Testimonial 1 - The Generational Client (Section 9):**
    *   *Layout:* Centered, large serif quote. Subtle background texture (like linen). Attribution includes the number of years they've been a client.
    *   *Intent:* Build profound trust through longevity.

11. **The Fitting Process Step 1: Consultation (Section 10):**
    *   *Layout:* Alternating zig-zag layout (Text Left, Image Right). Image shows a client discussing styles with Imran.

12. **The Fitting Process Step 2: Measurements (Section 11):**
    *   *Layout:* Alternating zig-zag layout (Image Left, Text Right). Image shows precise measuring.

13. **The Fitting Process Step 3: The Baste Fitting (Section 12):**
    *   *Layout:* Alternating zig-zag layout (Text Left, Image Right). Image shows the unfinished suit structure.

14. **The Fitting Process Step 4: Final Delivery (Section 13):**
    *   *Layout:* Alternating zig-zag layout (Image Left, Text Right). Image shows the polished final look.
    *   *Intent (Sections 10-13):* Demystify the bespoke process and highlight the personal touch.

15. **Metrics of Mastery (Section 14):**
    *   *Layout:* 4-column grid. Large typography numbers (e.g., "20+", "10k+", "50+") with small labels ("Years Experience", "Garments Crafted", "Global Destinations").
    *   *Intent:* Quantitative proof of authority.

16. **Testimonial 2 - The NRI Client (Section 15):**
    *   *Layout:* Different layout from Testimonial 1. Perhaps a side-by-side with an image of the client wearing the suit abroad.
    *   *Intent:* Reinforce the NRI value proposition.

17. **Behind the Seams/Blog Teaser (Section 16):**
    *   *Layout:* 2 or 3 column grid of recent articles. Focus on typography and elegant thumbnail images.
    *   *Intent:* Show ongoing thought leadership and passion for the craft.

18. **The Gift of Tailoring / Vouchers (Section 17):**
    *   *Layout:* Card based layout with a dark, rich background (using the dark mode tokens specifically for this section to make it pop). Gold accents highlighting the "gift" aspect.
    *   *Intent:* Drive secondary revenue stream.

19. **Location & Atmosphere (Section 18):**
    *   *Layout:* Split screen. Map integration (elegantly styled, not default Google Maps) on one side, an image of the Patiala storefront on the other. Address and hours in clear typography.
    *   *Intent:* Ground the digital experience in a physical, historic location.

20. **Final Call to Action (Section 19):**
    *   *Layout:* Centered, ample whitespace (`py-40`). Strong declarative statement ("Begin Your Bespoke Journey"). Two buttons: Primary ("Book Consultation"), Secondary ("View Pricing").
    *   *Intent:* Drive the primary conversion goal.

21. **Global Footer (Section 20):**
    *   *Layout:* Multi-column. Brand mark, navigation links, contact info, newsletter signup (elegant single-line input with a subtle submit arrow). Legal links at the very bottom.
    *   *Intent:* Functional closure to the page.
