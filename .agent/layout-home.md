# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

**Purpose**: High-conversion landing page. Showcases 24/7 reliability, premium custom 6-pound cakes, and high-end gifts.
**Vibe**: Boutique, warm, inviting, rescue-ready.

## Sections (Visual Rhythm)

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**
   - Minimal logo (A-One).
   - "24/7 Support" badge prominently displayed.
   - Primary Navigation + CTA "Order Now".
2. **Hero: The 24/7 Rescue**
   - High-contrast, large warm serif heading.
   - Dynamic fluid layout: large evocative image of a fresh cake on the right, bold text on the left.
   - 2 Buttons: "Order Custom Cake" (Primary), "Emergency Gifts" (Secondary).
3. **Social Proof Bar**
   - "5.0-star reputation" banner.
   - Logos/names of local institutions or glowing Google reviews.
4. **Value Proposition: Why Us?**
   - 3-column asymmetric layout.
   - Emphasis on 24/7 service, premium ingredients, and near Shiv Mandir location.
5. **Featured Category: Signature 6-Pound Custom Cakes**
   - Large, edge-to-edge section.
   - Soft background color (e.g., `--secondary`).
   - Image gallery with staggered masonry layout.
6. **Featured Category: Thoughtful Gifts**
   - Alternating left/right image-text blocks.
   - Showcases bouquets, chocolates, custom hampers.
7. **The "Night Rescue" Concept Block**
   - Dark mode-inspired section even in light theme (using `--secondary` night colors).
   - "Midnight Cravings? Forgotten Anniversaries? We've got you."
   - Urgent CTA.
8. **Testimonials Slider**
   - Clean, geometric cards floating on the background.
   - Serif quotes.
9. **Interactive Element: Build Your Cake (Teaser)**
   - Small interactive preview (flavor + size selector UI mockup).
10. **About the Crafters**
    - Split layout: founder photo + story. "Bridging local artisanal quality and digital-first convenience."
11. **Location & Delivery Information**
    - Map graphic or stylized location pin near Shiv Mandir, Patiala.
    - Clear delivery radii and times.
12. **FAQ Snippets**
    - Accordion style, 3-4 most common questions.
13. **Blog Teaser: Cake Care & Tips**
    - 2-3 recent articles in a horizontal scrollable row or grid.
14. **Newsletter/Offers Signup**
    - Minimalist input field. "Join the A-One Family."
15. **Instagram / Social Feed**
    - 4x4 grid of high-quality photos. No margins.
16. **Secondary CTA Banner**
    - Bold, solid color background. "Ready to order?"
17. **Global Footer**
    - Multi-column link structure, newsletter, address, legal links.
