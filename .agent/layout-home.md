# Homepage Layout & Architecture Blueprint

This document outlines the visual rhythm and sequence of sections for the Harbhajan's Catering Patiala homepage. The layout should flow like a well-paced evening event.

## 1. Hero Section (The Welcome/Sagai)
- **Visual:** Full viewport height (`min-h-svh`). Deep, rich imagery in the background (potentially a high-quality video or an elegant slow-fade slider of culinary prep/venue).
- **Layout:** Centered or bottom-left heavy.
- **Content:** An elegant, large display serif headline (e.g., "Authentic Tradition, Elegantly Served"). Smaller, polished sans-serif subheadline.
- **CTA:** Distinct primary button ("Plan Your Event") and a subtle secondary link ("View Menu").

## 2. Trust & Heritage Bar
- **Visual:** A narrow, full-width strip sitting directly below the hero. Subdued background color (`bg-muted` or `bg-accent`).
- **Layout:** Flex row, evenly spaced.
- **Content:** Established year, key regions served (Patiala, Punjab), and subtle icons or logos of high-profile venues or awards.

## 3. The Philosophy (Value Proposition)
- **Visual:** Generous vertical padding (`py-24` or similar). Editorial layout.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric 2-column. Left: A poetic, deeply personal statement about Punjabi generosity. Right: A single, striking, softly rounded image of hands preparing food.
- **Typography:** Very large serif text.

## 4. Culinary Heritage (Feature Spotlight 1)
- **Visual:** Clean background, shifting to a slightly warmer tone.
- **Layout:** 3-column grid for standard screens.
- **Content:** Highlighting the core pillars of their food (e.g., The Spices, The Tandoor, The Sweet Shop). Each gets a card with a subtle border and an elegant icon.

## 5. Signature Experiences (Services Overview)
- **Visual:** Dark section (`bg-primary` or an espresso dark mode token) to create a dramatic break in the scroll. Light text.
- **Layout:** Alternating left/right image-and-text blocks (zigzag layout).
- **Content:** Wedding Catering, Corporate Galas, Private Dinners. Each with a "Discover More" link.

## 6. The Visual Feast (Gallery/Carousel)
- **Visual:** Edge-to-edge carousel of high-res, color-graded imagery.
- **Layout:** A horizontal scrolling section or a structured masonry grid.
- **Content:** Food, decor, and happy guests. Minimal text, maximum visual impact.

## 7. Words of Praise (Social Proof/Testimonials)
- **Visual:** Soft, light background.
- **Layout:** A central prominent quote, with smaller supporting reviews beneath or to the side.
- **Typography:** Serif italic for the quotes to emphasize the personal touch.

## 8. The Art of the Menu (Interactive Teaser)
- **Visual:** Split screen or tabs.
- **Layout:** Left side: Tab navigation (Starters, Mains, Desserts). Right side: A gracefully fading list of sample dishes with descriptions.
- **Content:** Teasing the depth of the offerings.

## 9. Behind the Scenes (Team/Heritage)
- **Visual:** A candid, warm portrait of the founders/head chefs.
- **Layout:** Centered content block over a parallax background or a clean split layout.

## 10. The Process (How It Works)
- **Visual:** Clean, structured.
- **Layout:** 3 or 4 step vertical timeline or horizontal flex row with connecting lines.
- **Content:** Consultation, Tasting, Execution.

## 11. Highlighted Venues
- **Visual:** Grid of venue partners in Patiala.
- **Layout:** Logo or image grid with subtle hover states.

## 12. Sustainability & Sourcing
- **Layout:** A brief, elegant block emphasizing local farm sourcing.
- **Visual:** Earthy tones.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions (Teaser)
- **Layout:** A clean accordion component with 3-4 top questions.
- **Content:** "How far in advance should we book?", etc.

## 14. Press & Media
- **Layout:** A simple flex-row of logos from publications or local news.

## 15. The Journal/Blog (Latest Updates)
- **Layout:** 2 or 3 recent posts in a grid.
- **Content:** Tips for planning a Punjabi wedding menu, seasonal ingredients.

## 16. Immersive Video Teaser
- **Visual:** A wide aspect-ratio video player or auto-playing background video with a subtle play button.

## 17. The Call to Action (The Invitation)
- **Visual:** High contrast. Rich terracotta background with off-white text.
- **Layout:** Centered, large typography. "Let us host your next milestone."
- **Interactive:** Large, polished button leading to the Contact page.

## 18. Newsletter/Updates (The Waitlist/Connection)
- **Layout:** Simple inline form. "Join our table for seasonal updates."

## 19. Final Social Proof
- **Layout:** A small badge or floating element indicating a trusted rating (e.g., Google Reviews 5-star).

## 20. Footer (The Farewell)
- **Layout:** 4-column grid on desktop.
- **Content:** Logo, navigation links, contact info (address, phone), social media icons, and legal links.
