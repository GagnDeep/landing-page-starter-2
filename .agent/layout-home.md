# Layout Intent: Home Page

The home page serves as the digital flagship for Stitching Solutions, blending editorial imagery with clear, concise service offerings. The design should guide the user through the boutique's story, expertise, and easy booking process.

## Sections (20+ required for rich storytelling)

1. **Hero Section:** Full-bleed editorial video or high-quality image of a beautifully tailored garment. Minimal text, high-contrast serif headline ("Precision in Every Stitch"), and a prominent "Book a Fitting" primary CTA.
2. **Brand Introduction / Philosophy:** A short, centered paragraph explaining the boutique's commitment to alteration-free, reliable tailoring.
3. **The "Why Us" / Value Prop Grid:** 3 or 4 elegant cards highlighting core values: On-time Delivery, Master Craftsmanship, Owner-Led Service, Perfect Fit Guarantee.
4. **Featured Services Teaser:** An asymmetrical grid showing categories (e.g., Bridal, Everyday Wear, Evening Gowns) with hover effects that softly reveal more information.
5. **The Process (Step-by-step):** A visually connected timeline (e.g., 1. Consultation, 2. Measurement, 3. The Making, 4. Final Fitting).
6. **Signature Lookbook Carousel:** A horizontal, slow-scrolling carousel of the boutique's best work.
7. **Founder's Message / Owner Spotlight:** A warm photo of the owner with a personal quote about their dedication to the craft and the Patiala community.
8. **Testimonial Spotlight (Featured):** One large, high-impact review from a satisfied customer, emphasizing the "alteration-free" promise.
9. **Testimonials Grid:** A masonry or standard grid of 3-4 smaller, curated reviews.
10. **Fabric & Material Quality Section:** A macro photography section focusing on the high-quality threads, fabrics, and tools used in the boutique.
11. **Local Presence & Reliability:** Information specifically targeting the Patiala region, emphasizing local trust and walk-in accessibility.
12. **Before & After / The Transformation:** An interactive slider or elegant side-by-side comparison of poorly fitted vs. bespoke tailored garments (optional, conceptual).
13. **Pricing Philosophy:** A brief note on transparent pricing—we charge for quality and precision, no hidden costs.
14. **Urgency/Availability Banner:** A subtle but noticeable section indicating current booking lead times (e.g., "Currently accepting bridal consultations for next season").
15. **FAQ Snippet:** 3-4 of the most common questions (accordion style) to remove immediate barriers to booking.
16. **Newsletter/Style Guide Opt-in:** An elegant form offering a downloadable guide to measuring yourself or seasonal style trends in exchange for an email.
17. **Behind the Scenes / The Atelier:** A video loop or photo gallery showing the tailors at work.
18. **Secondary CTA Section:** A full-width section with a muted background color reiterating the call to action ("Ready for your perfect fit?").
19. **Instagram / Social Proof Feed:** A grid pulling in recent posts, showcasing the active, vibrant life of the boutique.
20. **Footer (Pre-footer content):** Location map, opening hours, contact details prominently displayed before the standard links.
21. **Standard Footer:** Comprehensive links (About, Services, Legal, etc.), newsletter signup duplicate, and social icons.
